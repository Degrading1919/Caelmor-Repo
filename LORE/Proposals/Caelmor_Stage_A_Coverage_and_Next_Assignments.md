## Rating method

- **Strong:** several concrete, mutually supporting canon anchors exist, though they may still need production selection.
- **Partial:** a recognizable direction exists, but causal links, dates, people, or operating rules are incomplete.
- **Thin:** only a few anchors or thematic assertions exist; a worker cannot yet derive a deep, connected story from them without proposing substantial new content.
- **Missing:** no approved, usable account or control document was found.
- **Contradictory:** current sources or a newer Director decision make incompatible factual claims. This rating can coexist with strong thematic material.

Ratings describe the **current canon corpus**, not the prior proposal's potential. A long dossier is not automatically deep: named lists and hooks can still lack relationships, consequences, and an evidence path. Stage A does not certify material-culture readiness.

## What Stage A has established

The four regions have **strong identities** and enough canon anchors to support a long campaign, but their chronological and magical truth is not yet coherent across files. The most damaging risks are the 30-year war date migration, postwar Dreamer placement, precursor/formal Remnant split, Lowmark's new practical magic, the Mire's site/threat identity, and v1 scope labels attached to future-region material. Stage A records these risks rather than declaring them solved. It does not perform the regional expansion or the proposed “Lore Foundation Lock.”

The older proposal in `4d2d6b6` contains candidate civic, repair, memorial, and callback ideas. A later worker may read and classify those ideas as **retain for options / revise / reject**, with a source claim and scope reason for each. No proposal in that commit is imported into canon or this branch.
