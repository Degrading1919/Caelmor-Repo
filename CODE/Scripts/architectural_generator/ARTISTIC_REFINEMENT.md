# Exterior authenticity and household investment review

Working art interpretations, not new lore. Source: approved regional dossiers,
Phase 1 grounded mythic tone, user's industrial Emberholt direction and the
merged lessons-learned PR #3 baseline. Existing v0.4 wealth/clay sheets remain
the before reference; do not overwrite their images or manifests.

## Compact acceptance set, fixed before implementation

Seed 91, COTTAGE, all four regions x MODEST/ESTABLISHED/AFFLUENT. Hold
craftsmanship STANDARD, age 35, maintenance .7, MEDIUM/EFFICIENT geometry and
context AUTO constant. Render neutral front/east and rear/west views. Use one
orthographic scale per region across tiers, fixed lighting and no randomized
ground dressing. These 12 settings are the early falsification set, not a final
human acceptance study. Inspect each region as its own three-column comparison.

Observable requirements:

| Region | Modest | Established | Affluent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lowmark | Simple multipurpose hall, inexpensive external work shelter | Farm/work household with usable covered work range and storage | Separate private domestic rooms plus service range, sheltered yard and investment in joinery |
| Thornfell | Compact sheltered stone hall, quiet continuous weather verge | Lower entrance/store range forming a wind-protected approach | Private room range and sheltered court; signal towers reserved for justified public/defensive function |
| Mire | Small practical landing and repairable pier/board construction | Full working deck with shelter and substantial support | Separate private pavilion, screened access and sheltered household circulation |
| Emberholt | Squat heavy work dwelling with hearth/flue, restrained iron reinforcement | Artisan shophouse with hierarchy between work and domestic spaces | Compound/private court, deep entrance and stepped volumes; crafted iron has real attachment/load roles |

Wealth must change at least two useful construction decisions beyond bounding
dimensions/palette. All tiers retain regional identity and credible access.
Wealth is independent of craftsmanship, maintenance and age; a modest building
must not automatically be broken, and a rich one need not be maintained.

Ordinary Thornfell homes use restrained sloping coping, not jagged ceremonial
stairs on every gable. Emberholt retains volcanic-stone/iron angular weight but
breaks uniform apartment-like frontage through work/entry hierarchy, full-height
flues, ground-bearing reinforcement and purposeful lower ranges. Proportions
and masonry still require human art judgement.

## Integration constraints and schedule

Workers own regional modules only. Root owns shared Context/facade interfaces,
Mire, rendering, integration and final review. Retain existing main semantic IDs,
determinism, saved Settings shape, protection, legacy path and all existing types.
No canon, runtime/C#, catalog expansion or Unity changes.

Use actual door records and solid_stations for projections; no ornament across
apertures, unsupported upper doors or roof crossings. New occupied masses may
touch shared blind faces but must not overlap. Roofed open shelters need explicit
supports and clear approach paths. Do not exempt ordinary details from geometry
validation to silence an error.

First run representative Python generation/validation, then the compact neutral
set and inspect it. Correct construction before expanding to existing WORKSHOP
examples and material/condition views. Run targeted actual Blender aperture/BVH
checks for affected faces, then broader existing regression and native ZIP checks
once at integration. Freeze formatting/docs before final packaging; rerun only
gates affected by any late change. Final production-art acceptance is the Creative
Director's; internal review reports limitations rather than self-approval.

## Construction precedents and boundaries

The [Historic England National Farm Building Types guide](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/national-farm-building-types/national-building-types-2014/)
supports reading a forge, cart shed, store and dwelling as a working farmstead.
The [Chirbury smithy record](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/heritage-gateway/results/record/2f000900-da89-446c-af1a-55ae6fb05a9d/MSA15894)
describes distinct store/forge/access areas and a real flue: this informs useful
space hierarchy rather than copying its building. The
[Chamberbane listing](https://portal.historicenvironment.scot/apex/f?p=1505%3A300%3A%3A%3A%3A%3AVIEWTYPE%2CVIEWREF%3Adesignation%2CLB6004)
documents a rubble platform, narrow plan and recessed openings, supporting a
restrained Thornfell household interpretation. These mostly later vernacular
survivals do not establish a universal medieval design. Emberholt's iron sheets,
corbels and monumental household tradition remain original mythic art choices,
not claims about historical manufacture or newly approved Caelmor technology.

Early review rejected a first Emberholt pass that left wide blank apartment-like
bodies under added detail. The next pass articulated narrow domestic bodies and
lower working/private ranges. Roof crossing checks also rejected close Lowmark
shed eaves; independent ranges now have sufficient weather separation. Retain
these early panels as evidence of the rejected results, separate from final
geometry/material comparisons.
