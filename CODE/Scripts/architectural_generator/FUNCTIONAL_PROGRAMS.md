# Function-first architectural programs

Working art interpretations, not changes to approved lore. Version 0.6 starts
with Smithy, Market Hall, Stable and Gatehouse; all other functions retain their
existing regional builders. The complete v0.5 archetype gallery is historical
evidence and must not be overwritten or regenerated for this change.

## Construction contract

Purpose allocates spaces and clearances before the regional shell is built:

| Program | Required spatial organization | Observable neutral-clay evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Smithy | Hot work, operator space, cool material handling and a ventilated working frontage | Open forge workspace, hearth/hood/flue, anvil and separated fuel/quench areas |
| Market Hall | Public circulation separate from repeated vendor/servicing bays | Sheltered trading frontage, visible counters and a genuine through aisle |
| Stable | Animal-sized stall bays, feeding, hay storage and a handling apron | Divided stalls with accessible fronts, mangers/racks and a gentle approach |
| Gatehouse | Controlled through-route, flanking guarding positions and defended thresholds | Tall guarded gateway, aligned entry/exit, raised gate control and accessible guard positions |

The shared Builder constructs parameterized masses, bays, integral fixtures,
ground-bearing decks/ramps and semantic route/station records. It supplies local
roof/foundation/material traditions after the functional arrangement is chosen.
No saved mesh kits or generic interior/furnishing system are introduced. Required
functional equipment survives LOW detail. Regional adaptation must affect actual
weather protection, support, access or arrangement, rather than just colors.

Open facades declare access reservations before frame/arcade stations are placed.
Masonry openings receive actual bearing piers. Boxes registered as functional
stations cannot intersect declared clear human/public/animal/service routes.
These semantic checks supplement actual triangle/BVH probes; metadata alone
cannot establish a usable entrance, ventilation or an artistic read. Existing
roof, complete aperture/frame, occupied-volume and supported-access guards remain.
Public transit routes must retain at least 1.2m clear plan width; this authoring
constraint is distinct from local standing work pads. Mire's market reserves a
1.35m rear cross-aisle before allocating stalls. A frame reservation is not a
wall opening: a bridge connection must have a real door aperture and body-sized
clearance through the rendered wall and its collision proxy.

## Small acceptance set and evidence schedule

First inspect Lowmark Smithy, Thornfell Market Hall, Mire Stable and Emberholt
Gatehouse at seed 91, ESTABLISHED, STANDARD, age 35, maintenance .7,
MEDIUM/EFFICIENT, World construction/grammar on and AUTO local context. Capture
two opposing exterior angles, clay and materials, reusing each native build.
Reject sealed or hidden working spaces and generic house-shaped solutions before
extending the four programs across regions. Final comparison covers only these
four functions; it is not another 80-building gallery. Keep early rejected views
separate and rerender only changed configurations. Final art acceptance remains
the Creative Director's, not a successful agent recognition test.

## Construction-quality comparison contract (0.7)

The Creative Director found the 0.6 programs functionally clearer but still too
simple. A two-case controlled study holds native geometry/settings constant and
changes lighting or camera elevation separately. It reveals more relief and
work equipment, but does not remove the broad undifferentiated roofs, primitive
equipment or flat defended frontage. This supports a geometry-first revision
for those cases; it does not isolate individual shaders or establish a universal
finding for all buildings. Historical renders remain in `evidence/function_v06`;
the control is in `evidence/function_v07_diagnosis`.

The next matched clay/material set must show these actual construction cues:

- Smithy: readable hearth capture, shaped anvil/bellows/quench, a supported hot
  working bay and separate stock handling, with an unobstructed exhaust path.
- Market Hall: framed selling places and roof support rhythm related to their
  layout, rather than low boxes inside an otherwise empty shell; clear public
  and vendor circulation including the Mire quay connection.
- Stable: load-bearing bay divisions, open constructed gates, feeding points
  and an identifiable hay-service end, without narrowing animal routes.
- Gatehouse: a deep supported defended threshold with gate mechanics and
  flanking construction; masonry load paths and weather/defensive edges must
  remain plausible in both opposed views.

Use seed 91 and the existing established/standard/age-35/maintenance-.7 medium
efficient settings and studio. Review early representatives before the final
16-configuration/64-frame comparison. New supports must obey full-body weather
envelopes; equipment must obey actual circulation/exhaust clearances. Technical
passes and internal artistic assessments remain distinct from Creative Director
acceptance. These changes are interpretations of existing construction cues,
not new canon or an expansion of the function catalog.

## Sources and inference boundaries

Approved region dossiers remain the authority: Lowmark's agricultural/trading
settlements and Crestbridge stables; Thornfell's wind/stone/ridge traditions;
Mire raised banks, stilts and paths; Emberholt forge culture and open-air markets.
Floor heights, stall widths, ramps and equipment layouts here are authoring
choices, not newly established historical or Caelmor canon facts. A raised Mire
stable is a bank/ridge husbandry adaptation with a gentle approach, not a claim
that animals can occupy a flooded or unsupported site. Site/terrain selection
remains the artist's responsibility.

- [Historic England archaeometallurgy guidance](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/archaeometallurgy-guidelines-best-practice/heag003-archaeometallurgy-guidelines/): sheltered hearth/bellows work; hearth forms vary.
- [Historic England commerce and exchange guide](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/dlsg-commerce-exchange-buildings/heag121-commerce-and-exchange-lsg/) and [Elstow Moot Hall](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1136906): covered trading and shop/public-space relationships; not a universal hall plan.
- [Historic England agricultural buildings guide](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/dlsg-agricultural-buildings/heag122-agricultural-buildings-lsg/): ventilation, grooming, stall divisions and hay storage; later typologies do not define every medieval stable.
- [Etal Castle listing](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1011644): defended passage, flanking towers, guardrooms and portcullis housing.

References inform construction principles only. Geometry/code/materials remain
original under the existing GPL-3.0-or-later add-on license; no external models
or RuneScape designs are copied.
