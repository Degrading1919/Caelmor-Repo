In the supplied Fort Forinthry clay screenshot, the base, joints, and gate surround carry most of the depth. The broad middle wall has much less relief, and the material screenshot adds the finer courses. Sampled near-parallel stone faces in the extracted wall and rear gate GLBs also sit at multiple offsets. The transferable principle is to spend geometry on footing, bond, and water shedding. The study uses its own proportions and joint schedule. The extracted assets and screenshots stayed private.

The candidate keeps a continuous core and adds staggered facing courses with eased, tapered stone profiles. The footing projects furthest, especially at the end. Alternating long headers mark the tower joint and outer end. The upper stones seat the cap, and the cap overhang sheds water clear of the face. The ordinary field is shallower. A seed varies the small cuts and projection within each role's range. The study dresses the road face of one return.

At the original gameplay camera, the baseline reads as a plain extrusion. The candidate reads as stacked masonry even in clay. The strongest cue is the alternating depth along the exposed end. The foot and cap are easier to distinguish in the same-frame crops. There is still a regular cadence at the end, and the return is a small part of the whole silhouette. That limits what this angle can establish about a longer wall or an approach in direct light.

The triangle count rose from 66,483 to 67,143 across the Gatehouse, an increase of **660 triangles, about 1.0%**. The tested change affected one geometry buffer and retained its stone material assignment and the existing atlas pipeline. Blender matched the baseline settings, camera, and lighting. It verified deterministic replay, preserved all 15 collision proxy meshes, and confirmed that the new wall bounds stay clear of the five declared routes. The return is a visual site interface in both cases. Its adjoining site wall and collision would be authored separately. The GLB round trip preserved the wall's triangle count, texture bindings, and sampled material pixels.

**Pass** for the controlled technical study. The geometry merits a reusable option for short Thornfell wall returns after artistic review.
