## Reading the evidence

The fixed comparison settings are seed 91, ESTABLISHED wealth, STANDARD craftsmanship, age 35, maintenance 0.7, MEDIUM detail, EFFICIENT geometry, World construction and Regional Grammar on, and context AUTO. AUTO resolves to RURAL (Lowmark), RIDGE (Thornfell), SORROWMERE (Mire), and EMBERHOLD (Emberholt). The generator source commit is `809300bcfddf67601fa6719ebf4e91aef9135098` (shown as `809300bc` on sheets). Camera scale is common across the four regions within each type and independently fit across types; the two views use opposed front and rear angles. The first view includes the entrance/functional frontage. These are flat studio-ground previews, not settlement or terrain presentations.

Each archetype manifest records its source revision and source hash, settings contract, four regional settings and validation records, geometry hashes, resolved context, and both view records with camera direction and orthographic scale. The contact-sheet manifest records each sheet hash and dimensions, plus the declared and observed hashes of each included individual render. A missing, failed, invalid, hash-mismatched, or wrongly sized view is shown as a labeled placeholder; it is never replaced by another image.

The clay pass is for reading massing and silhouette. The material pass exposes the current regional surface treatment. These are generated exterior previews; they do not establish interior completion, gameplay readiness, player experience, or art acceptance. Use the linked individual renders and manifests for per-view inspection and provenance.

## Reproduce and verify

The completed pass contains **80 rendered configurations, 320 individual PNGs
and 20 contact sheets (4316 × 3080 pixels each)**. There were **zero unsupported,
invalid or failed combinations**, and no settings exceptions or substitutions.
File coverage, hashes/dimensions, matched cameras/settings, and unchanged generator
source/installable archive checks passed. Independent read-only presentation review
of Miller House, Watchtower, Market Hall and Boathouse sheets found no concrete
labeling, framing, ordering or render-contamination blocker. This was a bounded
presentation check, not artistic acceptance of the buildings.

From `CODE/Scripts/architectural_generator`, with Blender 5.0.1:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.0\blender.exe' --factory-startup --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tests/render_household_review.py -- --archetype-gallery --gallery-resume --source-revision 809300bcfddf67601fa6719ebf4e91aef9135098
./tests/compose_archetype_gallery.ps1
py -3 tests/verify_household_evidence.py --archetype-gallery
```

Resume accepts only complete matching source/settings/image hashes. Each native
backend build generates and validates one configuration; its objects are reused
for all four images by temporarily replacing and restoring material slots. The
existing studio, opposing cameras, camera-fit calculation and System.Drawing
composition are reused. No architectural logic, installed package, settings
defaults or existing evidence were changed. [`verification.json`](verification.json)
records coverage, hashes and any unsupported/failed combinations. Verification
checks provenance, camera/settings consistency and file coverage rather than
rerunning unrelated regressions, exports or engine tests. Artistic defects are
intentionally left visible for Creative Director review.
