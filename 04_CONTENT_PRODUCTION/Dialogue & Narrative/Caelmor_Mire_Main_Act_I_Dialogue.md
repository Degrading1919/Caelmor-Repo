# Mire Main Act I: Cast and Dialogue (The Name at Sorrowmere, The Banners That Refuse to Fall)

**Status:** Authored production content for Creative Director milestone review (2026-11-08). This is the companion to `04_CONTENT_PRODUCTION/Quests/Caelmor_Mire_Main_Act_I.md`, which owns step ids, flags, encounters and mechanics. Characters marked **[CANON ANCHOR]** come from `LORE/Characters/Caelmor_Characters_Region3_Mire_Of_Fallen_Banners_Anchors (Not Full List).txt` and obey the Mire rows of the Part 4 character ledger (`00_ADMIN/Reference/Caelmor_Part4_Character_Family_Continuity_Ledger.md`): nothing here gives them a new age, wartime unit or service, kinship, travel or record custody beyond what the ledger allows. Characters marked **[NEW]** come from the Mire bible §5.2 or are incidental role names, and are not canon until promoted (MD-16: promote nothing). Every node that names Oswin Garvey's causeway decision or his crews' standing-up is tagged `[CAUSEWAY]` (bible §3.1). Every node that carries the Hayle/Ferrow case is MD-2 content (tagged once per section, not per line). Nothing here is production JSON.

**Node format** (Lowmark/Thornfell standard): `NODE SPEAKER: "Line." (stage note)`; conditions in braces or brackets before the line; player options as `> [condition] "Option." → NEXT {effect}`. Ids follow bible §11.2: dialogue `<CODE>-D<n>.<nn>`, barks `<CODE>-B<n>.<nn>`, inspect texts in `-I` tables, documents in full.

**Voice rules (bible §5.3, binding):** plain, heavy and kind; short sentences; no false hope. Seen, then thought, said out loud. Nobody speaks for the dead: no "she'd want", no "at peace". Nobody calls anything magic; the ground is heavy, full, remembering; Wraiths are "impressions" to Memorykeepers and "the risen" to crews. Water and ground grammar ("she's running / slack / sour"; "that ground won't have you"). Care before explanation: food before questions. Record-keeping is love. Strangers are "traveller"; people who saw a reading say "witness". Humour is dry and kept for tables and work.

## Part 2: Cast notes

### MR-M1 (`MNS`) `[MD-2]`

#### Ysolde Haverel, Memorykeeper, keeper of the Names Below register [NEW]
- **Role:** keeps Sorrowmere's register and index boards; reads hearings; writes entries.
- **Want:** a page that will still be true in fifty years. **Fear:** rounding a number because a room wants it rounded.
- **Knows:** the register, its forms, the stranger's seat as a custom. The two-holds rule. **Doesn't know:** why the seat exists (R16, withheld); anything a book does not say.
- **Voice:** precise, kind, unhurried. Will not round. Says the source before she says the thing. When she is moved she becomes more exact, not less.

#### Nell Ferrow, fostered woman of Sorrowmere [NEW]
- **Role:** the living person at the centre of the case. Came in off the Standards road as a small child.
- **Want:** for everyone to stop deciding things about her. **Fear:** that whichever name is written, she loses someone.
- **Knows:** her life as a Ferrow; that she "came in on the road"; a scrap of a tune. Has the charm on a cord. **Doesn't know:** her birth name.
- **Voice:** funny when frightened, and frightened a lot this week. Short jokes, mostly at her own expense. Decides about her own name, and asks the witness first because she wants to hear it from somebody who isn't anybody's cousin.

#### Agna Ferrow, Nell's foster mother [NEW]
- **Role:** raised Nell. Old, sharp, lame on wet ground.
- **Want:** "Ferrow" on the page. **Fear:** being written out of thirty years.
- **Voice:** orders people to sit and eat. Says the hard thing first. "I did the raising. Write that." Never cruel to Dunstan; never warm to him either, until the table.

#### Dunstan Hayle, Marshfall peat-cutter [NEW]
- **Role:** keeps the Hayle household book. Was a boy when his mother and small sister were lost on the Standards road.
- **Want:** his sister's name cut on a stone. **Fear:** that there was something he could have done at nine years old.
- **Knows:** the book; the house mark; his mother's song (he does not know he knows it until he sings it). **Doesn't know:** that the bones are his mother's, until the reading.
- **Voice:** says less than he means. Brings food instead of words. Calls his mother "Mam". When he nearly says "Linny" he stops and starts the sentence again.

#### Edris Hollowmere, informal caretaker [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** groundskeeper of the Fallen Fields; soft-spoken; patient to exhaustion; refuses to rush anything that involves the dead; survived the war by tending wounded; chose to stay; believes the Mire "knows when it's being respected".
- **In MR-M1:** walks the causeway with her crew; gives her care account; keeps her care leaf; closes her old station.
- **Knows:** what she did that night. That the causeway should have waited (MR-M2). **Doesn't know:** who the child's mother was.
- **Voice:** quiet, slow, exact about her own actions and silent about their meaning. "That's what I did. What it means is Ysolde's work."
- **Ledger guard:** owns her own care account only; no unit, age or service; never turns a manifestation into a witness; "respect" is ethics, not proof the land has agency.

#### Mara Fenholt, bone-gatherer and marker-setter [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** locates remains when the ground releases them; never forcing, never digging too deep; quiet, methodical, deeply respectful; marks with simple stones, never names.
- **In MR-M1:** keeps the marker and the tape; gives her account of what she saw.
- **Voice:** few words, all of them about the ground. "Seen" and "thought" in that order, and often only "seen".
- **Ledger guard:** never names a person or an army; a bone needs a second channel before anyone is named.

#### Lysa Thornettle, herbalist and peat healer [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** treats exhaustion, memory bleed, grief that won't settle; healing is about making space.
- **In MR-M1:** sits with Agna, takes Nell to Fenward, walks the cart home as the reed-cutter's healer, teaches the lantern moss.
- **Voice:** practical, warm, never soothing with lies. "I won't tell you it'll be all right. I'll tell you what we're doing about it."
- **Ledger guard:** present care only.

#### Hob Merrit, Marshfall fowler and fletcher [NEW]
- **In MR-M1:** lends the hide, or a bird off his string. One line of fowling craft.
- **Voice:** cheerful, patient, swears at herons.

### MR-M2 (`MBR`)

#### Calwen Reed-Binder, banner tender and weaver [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** collects banner fragments from the peat; washes, binds and stores them without sorting by allegiance; "They all bled the same once they hit the mud"; knows which fabrics rot slower; refuses to identify banners by faction; says the land already knows who they belonged to.
- **In MR-M2:** gives the quest; teaches the fold and the states; reads bindings and refuses whose; does either rite properly; keeps the shard.
- **Voice:** dry, exact about cloth, immovable about sides. Uses "seen / thought" with a straight face to shut down a question.
- **Ledger guard:** a banner needs a second channel before naming anyone; never confirms the Banner That Sank Twice is the one in the story.

#### Corra Sallis, young Bannerbearer [NEW]
- **Want:** to carry a real banner, not a mood banner. **Fear:** being sent home.
- **Voice:** earnest, quick, a little breathless. Says "Yes, Calwen" and does the opposite. Knows the old stories by heart and tells them properly, with "they say" in the right places.

#### Oswin Garvey, Reedsward crew-lead [NEW] `[CAUSEWAY]`
- **Role:** relaid the Standards Causeway this spring without waiting for the Silt hearing.
- **Want:** people fed before the long wet. **Fear:** that he woke something by being decent.
- **Knows:** why he relaid it; that his crews stood the standards up. **Doesn't know:** that standing them up is what woke them.
- **Voice:** tired, decent, defensive. Confesses without being pushed twice. "People eat before hearings."

#### Returning: Mara, Edris, Ysolde, Dunstan
- **Mara:** digs to the foot, never under; will not carry what isn't bone.
- **Edris:** "I asked you to wait." Says it once, to Oswin, and nothing else to him.
- **Ysolde:** keeps the books as records; will not touch the shard.
- **Dunstan:** stacking banners; lines vary by `MR_NAME_RECORD`.
