## Part 2: Cast notes

### Corvin Tath, grain factor [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** manages grain flow for House Fenmere at the Crestbridge granaries. Speaks in numbers, weights and losses, never in opinions. Methodical and unsentimental. Keeps duplicate tallies "just in case". Refuses Emberholt priority contracts. Burned one ledger after the armistice and regrets it.
- **In Act I:** his two tally racks agree, which is the problem. He first reads the goblin hole as the answer.
- **Want:** a count that closes. **Fear:** a count that closes on paper and not in the bays.
- **Knows:** exact draws (120), the cart count (40), how sacks leave his granary. **Doesn't know:** where the grain went, or the chute.
- **Voice:** sums and units. Turns a feeling into a number: "I'm eighty sacks of worried." Never says "I think". Doesn't thank anyone; he pays them.
- **Canon guard:** the burned ledger is **not** mentioned aloud in Act I. The bible gives him one aloud regret, and it is reserved for MQ10. The bare shelf in the tally room is the only nod to it.
- **Change:** he cuts a third tally stick in front of the player. That is trust, shown and not said.

### Aldric Fenmere, acting Steward [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** governs as a steward who administers. Polite, reserved, impeccably fair; rarely raises his voice; treats decisions as necessity. Keeps ledgers older than the war and refuses to destroy them. Quietly ashamed.
- **In Act I:** a witness to the Board's draw, as stewards are. He reads the slip and cannot act on it.
- **Knows:** Fenmere's old supply compacts name a wartime channel called the Relief Cut, closed after the armistice. The Board is pressing his stewardship. **Doesn't know:** that the Cut is open, or who sealed the slip.
- **Voice:** complete, courteous sentences with the conclusion last. Calls the player "you" and Corvin "Master Tath". Uses "necessary" when he means "I'm sorry".

### Keeper-Captain Bram Hesketh [NEW]
- **Role:** commands the Crestbridge Keepers.
- **Want:** no riots. **Fear:** that he signed something terrible.
- **Knows:** he signs blanket emergency draws because the Board says the far bank will riot without grain. **Doesn't know:** the Cut, the chute, any of it.
- **Voice:** clipped and tired. Speaks of his keepers by number and with pride: "Twelve on the bridge. Good ones." He never finishes a sentence about the Board.

### Edda Marrick, Provisioner, House Marrick, Provision Board [NEW] `[ANTAGONIST]`
- **In Act I:** glimpsed only, thanking Hesketh in the granary yard (MQ2).
- **Want:** her house recognized as Lowmark's true steward. **Fear:** being remembered as a profiteer.
- **Voice:** warm, reasonable and generous with other people's time. She speaks of "the households" and never names anyone. She never raises her voice. She is pleasant to the player and does not stop walking.

### Gideon Sallow, Marrick's river factor [NEW] `[ANTAGONIST]`
- **In Act I:** seen across the Hollow Lock's chamber (MQ4); escapes by skiff. Not fought until MQ10.
- **Want:** to be paid. **Fear:** the fen.
- **Voice:** cheerful and quick. Talks to his crew like a man running a fair stall. He touches the lock-chain before every flush and says "Fen's listening." It is the only time he isn't smiling.

### Bryn Ruddock, orchard ditch-turner [NEW]
- **Want:** her trees kept. **Fear:** being called a mage.
- **Knows:** every ditch on her berms, and the nights her pool rose with no rain (she chalks them). **Doesn't know:** what rises in the pool, and she doesn't want a word for it.
- **Voice:** brisk, proud, and profane under her breath ("Rot and roots"). She praises a ditch the way other people praise a horse. Any hint that what she does is "magic" gets a flat stare.

### Lessa Mirepath, fen-edge guide [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** guides caravans through marsh edges. Quiet and practical. Speaks to the land as if it listens. Treats some paths as permanently closed regardless of payment, and refuses to explain why routes are "done".
- **In Act I:** has held a rope on Orrin Tull for a day and a night at Widow's Step. She won't walk the drowned causeway. "That path's done."
- **Knows:** Orrin's boots, the Step's stakes, and the fen edge's footing. **Doesn't know:** why the water went wrong, and she wouldn't say if she guessed.
- **Voice:** few words, and often not to the player. She talks to the bank and the reeds in the same tone she uses for people. Never explains.

### Elspeth Quill, Flow-Mender [NEW]
- **Want:** fewer people walking into water. **Voice:** gentle and practical. She lists causes like ingredients. Never wastes softmoss and says so.
- **In Act I:** treats Orrin at Reedmere. She gives only ordinary causes, and they are true.

### Ansel Corrie, Wraithwatcher (returning from TL) [NEW]
- **Change from TL:** in the True Line he only foreshadowed. In Act I he fights beside the player once (the orchard), teaches the one rule every Watcher knows, and **writes down what the player tells him**. He does not solve anything. His single contribution of knowledge is patrol geography (Bramblelock).
- **Voice:** gentle gallows humour. Writes as he talks. "Drift", "heavy water", "fills".
