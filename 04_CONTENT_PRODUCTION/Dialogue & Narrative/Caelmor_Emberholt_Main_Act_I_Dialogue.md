**Status:** Authored production content for Creative Director milestone review (2026-11-14). This is the companion to `04_CONTENT_PRODUCTION/Quests/Caelmor_Emberholt_Main_Act_I.md`, which owns step ids, flags, encounters and mechanics. Characters marked **[CANON ANCHOR]** come from `LORE/Characters/Caelmor_Characters_Region4_Emberholt.txt` and obey the Emberholt rows of the Part 4 character ledger (`00_ADMIN/Reference/Caelmor_Part4_Character_Family_Continuity_Ledger.md`): nothing here gives them a new age, wartime service, kinship, travel or record custody beyond what the ledger allows. Nodes that name the valve order, Sarne Ardane, Ralenne's scene or the EH-M1 choice are tagged `[ALLOCATION]`; the EH-M2 offer lines `[ED-20]`; the EH-M3 gate and variants `[ED-7]`; the chamber roll's legible line `[INITIATE]` / `[ED-3]`; protected Volkhari testimony and Maeryn's heritage `[ED-12]`; the broadsheet `[ED-14]`. Nothing here is production JSON.

**Node format** (Lowmark/Thornfell/Mire standard): `NODE SPEAKER: "Line." (stage note)`; conditions in brackets before the line; player options as `> [condition] "Option." → NEXT {effect}`. Ids follow bible §11.2: dialogue `<CODE>-D<n>.<nn>`, barks `<CODE>-B<n>.<nn>`, inspect texts `<CODE>-I<n>` in tables, documents in full.

**Voice rules (bible §5.3, binding):** civic pride first; criticism comes from inside. Craft, never magic; nobody calls a vein alive. Fire grammar: running warm, banked, flaring, gone grey. Wardens read tells: "Read the tell, not the talk." Volkhari speak short and guarded and decide what is told. Children are children: no sympathetic speaker calls a child gifted, dangerous or an asset; teachers say "student". The player is "outsider" or "traveller", then "warden's hand" after the ward is reopened. Other regions are never caricatured; no enemy has a direction. Humour is quick and warm and lives in Cooling Houses and forges. Plain sentences.

## Part 2: Cast notes

### EH-M1 (`ECW`)

#### Veska Dunmor, Cinderwall fire warden [NEW]
- **Role:** keeps the Cinderwall junction and the ward's line. Blamed for the cold ward.
- **Want:** her name back, and the ward warm. **Fear:** that being right never matters if you're quiet about it.
- **Knows:** every tell in the ward; the warden's rules; that she protested in writing twice; that the board never balances. **Doesn't know:** where the shortfall goes; who cut her page.
- **Voice:** proud, tired, exact. Speaks in short instructions. Points instead of explaining. Never says "I told you so", although she did.

#### Yorven Khelt, District Overseer of Cinderwall [CANON ANCHOR]
- **Canon:** blunt; voice like crackling coal; loyal to his district; rose from miner; keeps Deepbrand and Emberguard heavy-handedness out; has seen surges that contradict the official safety line; knows older tunnels.
- **In Act I:** wants an outsider on the line (EH-M1); keeps the ledger in his strongbox (EH-M2 `evidence`); comes up out of his old tunnel and leads the player out (EH-M3).
- **Knows:** Cinderwall, its tunnels. **Doesn't know:** what the precursor did; who draws on the main.
- **Voice:** loud, plain, fair. Makes jokes that are also orders.

#### Hennet Ulm, Cooling House cook [NEW]
- **Voice:** feeds first. "Sit down; you're running hot." Bangs a ladle when he wants quiet. Knows everybody's soup order and most of their business.

#### Odile Prace, valve-house tallyman [NEW]
- **Voice:** counts aloud, even mid-sentence. "The board's never balanced. That's not news; it's weather." Honest about the book, protective of it, never political.

#### Aubin Harl, night clerk at the Gate Valve House [NEW]
- **Role:** cut Veska's protest page from the book. Kept it.
- **Voice:** young, quick, ashamed. Confesses badly and all at once.

#### Ralenne Kharveil, Magistrix of civic ceremonies [CANON ANCHOR] `[ALLOCATION]`
- **Canon:** theatrical, charismatic; sincere devotion to morale; receives script "suggestions" she believes inspired; unaware of conditioning.
- **In EH-M1:** did not read an order in her name; owns it; countersigns a reopening; comes to Cinderwall for the account.
- **Limits:** never a villain, never targetable; no line touches her scripts' conditioning (EH-M5's business).
- **Voice:** big, warm, a little performed, and genuinely shaken when she sees the slate. Speaks in images ("one city") and means them.

#### Sarne Ardane, the Magistrix's aide [NEW] `[ALLOCATION]`
- **Voice:** polished, embarrassed, not cruel. "It was nine nights." Does not name the salon. Has never been to Cinderwall.
