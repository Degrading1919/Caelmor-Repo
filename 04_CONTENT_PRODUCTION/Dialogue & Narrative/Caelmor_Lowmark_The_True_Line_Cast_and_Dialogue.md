## Part 1 — Lowmark voice rules

These rules apply to every Lowmark NPC, not only this quest. They are drawn from the dossier's culture section (§III, §XII) and the character knowledge limits in the Part 4 continuity ledger, and extended where marked [NEW].

### 1.1 Register

- **Plain and concrete.** Lowmarkers talk in the nouns of work: notch, post, dike, sack, stool, bank, ditch, sluice, tally. Abstractions are rare and sound slightly embarrassed.
- **Understatement over drama.** Fear is said flatly ("Water's over the notch"), grief sideways ("Remembrance should be light enough to carry"). Raised voices mean something has gone badly wrong.
- **Seasons, not days.** Time is measured in harvests, floods and crossings (dossier §XII). People who count hours are usually from Crestbridge, or carters paid on delivery.
- **Dry humor.** The default mode under pressure. Jokes are short and aimed at oneself, at weather, or at authority.
- **Suspicion of loud power.** Flamboyance, grand claims, and "big magic" draw unease ("That's Emberholt talk") [NEW phrasing; attitude CANON §III].

### 1.2 Line length

- Typical line: 6–20 words. At most two sentences per line for named characters.
- At most one longer line per conversation, and only when it reveals who the character is (Tenet 18).
- No conversation keeps the player more than six NPC lines from a choice or from control.
- No exposition about the war, the Riverwardens or magic unless the character would plausibly say it to a stranger.

### 1.3 How Lowmarkers talk about water practice and the strange

| Topic | Ordinary folk | Crews and keepers | Wraithwatchers | Never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple farm water practice (CANON, openly taught) | A knack or a skill: "She's good with the ditch-water." "Turning the field water." | A job someone is trusted with. | Not their concern. | "Spell", "casting", "mana", "arcane". |
| Loud or combat magic (CANON, feared) | Lowered voice, short: "Don't talk like that." | Treated as a hazard. | "Not our business. Get people away from it." | Admiration. |
| Wraiths (CANON: place-bound water-memory manifestations, not souls) | "Hauntings", "something in the water", "the drowned" (belief). | "Bad water", "a bend that's gone wrong". | **"Drift", "heavy water"** [NEW jargon]. Lanterns, rope, smoke. | A credible character calling them souls, or one naming who a Wraith "was". |
| Odd water behavior | "The river's in a mood." "Water sat funny." | "That bend's done." | Logged by place and time. | A character explaining the cause with certainty. |
| The Riverwardens | Children's stories, old songs, "the listeners" [NEW folk term]. | Unknown. | Rumor. | Anyone in Lowmark speaking of them as a living people. |

### 1.5 Addressing the player

Who the player is remains undecided (quest package §14, item 2). Every line is written so that it works for any origin:
- No name, gender, title, homeland, ancestry or prophecy. "You" is the default; "friend" and "traveler" are used sparingly and only by characters who would use them.
- NPCs react to what the player has **done**, never to what the player **is**.
- Player options are short, first person, and plain. No option is a joke at an NPC's expense.

### 1.6 Names

Lowmark names are short with rural, work-and-landscape surnames (canon: Rowe, Reed, Fen, Pell, Tath, Mirepath, Fenmere). New names follow the pattern and were checked against every `LORE/` and reference file for collisions: Avery, Tarrow, Ferrin, Corrie, Dace (no matches found).

## Part 2 — Cast

Each entry lists want, fear, what the character knows, what they believe, what they don't know, voice, and how they change. "Knows / believes / doesn't know" follows the Part 4 and Part 5 character controls, so no character says more than they could.

### Maelin Rowe — barge master [CANON ANCHOR]

- **Canon:** long-route barge master at Brookhollow docks / Whisperflow Run; has run Lowmark's rivers since before the armistice; calm, efficient, observant; never asks what she's hauling; refuses night passage through specific stretches.
- **In this quest:** keeps the Whisperflow crossing. She has refused to cross since dawn because the marker is wrong and she can't prove it to a keeper.
- **Want:** nobody drowns on her crossing.
- **Fear:** being made to cross by paper.
- **Knows:** every post on this bank; that posts here have always been seated on "the old stone"; that her teacher did the same; that this bend sometimes "does that" with the water.
- **Believes:** private river knowledge shouldn't need explaining.
- **Doesn't know:** what the stone is, who carved it, who moved the post.
- **Voice:** fewest words in the cast. Statements, not questions. Never explains a bend. Gives instructions as facts. Dry, rare humor. Calls Hollis "Avery" and the player "you".
- **Change:** learns that private expertise needs an explanation people can check (Part 5 story web). She hands the player the choice instead of taking the evidence herself.

### Hollis Avery — Crestbridge Keeper at the Whisperflow tollpost [NEW]

- **Role:** junior Crestbridge Keeper, posted to the Whisperflow tollpost this season. Born after the armistice.
- **Want:** to do the job properly and get the grain to the people waiting for it.
- **Fear:** being the keeper who held up bread on the strength of a feeling.
- **Knows:** the emergency provision procedure; that the order is correctly formatted; the keepers' crossing sheet (which does not show the drowned pier).
- **Believes:** procedure protects people, because it is the only thing that can be checked.
- **Doesn't know:** the issuer's mark; the river.
- **Voice:** complete sentences, slightly formal. Cites the provision. Calls Maelin "Mistress Rowe". Corrects himself mid-line. Honest even when it's awkward ("That's... not wrong, technically.").
- **Change:** the player shows him that procedure can be used to lie. He shuts the crossing himself, by the river, and writes down the first thing he has ever written that mattered.

### Wynn Tarrow — carter [NEW]

- **Role:** carter for hire, hauling forty sacks of Crestbridge granary grain to the far-bank households. Paid on delivery.
- **Want:** to deliver and be paid; she has three mouths at home.
- **Fear:** a second day lost on this bank.
- **Knows:** the order was handed to her at Crestbridge yesterday at noon; she offered a coin to whoever would prove the line safe.
- **Doesn't know:** the issuer's mark ("I don't read marks, I read sacks").
- **Voice:** fast, blunt, counting. Jokes to keep her temper. Calls the player "friend".
- **Function:** the obvious suspect. Ruled out by Nan.

### Nan Ferrin — returning householder [NEW]

- **Role:** older woman going home across the river after a stay at her daughter's in Brookhollow. Carries a trunk and a hen called Duchess.
- **Want:** her own bed.
- **Knows:** the posts were all grey at dusk and the yellow one was there at first light; she was awake all night with her hip; Wynn snored under her cart until dawn; her mother's crossing rhyme and humming tune.
- **Believes:** the rhyme is just something her mother said.
- **Doesn't know:** what the stone is or who "the listeners" were.
- **Voice:** slow, teasing, precise about small things. Treats the hen with full formality.
- **Function:** the key witness, the rhyme, and the warmth of the camp.

### Dace — Maelin's deckhand [NEW]

- **Role:** Maelin's youngest crew hand.
- **Want:** to be useful, and to be forgiven.
- **Knows:** what it felt like when the skiff hit the pilings.
- **Did:** took the skiff out at first light along the false line for Wynn's coin and holed it on the drowned pier.
- **Voice:** quick, defensive, then honest in one breath.
- **Change:** apologizes to Maelin if the camp is fed.

### Tovin Pell — dock laborer [CANON ANCHOR]

- **Canon:** dock laborer and mill runner at Brookhollow and Crestbridge; notices reflections that lag, tools moved, water sounds without current; assumes something is wrong with him; sketches river bends and mill floors; never talks about it unless asked directly; does not understand, name, or seek answers.
- **In this quest:** loading Wynn's sacks. Years ago he sketched this bend, including the old post and a mark at its foot.
- **Knows:** his sketch; that "water used to sit funny there."
- **Voice:** apologetic, short, concrete. Describes; never explains. Hesitates before every admission.
- **Canon guard:** he never names the phenomenon, never seeks the answer, and is never the one who solves anything. The payoff is that someone tells him he isn't imagining it.

### Ansel Corrie — Wraithwatcher [NEW]

- **Role:** contracted Wraithwatcher walking the Whisperflow-to-fen-edge patrol every second night, with oil lantern, blessed rope and banner-thistle smoke (CANON tools; no spellcasting).
- **Knows:** where and when the water has gone wrong this month; that Stillwater's fog has been unusually heavy.
- **Believes:** his job is where and when; someone cleverer can do the why.
- **Voice:** gentle gallows humor. Writes everything down. Uses "drift" and "heavy water".
- **Function:** the hook toward movement 3 and Stillwater Fen. He foreshadows; he does not fight anything in this quest.

### Role-based crowd (unnamed)

Two or three households, a child and parent, and a couple of farm hands waiting at the cart-stand. Their barks carry the mood and the theme.

## Part 3 — Dialogue

### Format

```
NODE  SPEAKER: "Line."                       (stage note)
  > [condition] "Player option."  → NEXT     {effect}
```

- `[condition]` uses flags and items from the quest package §9.2. No condition means always shown.
- `{effect}` sets a flag, gives or takes an item, or emits the dialogue-node-reached event named in the quest package.
- Options already used are hidden unless marked *(repeatable)*.
- Stage notes describe gesture only; there are no cutscenes and the camera stays with the player.
