It turns the approved narrative architecture (`Caelmor_Narrative_Architecture.md` and its four companion files; rulings in `Caelmor_Narrative_Architecture_Review_and_Authoring_Sequence.md` §5.0) into a concrete, playable slate for the Thornfell Marches. Everything marked **[NEW]** is authored and not canon until the Creative Director promotes it. Everything marked **[PROPOSAL — DIRECTOR]** is a consequential story choice the content is built on but that Collin may replace; §3 lists exactly what changes if he does.

**Scope guard.** Thornfell is the Phase 1.3 v1.5 direction. Only Lowmark is approved for release. This package authorizes **no** release scope, no schema, no production JSON and no C#. It is schema-ready markdown for the day Roadmap 31.2/31.3/31.5 exist (Master Protocol §5.1). Technical needs are documented for later integration in the integration handoff (§12, Batch 4), never implemented here.

## 1. Thornfell in one page

***The Wall That Was Kept.*** Winter is coming early to the high passes. The player comes up to **Passwatch** as a stranger on the day the Ridge Wardens decide to close Windscar Pass, and is put to work at once: a highstead's winter food is on the wrong side of a broken bridge, and the horns are counting down the closing.

Over five milestone quests the player learns why Thornfell, a place that prides itself on standing together, is quietly coming apart.

**The answer has three parts, and the player learns them in this order:**

1. **Someone is getting ready for a war nobody declared.** A Blood-Oath splinter that calls its hoard **the Iron Winter** is filling a forgotten wartime cliff store-house with grain, iron and salvaged horn plates, on nobody's authority. Some of that grain is Lowmark relief grain bought on an account called the **Whinstone Store**. The splinter is rebuilding "the horn that split the ridge" as a deterrent against Emberholt. The Assembly does not know. (Act I: TF-M1, TF-M2)
2. **Thornfell's proudest story is half a story.** The horn that split Shatterfall Gorge was real, and it brought the shelf down on Thornfell's own rear guard. The Summit "guardian" that wakes when the rebuilt horn is tested is no judge and no warning; it is old stone answering a disturbance the splinter made. (Act II: TF-M3, TF-M4)
3. **The wall was kept by leaving people outside it.** Cornered, the splinter fires the wartime sealing works in a blizzard while a column of clanless highstead workers is still beyond the far gate. Bringing them through, the player finds the late-war closure roster cut into the gatehouse wall: the names of those Thornfell shut out when it sealed its passes and went home from the war. (Act III: TF-M5)

The player leaves with Thornfell's tool for the peace: **an honest closure**, a closing that names who is left outside, decided by an Assembly that has no ruler.

**Emotional line (Phase 1.1 §6):** Wonder (an echo that answers before the horn is blown) → Competence (reading horn calls, wind windows and null-points) → Attachment (Tova's ropeway, Runa's tally, Dorn's fear) → Agency (who goes through first, what happens to the stores, the horn and the names) → Mastery (high routes, Thornfell master commissions, a horn network that will matter later).

**What makes it an RPG and not a debate:** every quest has a place to climb, a weather window to beat, a sound to read, danger that telegraphs, and a moment that is strange. Thornfell is talkative only in the Assembly, and even there the player's answer is where they stand, not a speech.

### 1.1 Thornfell's readable system: sound

Lowmark taught the player to read water against a marker. Thornfell teaches **sound**. It is learned knowledge (Pillar A), never a spell.

| Element | What the player learns | Where first taught | Used again |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Ordinary Calls** | Six horn calls every Marcher child knows (§9.4). Each call has a sound and, once learned, a small on-screen call-shape glyph for accessibility. | TF-M1 (Dorn) | Every milestone; *Horn and Silence*; convergence alarm network |
| **Echo count** | Count the beats between a call and its echo off a face: fewer beats, nearer face; an echo that comes back *wrong* means the face has moved (rockfall) or the air has changed (storm). | TF-M1 (Aud, at the Narrows) | TF-M3 (gorge), TF-M5 (deadfall galleries) |
| **Wind windows** | The hard wind comes in gusts with a tell: the fluted faces whistle up a fifth before a gust. Shelter behind a face, then move. | TF-M1 | TF-M3, TF-M5, high routes |
| **Null-points** | Echohewn sound-dead pockets. Footsteps vanish, dust settles straight down. In a null-point, resonance cannot carry. | TF-M4 (Kaldren, Iven); foreshadowed in the Silent Gorge discovery | TF-M5, *The Notes We Keep* |
| **Refused calls** | Three calls nobody plays: **the Split** (the wartime forcing call), **the Third Horn** (an omitted reply), **Witness at the Gate** (two long, one held; meaning forgotten). | Overheard in TF-M1 (Ressa's refusal); explained in *Horn and Silence* | R06, R16 seeds |

## 2. Author truth

This is the private truth behind the arc. Players learn it gradually and never from one source. It keeps the architecture's three causes: **a present human decision** (the Iron Winter), **older damaged infrastructure** (the horn seat, the sealing works, the Summit halls), and **bounded resonance** (the Horn-Echo and the Summit node) that never testifies.

### 2.1 The old works [CANON core; NEW detail]

- **Echohewn halls and fluted faces [CANON].** Mostly acoustic engineering; rare works interact with dormant air resonance. Windscar's fluted faces channel wind and make the pass whistle before a gust. Echochamber Summit's halls carry a whisper impossible distances, and the Summit sits on a passive Stonebound node [CANON C11].
- **Null-points [CANON].** Echohewn sound-dampening pockets. Builders used them as safe rooms for loud work and quiet rooms for counting. The clans think they are solemn ground.
- **Horn plates [CANON].** Echohewn plates of tuned stone and horn, salvaged in the war and bolted into horns and emplacements to force resonance.
- **The Horn Seat at Shatterfall [NEW].** A wartime emplacement cut into the gorge wall: a stone seat with a rack of resonance plates aimed down the shelf. From here the wartime call **the Split** was sounded. A log tin was kept in a niche: the horn-station roster.
- **The sealing works at the Gatefall [NEW].** The far gate of Windscar Pass is a narrow cut with **deadfall galleries** above it: stacked stone held back by timber props, wedges and iron pins. Each gallery's marker stone is cut with the call-glyph that ordered its release. Most were dropped in the war and are **spent**. This autumn the Iron Winter quietly **re-propped** three of them with fresh pale timber (a clue visible from below, TDS10). Below them, the **gatehouse** holds the **gate-slab**, a great stone door on a winch that can shut the cut in a few breaths. [PROPOSAL — DIRECTOR TD-8: at the Gatefall the sealing was mechanical, ordered by horn call. This **narrows FT-02b for this one site only**: FT-02b's "horn-made collapses" stand for other passes and for Shatterfall.]
- **The closure roster [NEW].** When the passes were sealed in the war's last years, the gate-warden at the Gatefall cut into the gatehouse wall, beside each sealing date, the names of those known to be on the far side when the slab came down. Nobody ordered it; nobody read it after. Plaster and smoke covered it. The gate-warden is unnamed [TD-10].

### 2.2 The war [APPROVED FT-02b; NEW detail]

- Thornfell's forced horns brought passes down on attackers and on its own people. At **Shatterfall**, the Split was sounded from the Horn Seat while a Thornfell **rear guard** was still on the lower shelf below. The shelf fell on them. Thornfell never speaks of its own dead at Shatterfall [H16]: the rim cairns carry names with no bodies and the words "lost in the gorge storm". Only Maera's private cairn list says they "held the gorge" and gives their last sighting. Who blew the horn and how many died stay **withheld** [R06].
- Over the war's last years the Assembly pulled its forces home and sealed the high passes one by one. Refugees and late columns on the wrong side were stranded; some reached the Mire (R14); some came back over in the thaw and settled where they could. Thornfell calls this "holding the wall".
- **Hushfold [NEW, from Part 3]** began as a wartime shelter line for displaced families who could not go home. Its households are **clanless**. Several carry names that are cut into the closure roster. They do not know this.
- The sealings also caused Thornfell's own **hunger winter**: with the passes shut, trade and relief could not come in, and relief trains that reached the far gate were turned back. The gatehouse roster records those turn-backs beside the sealing dates (TF-M5). Thornfell remembers that winter and blames Emberholt. (Quiet mirror, never stated in Thornfell: the same sealings helped push Emberholt toward its gamble [War History §VI]. Seed only.)

### 2.3 The present wrong: the Iron Winter [APPROVED FT-03; NEW detail]

- **The splinter [NEW].** A handful of Blood-Oath households and clanless hires, led by **Ingrith Scarrow** `[SPLINTER]`, a former pass quartermaster who was a child in the hunger winter. She believes, sincerely and wrongly, that Emberholt starved Thornfell then and will try again. The splinter acts **without** its clans or the Assembly [FT-03]. Brynjar Korr is **not** a member and does not know [character ledger].
- **The stores.** The splinter has quietly reopened the **Whinstone Store**, a wartime cliff store-house above Windscar Pass, and is filling it with grain, ridge iron, weapons and salvaged horn plates. They call the hoard *the Iron Winter*: "what we're storing for". The store-factor **Bersi Coldbrook** `[SPLINTER]` keeps the ledger and buys on the "Whinstone account" through whoever will sell. Some of what he bought is **Lowmark relief grain**, still in sacks stencilled with the Fenmere granaries' relief mark. **How the grain reached Thornfell is never stated** [FT-03 ruling; map pass]. Bersi knows only that it came to Passwatch "by the usual hands".
- **The horn.** The hornwright **Eyvind Stall** `[SPLINTER]` is rebuilding a horn from Echohewn plates salvaged at Shatterfall by Gerrit Carrick's out-of-work miners. The splinter calls it the **Wall-Horn**: a deterrent, a thing to blow from the passes so Emberholt hears that Thornfell is ready. Eyvind tests it in the Summit halls because they amplify. Each test wakes the Summit node.
- **The rumours [R15 seed only].** The splinter passes around an unsigned broadsheet: *"When the forges eat the grain, the passes go dark."* Its phrasing will turn up, reversed, in Emberholt (EH-M1). Thornfell content plants the phrase and never explains it. No Remnant presence is shown or implied in Thornfell [FT-04 bounded reach].
- **The end game.** Ingrith wants the Assembly to declare the wall closed for winter so that the Iron Winter is the region's only reserve and she is proved right. When the player takes her horn, she takes the decision herself: she fires the Gatefall's live deadfalls in an early blizzard and blames the mountain. She knows Hushfold's winter column is still beyond the far gate. She tells herself they will shelter at the far hut: "they're clanless, they know the cold." That sentence is the war again.

### 2.4 The resonance [CANON boundaries]

- **The Horn-Echo of Shatterfall [CANON].** In violent storm winds a horn blast rolls through the gorge and echo-figures form in the haze and charge blindly. They are **resonance imprints**, not spirits. They never identify anyone; no UI, NPC or text names them after real people. **Some of them run uphill**, toward the Horn Seat and away from the enemy's approach. This is a seed, not testimony; the player makes any link from the roster.
- **The Summit node [CANON C11].** The splinter's horn tests couple Echohewn resonance with the node. The response is physical: hums, dust spiralling into rough shapes, pressure pulses. It does not speak, warn, judge or choose. It stops when the horn stops [R07].
- **No new scar at the Gatefall.** The Gatefall's dangers are ordinary: gusts with a whistle tell, loose stone from the re-propped galleries, and whiteout. Resonance phenomena in Thornfell stay where canon puts them (Shatterfall, the Summit).

### 2.6 What defeating an echo means

Horn-Echo figures follow the Lowmark drift precedent (Lowmark bible §2.5): **combat disperses, conditions decide.** Striking an echo-figure breaks it; it re-forms while the storm lasts and fades when the storm window turns. The Horn-Echo is a thirty-year-old canon scar: it is not cured by any quest and remains a revisitable storm event at Shatterfall after the story. The Summit node is never fought; it stops when the disturbance stops.

### 5.3 Voice rules (binding)

1. **Understated speech** [dossier §III]. Marchers use few words; a long speech is a sign of fear or anger.
2. **No one calls anything magic.** Wind has moods, stones "remember", echoes are "tricks". The player may never be told a phenomenon is magical by any Thornfell voice. Kaldren speaks of stresses and alignments, never origins.
3. **Weather is grammar.** "The hard wind", "the forgiving wind", "a fifth before the gust".
4. **Addressing the player** is origin-neutral and address-neutral (Lowmark D-3). Marchers say "walker" to strangers. A host who adds the player to a shelter tally (TME01, *A Place at Smoke Week*) uses their name from then on; nothing else changes.
5. **Humour** is dry and at one's own expense (the First Smoke custom of a ridiculous weather story).
6. **Emberholt** is distrusted rationally and never caricatured [dossier §XXI.5].

### 6.1 Milestone briefs

These briefs bind the Act packages. The packages author beats, steps, flags, encounters, dialogue and integration notes.

**TF-M1 The Closing of Passwatch (`TPW`).** *Offer:* on arrival at Passwatch (any route; MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED). Optional hook variants: a Lowmark caravan hand's farewell line (`LM_CIVIC_STANDING`), or asking at Passwatch about "the Whinstone Store" (Lowmark MQ4 Completed): nobody admits to the name, and a haulier looks away. **Situation:** an early storm is coming. Vessa Galt will shut the **Passwatch gate** at dusk on hazard grounds **until the storm breaks** (a storm closure, not the season's sealing). Dorn's horn post counts the closing down with the **Close** call at intervals (a per-player quest clock using the Lowmark keep-watch convention; it waits on the player once the bridge is built, so it cannot run out). Nettlehook's winter grain sledge, Ketil Rask's ropeway crew and a Stonewake-bound fur-and-iron caravan are beyond the **Narrows**, where rockfall has cut the rope bridge. **Beats:** (1) Dorn teaches the Ordinary Calls at the horn post (practical: answer a Gather; take cover on a Fall). Ressa passes, hears a young warden practise a wrong interval, and stops him without explaining (refused-call seed). (2) In Passwatch's store-yard, sacks bearing the Fenmere granaries' relief marks and Crestbridge tally notches (Lowmark MQ2's marks) sit under a tarp chalked "W. acct.", waiting for hauliers to take them "up the track". Required pass-by; optional inspect; nothing says how they came (R05 seed; TD-11). (3) Cross the goat track above the **Blind Corners** (agility trigger; walkable fallback); pass bandits are using the closing to rob the stranded (fight; they yield). (4) Rebuild the Narrows bridge with Aud Hammerlee: cut stormwood stakes on the slope (Woodcutting), set fittings at Aud's field anvil (Smithing), and run the guide line across in wind windows; crew alternative (Aud's crew does it, costing one more Close call). Echo count tells whether the face above is about to shed. **Strange moment:** in the storm, Dorn's Close comes back off the cliffs before he has finished blowing it. Aud laughs too loudly. Nobody explains. (5) The patched bridge will carry one heavy load before the last Close: **Nettlehook's sledge** (the highstead eats), **the caravan** (Stonewake's market and the Wardens' pay), or **split** (lighten both; half the sledge's load and the caravan's beasts wait at Three Calls Shelter until the storm breaks). (6) Escort across under a Fall. Vessa shuts the gate at the last Close and writes on her slate: *"Passwatch shut. Dusk. Storm."* Optional: ask her to write who is outside. "We write what we closed. Not who." (`TPW_NAMES_ASKED`.) Complete when the player is inside and Dorn blows **Rest**. The storm breaks in the epilogue and the gate reopens.

**TF-M2 The Iron Winter Stores (`TIW`).** *Offer:* Elira Hearthward at Stonewake's ridge-market: the Hearthguard storekeepers' winter allotments are short because a buyer is cornering grain and iron, paying with chits nobody can cash at Stonewake. **Beats:** (1) Investigate: a haulier's chit (stamped "W."), the market price board, Passwatch's now-empty tarp; follow sledge-runner marks up the **Whinstone track** (traversal; one null-point gully where the haul crew's voices vanish, foreshadowing TF-M4). (2) The **Muster Hut**: splinter crew drilling clanless hires; a broadsheet on the wall: *"When the forges eat the grain, the passes go dark."* (R15 seed; required pass-by, optional inspect). Fight through or take the cliff ledge round it (a route choice, not stealth). (3) The **Whinstone Store**: front gate barred; enter through the **old hoist shaft** (climb with a crank cage; the cage can be wound up to make a shortcut). A dungeon in four galleries: grain gallery (the Lowmark-marked sacks; required inspect), iron gallery (new spearheads of ridge iron; Ridge Crow nests in the vents), plate gallery (crates of Echohewn horn plates with Shatterfall grit; Eyvind's tuning sketches naming the Summit's great hall), and the counting room. (4) **Bersi Coldbrook** `[SPLINTER]` and two crews in the counting room; the fight stops at surrender. **The store ledger** (`TIW_store_ledger`, quest item) lists receipts on the Whinstone account, pay chits to "G. Carrick's men, Shatterfall, plates by the crate", and one line, dated some weeks before any Lowmark barge the player could have stopped: *"Relief grain, sixty-eight measures. Received."* With Lowmark MQ4 Completed, the player recognises the number and the account (the two-halves connection; R05). Bersi testifies after he yields: the reserve is **the Iron Winter**, "for when Emberholt comes for the passes". He bought on **standing orders** carried by a countersign he never saw signed, and he will not say how the grain came ("by the usual hands"). Purchase authority stays withheld (R05). **Strange moment:** the counting room's last bay holds the Wall-Horn's **trial frame**; touching a plate makes the rock hum and every crow on the cliff lift at once. (5) **How Brynjar learns** (TD-2): **privately** (the player brings him to the store; he sees Nettlehook's children's-ration line struck through on the ledger and **disowns** the splinter) or **in public** (the player reports straight to Hadrik; Brynjar hears it before the Assembly and **defends** readiness out of pride while privately furious). (6) **What happens to the stores** (`TH_STORES`): **reported** (Assembly seal; Wardens guard it; hunger holds until the Assembly decides), **seized for relief** (Elira's storekeepers carry it down to the highsteads tonight; the evidence is the ledger alone), or, **only on the private route**, **left as leverage** (the player and Brynjar keep quiet so the splinter keeps moving where they can watch it; Elira is angry; later scenes show the stores still filling). Complete on Elira's closing scene.

**TF-M3 The Echo of Shatterfall (`TES`).** *Offer:* the pay chits lead to **Carrick's Cut**, where Gerrit Carrick's out-of-work miners have been prising horn plates from the gorge for Eyvind. A storm is coming in; two salvage pairs are still on the lower ledges. Dorn comes because he will not let strangers near "that seat" alone; Maera meets the player at the rim afterward. **Beats:** (1) Down the gorge between wind windows; crumbling shelves (agility triggers with fallbacks; Mining to clear a jammed rock, crew alternative). (2) **Rope-rig rescues:** the stranded pairs are below overhangs in haze. To rig a lowering line the player must find **sound anchor rock** they cannot see: strike the face and count the echo (a quick, flat return is solid rock; a slow, doubled one is a loose slab). Two rigs, two lowerings, while the **Horn-Echo** rises with the storm: a horn blast rolls through the gorge and echo-figures form in the haze and charge in readable lanes. They disperse when struck and re-form while the storm lasts. Some figures run **uphill**, toward the Horn Seat (seed; nothing explains it). (3) **The Held Line:** at the Horn Seat approach, figures cohere into a line that holds the path (miniboss phenomenon; never named in UI or dialogue; endure and disperse it until the storm window turns). (4) The **Horn Seat**: the plate rack aimed down the shelf. With Dorn, the player strikes a plate and listens: its echo comes back from the camp shelf below, not from the enemy approach. **Strata** below the seat: the collapse lies over a Thornfell camp: clan-stamped buckles, cookpots, a horn with Thornfell bindings, on the near side of the fall. The **log tin** in the niche: the horn-station roster (`TES_horn_roster`, quest item), with an hour, the word *Split*, and beneath it in another hand, *"Rear guard not recalled. No answer."* (5) At the rim cairns (names, no bodies, "lost in the gorge storm"), Maera brings her private list: the rear guard "held the gorge", last seen at an hour **after** the roster's Split. Two honest records disagree; the player sets them side by side. Reading "No answer" off the roster, Dorn says the Third Horn was the reply that used to mean someone was still out, and that by the roster nobody blew it (seed for *The Third Horn*). (6) **Choice** (`TH_SHATTERFALL_ACCOUNT`), the player recommends and Maera decides: **amended** (she cuts "beneath our own horn" under the names), **beside** (the roster is lodged in the cairn's niche and the stone stays as it is), or **kept** (the roster goes to the Passwatch warden post's keeping; the stone stays silent for now). One rear-guard name is also on a Hushfold family's name-slip as "went over the pass" (register seed; *The Weight of a Name*). Complete at the rim.

**TF-M4 The Summit Hall (`TSH`).** *Offer:* Iven Ridgeborne brings the player a list: nights the Summit hummed "with no storm in it". Eyvind's sketches (TF-M2) name the Summit's great hall. Kaldren comes up because "somebody has to stop you all from being clever". **Beats:** (1) The ascent: carved stairs hugging cliff faces; air-channels that blow out torches; Iven's echo that arrives before sound (the player hears their own footstep from the next landing up). (2) **The whisper hall (triangulation puzzle):** a whisper at one mouth of the hall is heard at the others. From three listening mouths the player hears the crew's voices and tools from different angles and marks on Iven's sketch where each crewman stands and which alcoves have gone silent (null-points). The map decides the route in; a wrong guess only means a harder fight, never a failure. (3) **Null-points:** Kaldren shows how to confirm them (dust falls straight; your own voice goes flat). (4) The **great hall**: Eyvind `[SPLINTER]` and the crew have mounted the Wall-Horn and are sounding test notes. With each note the node responds: the air hums, dust spills into rough shapes, pressure pulses roll out on a readable rhythm. Fight the crew between pulses; they yield. Eyvind keeps blowing until he is stopped. (5) **Silencing by ear:** each plate rings a different pitch in the hum. The player listens for the plate that is "carrying the others" (loudest under the hum; Kaldren reads the stress in the frame and confirms), unbolts it and carries it into a null-point alcove, timed to the pulses. As the last plate goes quiet the hum stops and for one breath the whole Summit is silent; the dust spilling toward the player falls straight down. (6) **Evidence** (R07): Eyvind's **horn-trial record** (`TSH_trial_record`, quest item) dates every test; Iven's list is a dated Ridge Warden log; the dates match, and the player sets them together. Kaldren: "Stresses settled. That's all I can tell you." Eyvind, yielded, names his leader: **Ingrith Scarrow**, the Quartermaster, who "will close the wall herself if she can't sound it". (7) **Choice** (`TH_HORN`): **silenced** (plates left damped in the null alcoves; Kaldren keeps them), **broken** (cracked on the hall floor; nobody can rebuild the horn; Brynjar's clan loses its proudest boast), or **surrendered** (carried down to Ressa for the Assembly; used in *The Notes We Keep*). Unlocks Thornfell's high routes (agility-trigger routes; walkable fallbacks for click-to-move).

**TF-M5 Stonewake (`TSW`).** *Offer:* automatic after TF-M4 when the player next enters Windscar Pass or Stonewake: the early blizzard comes in and Ressa's Gather call sounds from every tower. **Situation:** Ingrith's last loyal crews have taken the **Gatefall** gatehouse and are dropping the galleries they re-propped this autumn to seal the far gate for the whole winter. **Hushfold's winter column** (Runa Weatherall's people, bringing the herds and the last wood down from the far shielings: the clanless do the far-side work) and **Vessa Galt's patrol** are still beyond it. **Beats:** (1) **Whiteout climb by horn alone:** above Three Calls the blizzard takes the screen to white. Sight is useless; Ressa's wardens are posted along the track and the player climbs toward their calls (Gather means come; Fall means stop and shelter; the echo count tells how close the face is). Fluted-face whistles still tell gusts. (2) **The deadfall galleries:** splinter crews signal each pull by horn using the old sealing calls. The player hears which call, reads the glyph on each gallery's marker, and knows which gallery is next; the grey-propped galleries are spent and cannot fall, the pale-propped ones are live. Reach the live gallery and drive out the wedge crew or cut the pull-line (fights yield). (3) **The gatehouse:** under soot and old plaster on the inner wall, the **closure roster**: dates, and beside each date the names of those outside when the slab came down, with shorter lines beside some dates: *"relief train turned back"*. Required inspect; the player takes a rubbing (`TSW_closure_rubbing`, quest item). Among the names: **Weatherall**. The turn-back lines fall in the hunger winter. (4) **Ingrith Scarrow** `[SPLINTER]` at the gate-slab winch with her last crew (human boss; yields at about 20%). She has counted everything and believes it; she is not a monster. (5) **Hold the gate:** the slab is half down. The player winds it up (channelled, three segments, progress kept) and holds the winch while the column comes through under a last loose deadfall. As Hushfold's people come in, **Runa reads from Hushfold's first tally book**, kept since the shelter line began, the way every host reads a tally on first snow: *"Weatherall, came over in the thaw"*, dated a thaw after a roster date (second custodian, required beat). Two stragglers can be found and brought in by answering the Lost call; with *The Third Horn* complete the player blows the restored reply themselves, otherwise Dorn does. Anyone the player does not reach, Vessa's patrol brings in at dawn (`TH_COLUMN`). (6) **Aftermath at Stonewake:** the Assembly meets the next day in the hall that carries some voices further than others. Hadrik chooses where people stand [CANON]; he asks the player where they will stand (`TH_STAND`). Who stands near the speaking stone is driven by earlier flags (§9.3). No evidence hearing: the rubbing is read aloud once, by Maera or Runa, and the Assembly decides its posture (`TH_ASSEMBLY_POSTURE`). Ingrith is held for a clan tribunal [CANON government form]. In every outcome the Assembly keeps a **closure account** from now on: a closing names who is outside (Thornfell's tool for the peace). Sets `TH_STANDING`.

### 9.2 Season presentation (per player)

Thornfell's arc runs from late autumn to snow. `TH_SEASON` is a **per-player presentation state** with two values, set by milestones, never world time: **autumn** until TF-M2 completes, then **snow**. Scope is deliberately small: a snowfall effect, snow cover on a short list of props, and the TF-M5 blizzard as a quest-local weather event. No NPC wardrobe changes. Co-op fallback: the host's state.

### 9.3 Assembly posture (TF-M5)

The posture is the Assembly's choice, read from who stands where. Hadrik places people; the player chooses only their own place.

1. Start at **watchful**.
2. **Toward open** (+1 each): `TH_STAND` = hushfold or cairn; `TH_HUSHFOLD_VOICE`; `TH_HIGHSTEADS_TOGETHER`; `TH_SHATTERFALL_ACCOUNT` = amended; `TH_BRYNJAR` = disowned; `TH_COLUMN` = player_brought; `TH_PASSWATCH_ROUTE` = sledge.
3. **Toward sealed** (+1 each): `TH_STAND` = brynjar; `TH_BRYNJAR` = defended; `TH_STORES` = leverage; `TH_BANNER` = blood-oath; `TH_SHATTERFALL_ACCOUNT` = kept.
4. Open minus sealed ≥ 3 → **open** (passes reopen at thaw by shared opening; closure accounts are public). ≤ −2 → **sealed_with_terms** (passes stay shut till thaw, with a closure account and a relief line to those outside). Otherwise **watchful** (closure accounts kept by the Wardens; readiness kept under Assembly account).
5. Every outcome adopts the closure account. None is wrong. `TH_HORN` and `TH_STORES` = reported/seized change Assembly lines only. Exact weights are tunable by the Gameplay Designer.

## 11. Authoring conventions (binding for every Thornfell package)

1. **Format.** Quest packages follow the Lowmark act-package format (`Caelmor_Lowmark_Main_Act_I.md`): the quest in one paragraph, selection record, canon grounding, summary card **with the §7.1 contribution-contract fields** (Anchor, World-truth row, Revelation role, Recurring evidence, State compliance), places, player-facing beats with objective text, encounters, discovery presentation spec, steps table, flags table, choices and consequences, rewards, failure/recovery/co-op, integration needs, mystery-ledger entries, open decisions, internal review log. Dialogue follows the Lowmark dialogue-file node format and §5.3 voice rules.
2. **Ids.** Content ids `thornfell.main.NN_slug` and `thornfell.side.slug` (suggested; the Schema & Content Engineer owns naming). Steps `S00_…`. Quest-local flags `<CODE>_…` with three-letter T codes; region flags `TH_…` from §9.1 only. Dialogue nodes `<CODE>-D<n>.<nn>`; barks `<CODE>-B<n>.<nn>`; encounters `<CODE>-E<n>`.
3. **Evidence rule.** Phenomena never identify a person, prove guilt, settle a claim or name the dead. Combat never reveals evidence. Records, objects, places and testimony do; every revelation has two custodians (§8).
4. **Three states only.** Inactive, Active, Completed. No fail or abandon logic, no re-instancing. A player who walks away resumes at the persisted step through an authored resume hook (default: the original offer). Choices are outcome flags. Every required proof has a guaranteed path.
5. **Rewards.** Access, knowledge, tools, small payments, standing. No lump quest XP. No new items beyond the four quest items unless the item pass defines them; reward tools are named functionally ("warden's rope", "bowyer's first stave").
6. **Tone.** Grounded, mythic, dry humour, understatement. At least one moment of wonder or unease per quest. No exposition dumps. No cutscenes; the camera stays with the player.
7. **Magic stays dormant.** No Thornfell NPC casts or recognises magic; every phenomenon is explainable as terrain or rare resonance; no player spellcasting.
8. **Splinter tagging.** Any node naming Ingrith, Bersi or Eyvind is tagged `[SPLINTER]` (§3.1).
9. **Map.** Relative placement only; mark **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Never state how Lowmark grain reached Thornfell.
10. **Status labels.** [CANON], [APPROVED — id], [NEW], [PROPOSAL — DIRECTOR].
11. **Scope.** No schema, JSON or C#. Technical needs go to the package's "Integration needs" and roll up into the integration handoff.
12. **Lowmark independence.** No Thornfell content requires Lowmark completion. Lowmark callbacks are optional variant lines read from Lowmark quest state or `LM_*` flags.
