**Status:** Proposal package for Creative Director milestone review (2026-11-02). Part of the package indexed in `Caelmor_Narrative_Architecture.md`. This file covers **deliverable 5**: how each important discovery is earned. It extends the method of `00_ADMIN/Reference/Caelmor_Part5_Long_Form_Mystery_and_Reveal_Ledger.md` (author truth, public belief, evidence, wrong reading, withheld truth, owner, payoff) from Lowmark to the whole journey. Thread numbers T1–T6 refer to that ledger. FT-nn ids refer to the foundational truths in `Caelmor_Narrative_Architecture_Factions_and_World_Truth.md`.

## 1. How revelations are earned

Work backward from each revelation to its evidence, then forward to the player's discovery paths.

1. **Owner.** One arc reveals it. Others may plant, never reveal.
2. **Two custodians.** At least two differently situated human or material sources. No manifestation, dream, Stonebound response, or confession alone.
3. **Seeds before payoff.** At least two seeds the player can encounter before the reveal, at least one of them in a different quest from the reveal.
4. **A plausible wrong reading.** The player should be able to believe something reasonable and wrong first. The ledger names it so writers can support it honestly.
5. **A guaranteed path.** Every revelation owned by a milestone has one required source inside that milestone (three-state rule: nothing missable on the main path). Revelations owned by an optional series (R12 in part, R14) are exempt by design and are never required by any milestone. Optional sources add weight.
6. **Recontextualization without erasure.** A later truth may change what an earlier act meant, never whether it mattered.
