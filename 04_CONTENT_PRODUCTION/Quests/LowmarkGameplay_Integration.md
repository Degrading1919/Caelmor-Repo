# Lowmark gameplay projection

Schema & Content Engineer contract, 2026-11-01. LowmarkGameplay.json conforms to DESIGNS/Templates & Schemas/GameplayContent.schema.json. This additive executable projection does not replace ResourceItem, Recipe, PlayerSave, network schemas, or promote new narrative to canon. Source dialogue and quest mechanics come from the adjacent True Line package and its cast/dialogue companion. Relative positions are greybox placement pending the POI map. Opus's authored documents remain the narrative authority; further content can use the generic contract.

Actions are target-bound and host range/visibility checked. Quest stages are zero based; maximum -1 is unbounded; an empty quest is independent of quest stages. Conditions combine all, none, and a minimum count of distinct any-flags. Once is a persistent receipt flag; empty allows repeating. Positive inputs/outputs form one atomic inventory transaction; removeItems transfers all held copies of optional evidence. Carry objects are transient, never inventory. RequiresCarry '-' requires empty hands; carry '-' releases the load. Repeatable pickups survive interrupted carries until delivery. Channels use authoritative 100 ms ticks and revalidate at completion. A persistent enemy kill flag never grants repeated corpse harvests.

Contract additions confirmed with Systems Engineer: each action has conditionalOutputs, an array of positive item amounts with all/none flag conditions evaluated against the action's pre-effect state. These join the same atomic transaction as ordinary inputs and outputs. This makes the dispatch order transfer automatic in D7 when the player learned about it; the retained order_transfer interaction only reads it and cannot grant another copy. Each target has an explicit nodeInstanceId: zero for non-gather targets and stable positive identities for gathering placements (1001–1009 in this pack). Identities are authored here rather than derived at runtime. Quest all-flags gate availability and lifecycle activation. Hazards are generic target-bound, once-only telegraphs with windup ticks, damage, radius and collision-validated push distance; their once receipts belong to saved gameplay state.

Gameplay Designer calibration: health 100; melee 6 damage per 20 ticks; boar 150 HP gives 50 seconds of uninterrupted attacks. Authored windups are 0.8/1.0/1.2/1.5 seconds. These are reversible technical calibration, not a locked balance pass. Woodcutting 22 XP per 4 seconds and Hunting/Cooking 28 XP per 5 seconds approach the approved level-30 target around 11 hours of engaged actions. All 99 thresholds copy the approved XP CSV. No quest XP. Currency is labelled Payment (8 provisional units); no currency name is invented. Cooking uses game meat and coppice timber as fuel; strips restore 25 HP. Ordinary coppice, hunting, cooking and food provide a repeatable loop beyond the quest. All actions require only entry level.

The Bank Walk uses the same existing cutting tool, Woodcutting action XP and ordinary wood state. The jam limb yields one wood; authorized intact leaner/holder removals yield two wood and one tannin-rich bark, preserving the authored bark co-output rule. Building the landing consumes two ordinary wood; the fallen leaner's remaining limbs provide a one-time recovery supply. These amounts and the pooled payment of four units were ratified by the Technical Director acting as Gameplay Designer for this provisional playable calibration; they are not canon-locked balance. The five River Skitters use 12 HP, one damage, 0.8-second windup and three-second recovery, targeting roughly 20 seconds of uninterrupted entry-level melee. Measurement channels take two seconds; setup notches take three seconds with no XP; productive cuts take four seconds and award 22 Woodcutting XP. Jam and leaner hazards use the authored 1.5/2-second telegraphs; four damage and one metre of validated push are bounded technical calibration. Death recovery remains at the crew fire (-3/-3); the Bank Walk's separate bank-top recovery location awaits zone composition.

Runtime ownership: prepare a private draft during Simulation Execution, commit before host snapshots in finalization, discard on failure. Reuse the existing inventory store and coarse QuestRuntimeSystem lifecycle. No I/O in simulation. Explicit local gameplay checkpoints carry content identity and version and validate before restore; old persistence contracts remain intact.

Presentation dependencies: greybox actors/creatures and text dialogue need production assets, animation and audio. The stone event keeps camera control, suppresses HUD during its quiet hold, and never replays after restore. No far-bank destination is invented: crossing inspection reports open after completion while traversal awaits the POI connection. This single-host adapter does not establish multiplayer readiness.

## The Flood Kitchen contract and content — 2026-11-02

Source: `Caelmor_Lowmark_Side_Quests_Work_and_Skills.md`, FK.1–FK.12, with the same cast/voice authority as The True Line. Content ID is `lowmark.side.flood_kitchen`, quest index 2, gated by `quest_done`. The shelter enters the technical hub east of Whisperflow's fixture through an expanded floor; positions x18–36.2 are test-space coordinates, not a geographical assertion that Brookhollow is next to the other projected locations. New content is appended so the original array indexes remain stable.

Confirmed schema additions are additive to this executable projection:

- Every item has `tags`. Both dried game strips and fowl stew carry `cooked_food`. An action's `tagInputs` consumes the declared number of eligible items in deterministic ordinal-key order, after accounting for its fixed inputs, in the same atomic transaction as all other deltas. No quest-specific food ID is required for feeding or the own-food choice.
- Every action has `counterEffects` entries with an id and positive amount; every stage has `counterRequirements` entries with an id and positive minimum. Counter identities derive from the union of these declarations, with every required identity needing a producing effect. All flag/any and counter requirements must be satisfied together. Counters record completed production, not current inventory. Counter increments belong to the same private action transaction as item changes, XP and receipts; rejected, interrupted or overflowing actions must not increment them or consume ingredients. Their values persist in the additive gameplay checkpoint. Existing content has empty arrays and retains its original behavior.
- Every action has `flock` and `flockOperation` (empty, prepare, resolve or recover). The shared flock definition supplies a sentinel target, alternate open target, cover target, recovery target, closed target, all/none availability conditions, radii and warning ticks. Only the sentinel moves between the authored open points; the downed recovery target stays in the shallows. Preparation in cover and a walking approach preserve calm. Rushing or unprepared close approach starts a 15-tick warning and clears preparation. Stopping does not erase a warning; withdrawal beyond the alert radius cancels it. A second honk immediately switches to the other open point, with no idle respawn delay. Resolve requires preparation and open ground and creates only one pending recovery. Food and Hunting XP are awarded only by a completed recovery; duplicate resolution/recovery cannot create extra output. Reading the traces and sentinel remains helpful rather than a required progression gate.
- Enemy `boundaryTarget` and `boundaryRadius` limit both aggression and attack completion. Closed-ground geese stop when the player leaves the nesting section; the leech is limited to deeper recovery water. They are combat encounters, separate from the non-combat flock, and provide no Hunting recovery or fowl.
- `respawnRegions` use a target as the region centre, radius and all/none conditions for eligibility, and x/z as a trusted recovery destination. The shelter region selects 22/-6 after entry; the host still validates that destination. The default crew-fire recovery remains for other areas.
- `presentationEvents` supplies target, trigger flag, kind, duration, rise and persistent-rise policy. The True Line's transient stone event and the kitchen's persistent water step use the same generic event contract. The kitchen hush lasts 80 ticks and leaves the step 0.3m higher. Restoring state must preserve the higher step without replaying the event or hiding UI again. Camera control remains with the player. Production geese/room audio is not supplied by this data projection.

Gameplay Designer calibration ratified by the Technical Director: fuel is two ordinary timber or two dry-slat carries, followed by a three-second fire-lighting channel. Two recovered fowl make one four-portion quest batch using Nan's existing stores, with five-second Cooking and 28 action XP. The public recipe becomes available in S03 after the hearth is lit and the two initial recoveries are recorded, and remains available through feeding, choice and quest completion: one raw fowl plus one ordinary timber yields two fowl-stew portions. This prevents eating/donating quest food from soft-locking the four households or the own-food choice. Either recipe triggers the hush receipt on the first S03-or-later cook. The cooking objective requires four produced shelter portions through `fk_portions_cooked`: the Nan-stores batch adds four, the public recipe adds two. One batch or two public cooks therefore advances S03; no fixed-batch receipt is mandatory. The repeatable flock supply permits further cooking and replacement food, while feeding and the own-food choice accept any tagged cooked food. Recovery takes five seconds for one raw fowl and 28 Hunting XP. Fowl is food-only; no feather, hide or fat state is introduced. Fowl stew restores 20 health. Closing dialogue gives one bowl and four provisional Payment units once. No lump quest XP is given. Eating the bowl is prompted, not a completion condition.

Replay IDs: enter `FK_enter` at `fk_shelter`, then `FK_start`. Choose `FK_wood` or `FK_lift1` → `FK_deposit1` → `FK_lift2` → `FK_deposit2`, then `FK_light_fire`. For each bird use `FK_prepare` at `fk_cover`, walk to the current sentinel, `FK_resolve`, then `FK_recover1` or `FK_recover2` at `fk_recovery`. Subsequent cycles use `FK_recover_repeat`. Cook `FK_cook_batch`, then feed four distinct households with `FK_feed1`–`FK_feed4`. Choose `FK_crew`, `FK_family`, or `FK_own` (the last requires one tagged cooked food). Close with the matching `FK_finish_crew`, `FK_finish_family` or `FK_finish_own`; all share the persistent `FK_finish` receipt. `FK_tune` records the optional tune exchange. `FK_cook_repeat` and the repeatable flock loop remain useful beyond completion.
