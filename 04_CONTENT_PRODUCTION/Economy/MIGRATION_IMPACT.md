# Economy migration impact

## Authority and boundary

The economy is unfrozen for **data design**. The Stage 3/4 material in `03_CORE_SYSTEMS/Skills & XP v1/` and `03_CORE_SYSTEMS/Crafting & Economy v1/` is migration input, while `LORE/Regions/`, `LORE/Timeline/`, and current Caelmor files constrain world and material identity. The old freeze declarations in `00_ADMIN/Milestones/Stage 4.3 — Economy Snapshot & Implementation Plan.md` and `Stage 4.4 — Economy Snapshot & Implementation Complete.md` explain the implemented loop; they do not prohibit this analytical rebuild. This migration does not alter that loop or any runtime C# in `DESIGNS/Networking & Persistence/`.

The canonical **analytical** artifact is `04_CONTENT_PRODUCTION/Economy/caelmor_economy.sqlite`, rebuilt by `CODE/Scripts/caelmor_economy_builder.py` from schema-validated batches in `04_CONTENT_PRODUCTION/Economy/interchange/` and `balance_config.json`. Workers author interchange JSON under `CODE/Scripts/caelmor_worker_interchange.schema.json`; they do not write the database. The SQLite model and query entry points are documented in `DATA_MODEL.md`, with current coverage and findings in `ECONOMY_AUDIT.md`.

## What migrated—and what did not

|---|---|---|
| Stage 3.1's six gathering and eight crafting non-combat skills (`03_CORE_SYSTEMS/Skills & XP v1/Stage 3.1 — Non-Combat Skills Overview.md`) | All 14 keys are legal in the interchange schema and represented in `canon_skill_scope`/`skill_metrics`; the original seven-skill build is marked `primary`, other skills `extension`. | Absence of action/recipe coverage would be visible as a warning, not silently excluded. The current six batches cover all 14 skills across six bands. |

Station tags are analytical, not yet guaranteed runtime station keys. |

Several sample records were deliberately **not** migrated verbatim: `stabilizing_salts` points to missing `salt_flat_node`; `forge_iron_fasteners` consumes its own output; `dry_meat_strips` has no meat input and lists a processed food as if directly sourced from `game_carcass_node`; and `cut_timbers` similarly conflates gathering origin with recipe production. These are reference/provenance or transformation defects, not reasons to invent replacement nodes, skills, or filler resources. The sample `felled_tree_node`, `quarry_face_node`, `fiber_reed_patch_node`, `resin_bearing_tree_node`, and other generic concepts remain historical examples rather than locked content entries.

## New relationship semantics

`gathering_nodes.action_key` lets multiple regional placements share one mechanically defined `gathering_action`. `gathering_action_outputs` represents multiple guaranteed, weighted, or conditional item outputs; authored probability is only used where justified. `recipe_inputs` and `recipe_outputs` are quantity-bearing transformations. An item can therefore have many distinct gathering, recipe, or explicit external sources and many recipe-consumption or explicit consumption sinks.

The migration introduces a crucial distinction absent from the old resource-item flags: **use is not a sink**. Only `external_sinks` marked `consumption` and recipe inputs populate `item_sinks`; `equipment_use`, `tool_use`, `trade`, and `system_use` are authored separately in `external_uses` and populate `item_uses`. Equipping a pickaxe, using a bow, or trading a reusable part creates demand but does not remove the item. `item_metrics.sink_count` and `use_count` must not be interpreted interchangeably. For finished gear, zero consuming sinks can be intentional; for food, ammunition, or one-use repair kits, consumption must be explicit. `v_item_origins`, `v_item_transformations`, `v_item_connections`, `v_item_health`, `v_level_unlocks`, and `v_activity_rates` expose the resulting graph.

## Canon and provenance exclusions

The regional branches use public Lowmark river/reed/iron and dike contexts, Thornfell ridge/wood/wool contexts, Mire war-damaged landscape, and Emberholt emberstone/Ashsteel and industrial conduit contexts consistent with `LORE/Regions/Caelmor_Region_Merged.md` and its individual region files. Shared biological items carry ordinary yield while fauna/world labels live on nodes. A regional or activity name in an interchange batch is an economy-design placement extrapolation, not automatic promotion to narrative canon.

The batches explicitly exclude hidden Riverwarden Oasis and quest-gated fish, Glassed Hollow or hidden ritual access, supernatural relics/free-energy salvage, and quest turn-ins. The Scavenging/Mechanisms/Fabrication branch in `interchange/scavenging_mechanisms_fabrication.json` uses mundane recovered ironwork/conduit and repeatable upkeep kits. No quest is invented to justify a sink. Lore-derived materials such as Stormwood, Emberstone, Ember Shards, and Ashsteel are preserved as connected chains rather than multiplied into decorative variants.

## Balance, RNG, and runtime adapter implications

`balance_config.json` is labeled `PROVISIONAL_ANALYTICAL_BASELINE_NOT_RUNTIME_CANON`. `CODE/Scripts/caelmor_progression_calculator.py` deterministically derives the XP curve, band XP/hour targets, action XP, attempts/hour, and expected yields rather than requiring workers to hand-author bulk rates. Current authored gathering outputs are guaranteed; rarity labels describe economy role, **not** a drop chance. The configured weighted bonus chance is zero. Any future weighted pool needs an explicit rationale and calculable weights; a conditional output without a resolved condition must be surfaced as unresolved rather than assigned an invented probability. Exact level/band placement and rates remain design assumptions pending balance playtest, not lore facts or engine timing commitments.

The current C# contract has `ResourceNodePlacement.ResourceNodeKey`, a `(nodeTypeKey, skillKey)` grant mapping in `DESIGNS/Networking & Persistence/TableResourceGrantMapping.cs`, `GatheringResolutionResult.GatheringSkillKey`, and keyed crafting requests/deltas in `CraftingExecutionTypes.cs`. It does **not** directly consume the new node → shared action → output graph, analytical level/region gates, item-use relations, or generated rate tables. A later, separately authorized adapter would need to (1) map stable analytical item/node/action/recipe keys to runtime assets and placements, (2) flatten or look up node/action output grants for the existing grant contract, (3) reconcile exact unlock/tool/station/region rules with engine legality checks, (4) decide whether consuming-use events require runtime handlers, and (5) preserve atomic grant/craft/persistence semantics described in the Stage 4 implementation references. This report does not authorize replacing the existing runtime architecture or changing persisted keys.

The migration is thus complete as an analytical-data separation, **not** a runtime cutover. Consult `ECONOMY_AUDIT.md` for live coverage and quality findings before any adapter design. All 14 skills are covered and the current graph has no validation findings; provisional balance and the runtime adapter remain separate future decisions, not silently accepted canon.
