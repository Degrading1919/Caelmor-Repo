## Adversarial pruning record

The synthesis deliberately pruned or merged:

- eleven Hunting proof compositions into seven production-facing patterns;
- “closure challenged,” “false premise,” and compound passage examples into variants of HN-07 rather than separate nodes;
- danger as a modifier of existing hunts rather than a mastery-only predator source;
- ten woodland proof compositions into seven production-facing patterns;
- flood, wind, fire, rot, and obstruction as WN-04 authored conditions rather than five node families;
- public-repair and constrained-extraction variants into reusable WN-05–WN-07 relationships;
- destination-only ordinary-wood custody in favor of one tangible broad state;
- contextual specialist buying as the sole sale route in favor of D07’s baseline lawful market plus bounded contextual demand;
- species tiers, wood grades, superior hides, bark groves, carcasses, fat items, offcuts, fuelwood, repair stock, commission tokens, and generic component inventories;
- daily boards, rotating chores, live population simulation, dynamic market simulation, and management interfaces.
