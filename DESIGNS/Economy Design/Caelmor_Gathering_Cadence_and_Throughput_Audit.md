**Status:** Proposal-stage integrated audit of Hunting HM1–HM8 and Woodcutting WM1–WM8
**Scope:** Rhythm, attention, sustainable participation, output-event pressure, session behavior, dominance, and readiness for quantity/economic-flow design

## 1. Audit authority

This audit preserves:

- the approved HM1–HM8 and WM1–WM8 identities;
- fixed authored-action XP and the approved loop centers;
- the 1–99 XP thresholds and closed milestone-time envelopes;
- the sustainable knowledgeable progression mixes;
- Practical Mastery at level 80 and commemorative completion at 99;
- the approved unlock maps, including the intentional level 81–98 authored quiet span;
- no player-level XP multiplier, circuit bonus, new resource, or new skill.

Output rates count relationships rather than final item quantities. Exact item yield, price, processing ratios, market capacity, animation time, source respawn, and map distance remain later work.

## 11. Creative Director decisions

No Creative Director decision is required before the next economy pass. The proposal preserves the approved methods and calibration, and its remaining uncertainties are map verification, prototype feel, and concrete downstream quantity/economic-flow tuning.

The Creative Director should review two high-impact findings during audit:

1. HM8 can support roughly 31 sustainable weighted edible events per engaged hour before final yield tuning.
2. WM8 can support roughly 46 sustainable weighted ordinary-wood events per engaged hour, rising to about 55 during uninterrupted knowledgeable circuit play.

These are review items rather than requests to change method identity. The approved remedies are yield, source density, route composition, processing ratio, legitimate demand, and market tuning.
