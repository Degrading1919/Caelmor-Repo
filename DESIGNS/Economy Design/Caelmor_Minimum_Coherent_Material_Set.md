# Caelmor — Minimum Coherent Material Set

**Status:** proposal-stage material foundation approved in direction by the Creative Director. Material names remain provisional where later terminology work may improve them. This document selects material families and their physical purposes; it does not define inventory IDs, exact recipes, quantities, statistics, prices, XP, action times, gathering nodes, schemas, or canon amendments.

## Authority and constraints

This proposal works from the approved equipment component functions, aspirational equipment foundation, major equipment milestones, Lore Foundation Lock, and regional source/process proposals.

- Lowmark is the only full v1 region. Every required material is available through Lowmark production or Lowmark-accessible trade without fixing a route.
- Higher equipment milestones improve processing, construction, fit, integration, access, inspection, repairability, and provenance rather than replacing one material with a superior named tier.
- Stonebound and Mire manifestations are not material sources. Volkhari people, bodies, and inherited knowledge are not a supply chain.
- Great War salvage is finite, politically charged, and evidentiary. It is unnecessary to this foundation.
- Ritual or memorial importance does not grant magical material properties.
- No aspirational commission requires a unique capstone material.
- S01 remains deferred; no ranged requirement is inferred.
- Smithing and Leatherworking remain the principal v1 equipment crafts. Other labor or gathering relationships appear only where they have a physical or economic purpose.

## 1. Creative Director brainstorm options and ruling

The brainstorm compared a six-material ordinary baseline against additions for dedicated padding, rigid nonmetal structure, metal tiers, extra joining agents, and prestigious capstone substances.

| Option | Disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Six ordinary material families | **SELECTED** | Covers every approved function through three equipment materials and three indispensable process or maintenance supports. |
| Add wool felt | **RETAIN OPTIONAL** | Useful if a later form proves layered flax cannot provide sufficient damping, lining, or weather behavior. It is not presently indispensable. |
| Add seasoned hardwood | **RETAIN OPTIONAL** | Useful if an approved weapon or guard form requires a rigid nonmetal structure. Current form neutrality does not justify making it mandatory. |
| Split iron and steel into progression tiers | **REJECT** | Encourages direct material replacement and duplicates the role of one conventional steel family whose performance can deepen through processing. |
| Add wax, resin, or glue | **REJECT PENDING NEED** | Current mechanical joining, flax thread, leather construction, and tallow finishing leave no demonstrated missing function. |
| Add fantasy, salvage, or capstone materials | **REJECT** | No approved component needs them, and their prestige would substitute rarity for workmanship and earned commission legitimacy. |

**Creative Director ruling:** proceed with plain carbon steel stock, vegetable-tanned hide leather, flax textile, tannin-rich bark, wood charcoal, and rendered tallow. Treat them as the minimum coherent material foundation rather than automatically creating six literal inventory item IDs.

## Material identity versus process state

A material family may pass through physically necessary states without each state becoming a new material identity or progression tier.

- Hide may move from raw to prepared to vegetable-tanned and finished states.
- Flax may move from source plant to prepared fiber or tow, thread, and linen textile.
- Imported steel stock may be sorted, cleaned, formed, heat treated, fitted, and repaired.
- Bark may be prepared into a tanning medium; wood may be converted into charcoal; animal fat may be rendered into usable tallow.

The next processing and recipe pass will determine which states must be represented for clear production, trade, or repair. It must not create extra items merely to display every physical step.

## 2. Selected material portfolio

### 2.1 Plain carbon steel stock

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Plain carbon steel stock** — provisional; “plain” distinguishes conventional material from fantasy or legacy metals, not a low-quality tier. |
| Classification | Ordinary, imported conventional metal. |
| Physical functions served | Rigid structural support; edge-bearing work; impact-receiving work; local junction reinforcement; some secure control fittings; some sacrificial wear service; mechanical joining; repair compatibility. |
| Equipment/component uses | Blade structures and edge-bearing surfaces; Guard structures and impact faces; Harness protective structures and facings; justified hard closures, junctions, or wear fittings. |
| Source or import relationship | Lowmark lacks a complete approved metal source. Sound stock can reach its markets from conventional Thornfell or Emberholt production and other ordinary trade, without naming an exclusive supplier or route. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Imported worked stock is primary. Sound civilian scrap may be assessed and reused; war remains and sealed-site salvage are excluded from routine supply. |
| Broad processing path | Receive and inspect, sort by fitness, clean, heat, form, join, establish working geometry, heat treat where appropriate, finish, fit, inspect, and later refurbish. |
| Primary associated core skills | Smithing. Mining is an upstream regional relationship, not a mandatory Lowmark player dependency for using imported stock. |
| Repair and reuse role | Can be sharpened, dressed, straightened within safe limits, reforged or sectionally replaced where construction permits, and retired when hidden damage or distortion makes reuse unsafe. |
| Scarcity reason | Imported supply, transport interruption, forge fuel, skilled judgment, repair queues, and rejection of unsafe or undocumented stock. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational. Progression comes from controlled forming, heat treatment, geometry, matching, integration, inspection, and refurbishment standards rather than new metal tiers. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | It is the only selected material capable of sustaining the hard structural, edge-bearing, and direct-impact requirements. Removing it collapses three paths and hard fittings in the fourth. |

### 2.2 Vegetable-tanned hide leather

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Vegetable-tanned hide leather** — provisional at the catalog level; exact animal source and tanning plant remain unselected. |
| Classification | Ordinary biological material. |
| Physical functions served | Flexible structural support; flexible protective facing; local junction reinforcement; secure control and fit; fastening and articulation; impact damping; sacrificial wear service; joining support; repair compatibility. |
| Equipment/component uses | Riverpath Leathers primary structure and protective face; Harness fastenings and flexible interfaces; Guard retention, control, or damping where required; Blade control wraps or wear parts only where the eventual form justifies them. |
| Source or import relationship | Lowmark livestock and hunting byproducts provide a local basis, with ordinary outside hides available through trade when local supply is insufficient. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Hides enter the chain through husbandry, food processing, or justified hunting rather than animals killed solely to populate an equipment tier. |
| Broad processing path | Prompt cleaning and preservation, preparation, tannin treatment, controlled drying, softening, grading by physical use, cutting, joining, fitting, conditioning, and repair. |
| Primary associated core skills | Leatherworking. Hunting participates only when it is the actual hide source; livestock and market supply do not force a Hunting requirement. |
| Repair and reuse role | Can be cleaned, conditioned, patched, reinforced, refitted, and replaced in bounded sections. Overstretched, rotten, contaminated, or deeply torn material is retired. |
| Scarcity reason | Seasonal animal supply, spoilage before preparation, water and waste limits, tanning time, skilled finishing, and competition with ordinary household and work uses. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational. Later value comes from selection for purpose, controlled tanning, orientation, patterning, matched stretch, careful fitting, and maintainable sectioning. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | Neither steel nor textile can equally provide durable flexible structure, abrasion resistance, shaped control, fastening, and repairable protective facing. |

### 2.3 Flax textile — linen and tow

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Flax textile — linen and tow** — provisional. Linen, thread, and tow are useful processed expressions of one flax foundation rather than automatic separate material tiers. |
| Classification | Ordinary plant-fiber material. |
| Physical functions served | Flexible structural support in bounded roles; secure control and fit; flexible fastening; layered impact damping; body-contact lining; sacrificial wear service; stitched joining; repair compatibility. |
| Equipment/component uses | Linings, stitching, wraps, ties, repair patches, layered or quilted padding, and body-contact interfaces across the four paths where physically required. |
| Source or import relationship | Lowmark's approved farm-fiber foundation supports flax on suitable drier plots. Imported fiber or cloth can cover crop loss or specialized demand without becoming a mandatory source. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Cultivated and harvested through ordinary farm and household labor, then transferred to household or specialist processors. |
| Broad processing path | Harvest and dry, separate and prepare fiber, retain usable tow, spin thread, weave linen where needed, layer or quilt for padding, cut, stitch, wash, dry, patch, and reuse sound sections. |
| Primary associated core skills | Leatherworking integrates textile parts into protective equipment. Fiber preparation, spinning, and weaving may remain ordinary household or specialist labor rather than a new required skill. |
| Repair and reuse role | Thread renews seams; linen patches and replaces linings; tow or layered cloth renews padding. Clean sound remnants can serve bounded repairs rather than generating a universal scrap loop. |
| Scarcity reason | Harvest timing, retting or equivalent preparation burden, clean water, drying space, extensive labor, fire and pest risk, and competition with household cloth. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational. Progression comes from fiber preparation, even thread, weave suitability, layering, patterning, seam placement, matched compression, fit, and clean repair. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | Leather cannot equally provide breathable body-contact cloth, fine stitching, or layered tow padding, while steel has no flexible lining role. Removing flax forces several unrelated functions into worse substitutes. |

### 2.4 Tannin-rich bark

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Tannin-rich bark** — provisional functional name. Exact tree species and whether more than one source is accepted remain later ecological selections. |
| Classification | Ordinary plant-derived process material. |
| Physical functions served | Joining and finishing support through transformation of prepared hide into stable leather; workshop process support; repair compatibility when retanning or surface restoration is physically appropriate. |
| Equipment/component uses | Indirect but indispensable input to leather used by the Riverpath Leathers and any justified leather component in the other three paths. It is not an equipment component itself. |
| Source or import relationship | Managed Lowmark woodland can supply bounded bark streams from responsible cutting and other stewardship-compatible work; trade may supplement shortages. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Collected as part of approved woodland work rather than unrestricted stripping of living trees or vulnerable banks. |
| Broad processing path | Sort and dry where appropriate, break or prepare, extract a tanning medium with ordinary water, use it under controlled tanning practice, and dispose of spent matter away from shared water. |
| Primary associated core skills | Leatherworking consumes the prepared tanning medium. Any later Woodcutting relationship must follow stewardship and cannot exist merely to force a skill dependency. |
| Repair and reuse role | Supports fresh leather production and bounded restoration. Spent bark is not assumed to remain an equipment input or magical residue. |
| Scarcity reason | Woodland protection, season and source condition, competing fuel or civic uses, extraction time, transport bulk, and water-safe processing. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational as a stable process foundation. Later progression comes from control of preparation and tanning outcome, not superior named bark tiers. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | The selected leather requires a grounded tanning agent. Without it, leather appears as a finished resource with no physical transformation, labor, waste, or stewardship cost. |

### 2.5 Wood charcoal

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Wood charcoal** — provisional only if later terminology distinguishes workshop supply from household fuel. No tree species or quality ladder is selected. |
| Classification | Ordinary processed fuel. |
| Physical functions served | Workshop heat and process support, especially controlled heating for Smithing; limited finishing heat where physically justified. |
| Equipment/component uses | Consumed by the workshop processes that form, join, heat treat, repair, and refurbish steel components. It is not part of the finished equipment. |
| Source or import relationship | Lowmark managed woodland residues and appropriate offcuts can provide a bounded local basis; conventional fuel trade can supplement it. Structural stock is not casually burned during shortage. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Produced from stewardship-approved wood streams and ordinary fuel work, with fire, smoke, yield, and competing-use constraints. |
| Broad processing path | Select suitable feedstock, dry, convert under controlled low-oxygen heating, cool safely, sort for workshop use, store dry, and account for ash and fire risk. |
| Primary associated core skills | Smithing consumes the fuel. A later Woodcutting relationship is justified only if gathering and fuel preparation are actually represented. |
| Repair and reuse role | Enables reheating and refurbishment of steel, making repair a real economic sink. Ash or fines receive no automatic equipment use. |
| Scarcity reason | Managed cutting limits, drying and conversion time, fire risk, competition with household and civic heat, storage loss, and imported supply interruption. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational. Advanced work demands better heat control and fuel planning, not a chain of increasingly prestigious charcoal materials. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | Lowmark's repair smithing explicitly depends on accounted conventional fuel. Removing charcoal makes steel production and refurbishment consume unexplained heat. |

### 2.6 Rendered tallow

| Field | Selection |
|---|---|
| Working name | **Rendered tallow** — provisional where exact animal source is unnecessary. |
| Classification | Ordinary animal byproduct and maintenance material. |
| Physical functions served | Joining and finishing support in bounded applications; protective surface care; thread dressing; leather conditioning; repair compatibility; modest process support where lubrication is required. |
| Equipment/component uses | Maintenance of leather and selected steel surfaces, preparation of some flexible interfaces, thread or seam work where justified, and continued service of finished equipment. It is not a power-bearing component. |
| Source or import relationship | Lowmark husbandry and ordinary animal processing support local production; markets may receive additional supply. No special beast source is required. |
| Broad acquisition basis | Recovered as a useful byproduct from animals already entering household and food economies, then transferred to renderers or workshops. |
| Broad processing path | Separate suitable fat, clean, render with controlled heat, strain, cool, store safely, apply sparingly, and renew during maintenance. Exact blends are outside this pass. |
| Primary associated core skills | Leatherworking is the main equipment user; Smithing may use it in bounded surface-care or workshop applications. Hunting is relevant only when it genuinely supplied the animal. |
| Repair and reuse role | Extends the service life of leather, thread, and selected steel surfaces. It is consumed through maintenance and cannot repair structural failure by itself. |
| Scarcity reason | Seasonal animal processing, food and household competition, spoilage or contamination, rendering labor, clean storage, and uneven market access. |
| Progression relevance | Early through aspirational as a maintenance foundation. Later value comes from correct preparation and use, not a sequence of superior fats. |
| Why it deserves separate existence | It supplies the cross-path conditioning and protective-maintenance role that steel, finished leather, linen, bark, and charcoal cannot perform. Without it, repairability lacks a routine finishing input. |

## 5. Progression role of each material

| Material | Early relevance | Mid relevance | Advanced relevance | Aspirational relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain carbon steel stock | Dependable basic structures, surfaces, and repairs. | Purpose-specific geometry and more deliberate hard junctions. | Controlled heat treatment, force paths, service access, and verified repair. | Matched stock behavior, close tolerances, individual fit, staged inspection, and accountable refurbishment. |
| Vegetable-tanned hide leather | Basic flexible protection, control, fastening, and patching. | Purpose-specific fit, reinforcement, and replaceable wear zones. | Controlled tanning, orientation, matched flexibility, integrated damping, and section repair. | Individually patterned and matched construction whose later repair preserves fit and movement. |
| Flax textile | Thread, ordinary lining, wrapping, and simple padding. | Better seam placement, layered tow, fit support, and planned patches. | Controlled weave or layer selection, zoned padding, clean integration, and replaceable linings. | Matched compression and movement behavior, precise joining, and repair that preserves the commissioned fit. |
| Tannin-rich bark | Establishes ordinary leather production and repair context. | More controlled tanning outcomes for intended component roles. | Reliable preparation and waste control support selected flexibility and durability. | Consistency and custody of process support matched leather without creating a prestigious bark tier. |
| Wood charcoal | Makes routine Lowmark steel working and repair physically possible. | Supports more controlled joining and heat cycles. | Heat management and scheduling become part of precision and safe refurbishment. | Accounted, controlled workshop heat supports close-tolerance work; charcoal itself does not become prestigious. |
| Rendered tallow | Routine conditioning, surface care, and thread preparation. | Maintenance becomes an expected equipment sink. | Correct application supports long service without hiding structural damage. | Ongoing care preserves fitted components and documented serviceability; no superior tallow tier is introduced. |

The same foundation remains relevant throughout progression. Early work proves basic function; later work extracts more dependable behavior through knowledge and construction.

## 6. Source, process, and lore grounding

### Lowmark production and access

- **Local foundations:** hides, flax, tannin-rich bark, some charcoal feedstock, and tallow follow Lowmark's husbandry, farm fiber, managed woodland, household labor, and repair culture.
- **Import foundation:** plain carbon steel stock follows Lowmark's approved dependence on conventional outside metal and its market and crossing relationships with Thornfell and Emberholt.
- **Processing foundation:** Lowmark repair smiths, Leatherworking labor, household textile work, curing and tanning specialists, fuel workers, carriers, and markets already have proposal-level social and physical reasons to exist.
- **Water boundary:** washing, tanning, cooling, and cleaning use ordinary water infrastructure under C09. No equipment process uses combat water magic or creates a player spell system.
- **Custody boundary:** reputable sourcing and workshop records can support trust, but they remain provenance and acquisition evidence rather than components or consumable tokens.

### Cross-region implications

- Thornfell and Emberholt may supply conventional metal stock without either becoming an exclusive source.
- Lowmark may supplement fiber, hides, tanning inputs, fuel, or finished intermediate work through trade during local shortage.
- The Mire is not required to supply any v1 equipment material and no Mire manifestation enters production.
- Exact routes, shipment order, source sites, trade volume, and monopolies remain open for later map and economy work.

### Special-material finding

No special or fantasy material is required. The aspirational identities remain grounded in civic legitimacy, demonstrated use, skilled commission work, fit, maintenance, and provenance.

### Portfolio-level audit

- **Material count:** six foundational material families.
- **Equipment materials:** steel, leather, and flax.
- **Process and maintenance supports:** bark, charcoal, and tallow.
- **Function coverage:** all fourteen approved functions have at least one primary source; construction-dependent functions have credible secondary coverage.
- **Progression:** no milestone requires a material swap. Later equipment demands more control over the same materials and their integration.
- **Skill scope:** Smithing and Leatherworking own finished-equipment responsibilities. Mining, Hunting, or Woodcutting become dependencies only where later acquisition design establishes a real source relationship.
- **Inventory scope:** six foundations do not mean six IDs. Necessary states may become recipe inputs or outputs; insignificant states remain process description.
- **Lore scope:** all sources follow approved ordinary production or trade. No supernatural, atrocity, memorial, or unresolved population claim supports supply.

## Final selection

### Exact minimum approved material set

1. Plain carbon steel stock.
2. Vegetable-tanned hide leather.
3. Flax textile — linen and tow.
4. Tannin-rich bark.
5. Wood charcoal.
6. Rendered tallow.

These are six material foundations, not a mandate for six inventory records. Legitimate physical states and outputs will be selected only when the processing or recipe model needs them.

### Unresolved material decisions

- Final player-facing terminology for the six provisional names.
- Exact animal source mix for hide and tallow.
- Exact flax cultivation and processing representation.
- Exact botanical source for tannin-rich bark.
- Exact steel stock forms received by Lowmark and which working states require representation.
- Which states are inventory items, workshop states, or descriptive process steps.
- Whether a later approved form demonstrates the need for optional wool felt or seasoned hardwood.
- Exact proportions of local production and imported supply, without fixing routes.

### Gaps

**None at the material-foundation level.** Every approved function and all four equipment paths have ordinary, Lowmark-accessible material support. The unresolved decisions belong to processing, recipe representation, equipment form, gathering, or map placement.
