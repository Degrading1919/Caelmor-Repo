# Caelmor — Hunting Cadence and Throughput

**Status:** Proposal-stage analytical balance following approval of HM1–HM8, their XP calibration, and their unlock map
**Scope:** Complete-loop rhythm, attention, sustainable availability, output-event pressure, and session behavior; not runtime timing or final item yield

## 1. Cadence policy

This pass keeps every approved fixed action XP value and every calibrated loop center. Ranges around those centers are tolerance bands for map and prototype work, not random timing. A knowledgeable player saves identifiable work: invalid reads, duplicated preparation, poor route order, unnecessary return travel, or damaged recovery. Knowledge never multiplies XP or makes the authored resolution instantaneous.

An **output event** remains a relationship, not an item quantity. One edible recovery event, hide-qualified recovery event, or renderer-eligible relationship may later produce a tuned inventory quantity. Source eligibility remains deterministic once the actual source and recovery conditions are known.

Meaningful interaction means a decision, confirmation, correction, or committed action. It does not mean every animation requires another click.

## 3. Sustainable availability

Sustainable share is a plausible share of **engaged time** in the relevant band when a player prefers that method. It is not a quota, cooldown, or closure roll. When conditions do not exist, the player selects another valid method rather than waiting.

| Method | Availability class | Sustainable engaged-time share when preferred | World basis |
|---|---|---:|---|
| HM1 | Durable / routinely repeatable | 80–100% | Common margin opportunities and short ordinary receiving routes |
| HM2 | Source-condition-limited but regionally durable | 60–80% | A current cultivated-edge pressure claim and eligible source must exist; stale claims are rejected |
| HM3 | Route-limited, routinely repeatable | 70–100% | A verified wet-margin circuit can support long sessions while local access remains open |
| HM4 | Route-limited and movement-window-limited | 65–85% | Fresh movement evidence and a valid interception opening are required |
| HM5 | Route-limited, durable high-skill supply | 50–75% | Traveling-grazer openings recur, but full recovery and receiving capacity constrain repetition |
| HM6 | Compound opportunity | 10–25% | An ordinary hunt and a separate supported hazard must coincide; decline remains valid |
| HM7 | Trust/access-limited and authored-condition-limited | 5–10% | Bounded inquiries require testimony, evidence, and a legitimate custodian; they are not generated filler |
| HM8 | Compound route opportunity | 65–75% post-80 | Three valid constituent legs and a credible shared return must coexist; the approved 80% XP share is about 73% of engaged time |

These shares preserve the approved sustainable knowledgeable path. They regulate nothing by fiat: ecology, evidence, access, route composition, and receiving capacity explain every limit.

Later tuning should first adjust item quantity per recovery event, qualifying source density, processing ratios, route composition, or legitimate consumption. It should not add filler outputs, junk recipes, rare trophies, or arbitrary scarcity.

## 9. Verification requirements

### Map verification required

- HM1 receiving proximity and whether its 26–30 second return is credible.
- HM3 wet-margin circuit length and closure readability.
- HM4 fresh movement windows and whether return staging reduces real travel.
- HM5 route, field-recovery space, and tannery/renderer/market alternatives.
- HM8 adjacency: three valid legs plus shared receiving must fit 295–320 baseline seconds without invented shortcuts.

### Playtesting required

- Whether interaction intervals feel deliberate rather than passive or busy.
- Whether field dressing is meaningful where condition matters and compressed elsewhere.
- Whether HM6 danger preserves clear Hunting ownership.
- Whether HM7 evidence work remains engaging across one to three inquiries.
- Whether 30–60 minute HM8 sessions are comfortable and materially sustainable.
- Whether destination visits create satisfying banking choices rather than repetitive friction.

No Creative Director decision is required to carry this cadence foundation into prototype and downstream quantity work. The high HM8 food pressure is a tuning question with an explicit control order, not a request for a new method or material.
