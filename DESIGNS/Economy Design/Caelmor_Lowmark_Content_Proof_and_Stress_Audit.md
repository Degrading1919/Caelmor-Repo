# Caelmor — Lowmark Content Proof and Stress Audit

**Status:** Final second-stage proposal audit after candidate generation, integration, adversarial review, and revision

## Audit question

This audit asks whether actual provisional Lowmark content proves the existing gathering and supply architecture, rather than merely filling its tables.

The proof set contains:

- **4 provisional Hunting fauna functions**;
- **11 Hunting opportunity compositions** across five progression bands;
- **4 provisional managed-woodland source relationships**;
- **10 Woodcutting opportunity compositions** across five progression bands;
- **9 ordinary-economy content situations**;
- **9 complete source-to-sink traces**;
- the existing six-material foundation, nine equipment-chain inventory states, four player-facing recipe families, and ordinary-labor boundaries.

All new fauna, source relationships, conditions, and examples remain **PROVISIONAL**. They do not amend canon or authorize implementation.

## 1. Portfolio sufficiency proof

### Hunting

| Function | Distinct content job | Output role | Why it survives pruning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small field-margin forager | Short concealment, readable evidence, dry recovery, guided margin work | Edible relationship without hide or renderer feed | Teaches that Hunting value is not synonymous with Leatherworking and supplies the lowest-burden introduction. |
| Field-edge rooter | Close-cover crop pressure, territorial reversal, disputed damage | Edible relationship, eligible hide, possible renderer flow | Owns the crop-evidence and household-conflict problem that the moving grazer cannot reproduce. |
| Reed-margin flocking game | Collective alert, wet-margin approach, changing presence | Edible relationship without hide or renderer feed | Proves food-only Hunting under group behavior and wet recovery rather than repeating the field forager. |
| Wet-meadow traveling grazer | Long alert distance, crossing inference, route and recovery planning | Edible relationship, eligible hide, possible renderer flow | Owns moving-range and full-output route planning without adding a superior hide. |

The portfolio uses one shared parameterized alert-and-movement framework for flock and grazer presentations. H5 hazards use existing terrain/access states and, only if D02 permits, normal combat handoff. H5 and H6 are compound wrappers rather than mastery-only quarry.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Removing any of the four functions collapses a distinct behavior/output lesson; adding a fifth currently duplicates an existing role or creates an unsupported output.

### Woodcutting

| Relationship | Distinct content job | Ordinary-wood role | Why it survives pruning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household managed tree | Preserve living household value through pollard, boundary, orchard, or productive-tree maintenance | Destination-bound household/general or accepted fuel flow | Owns the living-source lesson and agricultural integration. Productive trees remain a variant, not a fifth family. |
| Managed renewal coppice | Repeated selective harvest with protected regrowth | Dependable household/general, repair, or accepted fuel flow; strong baseline bark relation | Owns renewable selection and the clearest responsible bark supply. |
| Bank-binding riparian tree | Cut only where obstruction, failure, and bank function justify intervention | Destination-bound repair/general flow; bark conditional on final ecology | Owns Lowmark water-edge restraint and awkward recovery. |
| Public repair reserve standard | Retain sound growth for future public need, then match source to a bounded repair demand | Destination-bound public-repair flow; bark conditional on final ecology | Owns future-stock custody and mastery selection without a superior timber material. |

Ordinary wood is provisionally destination bound. A valid intake is disclosed before commitment and remains available through successful resolution. No log, grade, stockpile, or hidden claim item is added.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Four relationships prove every W pattern. The fifth productive-tree family was **CONTENT REDUNDANT** and was merged into household managed trees without losing its lesson.

## 2. Progression proof through concrete compositions

### Hunting progression

| Band | Concrete proof examples | What changes materially |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | Visible Margin; Damage with an Owner | Learns output eligibility, basic sign, household purpose, clean recovery, and the difference between food-only and hide-bearing game. |
| Competent | Wrong Culprit; Quiet Water, Many Eyes | Compares evidence, may refuse an invalid hunt, respects a wet-ground closure, and manages group alert rather than following a simple trail. |
| Advanced | Crossing Inferred; Flock Is Elsewhere; Useful Quarry, Unsafe Return | Infers movement across clues, redirects after apparent absence, plans return before contact, and judges whether existing terrain/access risk invalidates value. |
| Masterful | Two Signs, One Opening; Closure Challenged | Combines movement, recovery, destination, and lawful redirection. Closure Challenged teaches the evidence/refusal grammar later recombined at mastery. |
| Practical Mastery | Commission with a False Premise; One Passage, Several Claims | Diagnoses mistaken testimony, chooses the smallest responsible response, and combines familiar behavior, access, custody, and economic need without new quarry or output. |

M2 and P1 are a teaching chain, not two required nodes. P2 is a compound coverage wrapper rather than a new mechanic or site.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** The progression changes evidence, behavior, preparation, recovery, stewardship, destination, and risk. It does not swap in a higher-tier hide animal.

### Woodcutting progression

| Band | Concrete proof examples | What changes materially |
|---|---|---|

## 3. Ordinary-economy content proof

Nine situations make the background economy visible:

1. household flax exchange;
2. husbandry and animal-processing handoff;
3. shared textile workroom;
4. tannery intake and leather exchange;
5. fuel-worker intake and charcoal store;
6. renderer intake and maintenance supply;
7. imported steel receiving and inspection;
8. market, accessible storage, and craft cluster;

These situations expose custody and physical work without creating Farming, Textile, Rendering, Charcoal-making, infrastructure crafting, or business management. Public works remain the responsibility of ordinary crews; the player may supply accepted inputs and perform only already-approved equipment craft or refurbishment where independently relevant.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Ordinary society supplies every represented equipment input and remains functional without the player. Player gathering adds source control, knowledge, responsive supply, and civic participation rather than monopolizing production.

## 5. Required playstyle stress tests

### Hunting-heavy player

The player rotates behaviorally distinct opportunities, retains eligible hides for Leatherworking, routes food and qualified renderer value through ordinary processing, and trades accepted surplus for other inputs. Contextual acceptance prevents infinite dumping; a disclosed destination prevents authored work from stranding output.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Exact demand and value remain **FUTURE BALANCE ONLY**.

### Woodcutting-heavy player

The player chooses legitimate household, renewal, bank, reserve, and recovery work. Ordinary wood travels through one declared main intake; eligible bark remains secondary; fuel does not override repair or stewardship. Ordinary cutters provide background supply.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Frequency of public repair, fuel demand, and general intake remains **FUTURE BALANCE ONLY**.

### Buyer/crafter

The player purchases lawful ordinary inputs, commissions textile conversion, Smiths or works leather from accessible storage, and maintains equipment without training either gathering skill. Stock condition and disruptions preserve world connection without making gathering mandatory.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Price and stock tuning remain **FUTURE BALANCE ONLY**.

### Mostly self-supplying player

The player hunts eligible hides, gathers bark through legitimate Woodcutting, performs Leatherworking, and supplies accepted wood to ordinary fuel workers while still relying on households, textile workers, renderers, traders, and steel importers. Self-supply means controlled participation, not solitary production of every input.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** No hidden skill or forced chore is required.

### Post-Practical-Mastery player

The player applies H6/W6 judgment to changing authored conditions, combines gathering with a valid delivery or repair need, maintains aspirational equipment, and gains nonnumeric payoff through earlier diagnosis, avoided invalid work, safer recovery, efficient routing, or purpose-limited access.

**Result: ARCHITECTURE VALIDATED.** Cadence and reward strength remain **FUTURE BALANCE ONLY**; no superior material tier is needed.
