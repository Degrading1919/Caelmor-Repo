# Caelmor — Lowmark Woodland Content Portfolio

**Status:** substantial provisional content portfolio for Creative Director audit. Every source family, working label, opportunity composition, ecological trait, and use assignment in this document remains replaceable. This portfolio proves that the approved six Woodcutting archetypes can support five progression bands without final species, resource tiers, new inventory materials, exact levels, XP, timings, yields, prices, coordinates, tools, schemas, lore edits, or S01 demand.

## Authority and fixed constraints

This portfolio instantiates the approved relationship:

```text
stewardship-approved selective harvest
→ ordinary wood as the primary relationship
+ tannin-rich bark when an eligible source and legitimate cut physically provide it
→ optional delivery of suitable ordinary wood to ordinary fuel workers
→ wood charcoal through ordinary labor
```

It uses the approved Woodcutting archetypes:

- **W1:** tended-edge maintenance;
- **W2:** renewal-stand harvest;
- **W3:** bank-protection intervention;
- **W4:** damaged-woodland recovery;
- **W5:** public-repair reserve selection;
- **W6:** constrained selective extraction.

The following remain binding:

- Ordinary wood is one broad provisional tangible inventory state outside the nine equipment-chain states. It is not a species catalog or progression tier.
- Tannin-rich bark is guaranteed when a valid source/action is eligible. It is never a random bonus, separate action, bark-only route, or bark-only node.
- Fuel workers make charcoal. Woodcutting may provide acceptable wood flow but does not gain a charcoal recipe or skill.
- No source exists only for XP, bark, fuel, or progression-band labeling.
- A source may appear across several bands when the work relationship changes materially.
- Public repair, household need, and woodland continuity can outweigh immediate sale.
- The player is one supplier among households, cutters, yard workers, public crews, and traders.
- Exact custodians, legal powers, routes, and sites remain unresolved.
- Great War remains are finite and evidentiary, not a renewable timber supply.
- No magical, Stonebound, Volkhari, or supernatural source is used.

## Portfolio design rule

The portfolio separates three layers that later content production must not collapse:

1. **Source family:** the ecological and working role of the growth.
2. **Source condition:** healthy renewal, overgrowth, obstruction, storm damage, flood damage, fire pressure, or reserved maturity.
3. **Work purpose:** household maintenance, renewal, bank safety, recovery, public repair, or constrained extraction.

Progression is created by changing the relationship among all three layers. A mature source does not become high-level merely because it is large, and a differently named tree does not constitute a new method.

### Tangible ordinary-wood rule

Valid Woodcutting produces tangible ordinary wood that the player may keep, bank, trade through a dependable baseline outlet, supply to household or civic repair demand, or offer to fuel workers when suitable. Purpose-bound work still preserves its disclosed special destination through successful completion, but ordinary wood is not locked to that destination. Eligible bark follows its tanning relationship separately. No opportunity creates species-specific logs, grades, repair-stock items, fuelwood items, offcut inventories, or multiple wood tiers.

## Source-family profiles

### S-WD1 — Household managed tree

**Working concept:** ordinary long-lived trees deliberately kept along fields, paths, yards, orchards, productive plots, property edges, or settlement margins and periodically maintained without treating the whole living source as disposable timber. Pollard/boundary and productive-tree presentations are variants of one source relationship, not separate node families.

| Required field | Provisional content |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Provides some combination of shade, boundary definition, wind moderation, soil holding, habitat, food, shelter, or other continuing household value while tolerating planned maintenance. Exact benefits depend on later species selection. |
| Plausible setting | Agricultural edges, maintained lanes, household yards, orchards, productive plots, and settlement margins where obstruction, damage, or source health creates real work. **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED.** |
| W-pattern support | W1 as the clearest use; W4 when storm damage or neglect turns maintenance into recovery. |
| Ordinary wood-use relationships | Household repair and maintenance, market intake, and accepted fuel-worker flow. No dimensional part, productive-tree wood, or species item is established. |
| Bark eligibility | Ineligible by default. Maintenance pruning should not become an automatic tanning supply. A later selected species could justify limited eligibility only through explicit review. |
| Charcoal suitability | Some sound prunings and rejected pieces can enter fuel-worker intake after household and repair needs. The player never converts them directly. |
| Stewardship and access | Household or local custodian direction; work should preserve the living source, its productive value where present, shared passage, adjacent crops, and nearby structures. |
| Public and household repair | Strong household relationship; modest public use where an edge obstructs access or produces suitable small repair flow. |
| Progression contribution | Teaches source eligibility, restraint, directional cutting around property, complete recovery, and the possibility that standing household value exceeds recovered wood. Later variants add damage assessment. |
| Continued usefulness | Close access, clear purpose, household demand, productive-tree care, and useful fuel or repair flow keep it relevant after Novice. |
| Replaceability | Replaceable by any ordinary managed tree form that credibly supports periodic maintenance. The household relationship and retained living value matter more than species or name. |

**Why it deserves a separate relationship:** it teaches that Woodcutting can preserve living household value while still producing useful work. Pollard, boundary, orchard, and productive-tree variants reuse this lesson rather than multiplying families.

### S-WD2 — Managed renewal coppice

**Working concept:** a deliberately renewed stand where selected stems are harvested in rotation and the rooted source continues producing future growth.

| Required field | Provisional content |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Maintains a recurring patchwork of younger growth and cover while producing repeated ordinary wood from a bounded area. Exact renewal biology awaits species review. |
| Plausible setting | Communal or household working woodland outside dense habitation, with visible cut history, protected regrowth, and access for recovery. **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED.** |
| W-pattern support | W2 as the defining pattern; W4 after storm, flood, or fire stress; W6 when renewal, access, and competing demand combine. |
| Ordinary wood-use relationships | General household and market flow, small and medium repair relationships, and fuel-worker feed when accepted. No dimensional grades are created. |
| Bark eligibility | Eligible on qualifying harvested stems when their bark is physically suitable. Eligibility is visible and guaranteed for that work, not rolled. |
| Charcoal suitability | A credible recurring feed source after sound repair or household claims; acceptance depends on condition and fuel-worker demand. |
| Stewardship and access | Rotation, retained standards or equivalent future growth, recovery closures, weather, and local demand govern which part can be worked. No quota simulation is required. |
| Public and household repair | Supports routine repair flows; can be redirected during shortage without becoming the only public reserve. |
| Progression contribution | Teaches selection among similar stems, regeneration logic, complete recovery, bark co-output, and destination choice. |
| Continued usefulness | Dependable access and mixed destinations make it useful at every band, while later work adds closures, shortages, and constrained recovery. |
| Replaceability | Replaceable by any locally plausible resprouting or rotational working-wood system. The renewal pattern is essential; the eventual species is not. |

**Why it deserves a separate family:** it is the portfolio's clearest renewable ordinary-wood relationship and its most legible source of tanning bark without creating bark harvest content.

### S-WD3 — Bank-binding riparian tree

**Working concept:** ordinary water-edge growth whose roots and living cover help hold vulnerable banks. The same source can become obstructive or unsafe, making selective intervention necessary.

| Required field | Provisional content |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Holds soil, moderates edge disturbance, shades water or bank margins, and catches debris. These are physical functions, not magic. |
| Plausible setting | Managed river, side-channel, irrigation, crossing, or floodplain edges where existing map and waterworks support it. Every concrete placement is **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED.** |
| W-pattern support | W3 defines its ordinary use; W4 covers flood or storm damage; W6 combines bank protection with difficult recovery or neighboring claims. |
| Ordinary wood-use relationships | Public or household repair, bank or path work, market intake where sound, or fuel-worker feed for rejected suitable pieces. No special water-resistant material is implied. |
| Bark eligibility | Eligible only when the authorized removal is physically suitable and the bark remains usable. Pruning protected living cover does not automatically yield bark. |
| Charcoal suitability | Secondary. Removed damaged wood or offcuts may be accepted, but healthy protective growth cannot be cut for fuel. |
| Stewardship and access | Bank condition, shared water, erosion risk, active flood, public access, and the need to retain roots govern work. Ordinary water stewards may identify need without creating magical practice or fixed office powers. |
| Public and household repair | Strong civic relationship where obstruction or failure threatens access or waterworks; ordinary wood can return to nearby repair when sound. |
| Progression contribution | Teaches the player to judge what must remain, sequence cuts around unstable ground, and recover material without worsening the bank. |
| Continued usefulness | Changing water and damage conditions can create varied work at several bands without changing the source's material identity. |
| Replaceability | Replaceable by a future canon-supported riparian species or managed form. It must retain the bank-protection relationship and cannot become a magical tree. |

**Why it deserves a separate family:** it binds Woodcutting directly to Lowmark's maintained water landscape and creates a real reason to refuse apparently available wood.

### S-WD4 — Public repair reserve standard

**Working concept:** sound managed growth deliberately retained over time because crossings, dikes, ferries, mills, boats, buildings, and other public works sometimes need demanding repair stock.

| Required field | Provisional content |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Mature retained growth provides canopy, habitat, and long-term woodland continuity while held against future need. No exceptional biology or magical property is assumed. |
| Plausible setting | A protected part of managed working woodland with credible extraction and custody links to public repair. **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED.** |
| W-pattern support | W5 as the defining pattern; W6 when difficult access, neighboring growth, damage, fire risk, or competing claims constrain extraction. |
| Ordinary wood-use relationships | Broad structural and public repair relationships. The document does not split ordinary wood into grades, beams, hull pieces, or separate inventory states. |
| Bark eligibility | Eligible on a valid harvested source where bark is suitable. It remains a secondary co-output and never justifies opening the reserve. |
| Charcoal suitability | Reserved wood is not fuel feed. Only ordinary wood released from repair custody and physically suitable for fuel work may be accepted. |
| Stewardship and access | Purpose-limited opening, visible reservation, source matching, future-stock protection, and direct custody to the repair need. No permit item or contract-management system is created. |
| Public and household repair | Strong public relationship. Household demand normally uses other supply unless an ordinary custodian releases surplus or rejects. |
| Progression contribution | Teaches matching source to need, minimizing collateral removal, preserving future capacity, and maintaining custody through delivery. |
| Continued usefulness | Recurring repair demand and changing constraint combinations support post-mastery participation without superior timber tiers. |
| Replaceability | Replaceable by any later ordinary species that can physically serve retained repair stock. The reserve relationship, not the name, carries its identity. |

**Why it deserves a separate family:** it expresses Lowmark's future-facing repair culture and gives Masterful Woodcutting a purpose beyond extracting more volume.

## Bark eligibility distribution

The portfolio deliberately does not make bark universal.

| Source family | Baseline eligibility | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| S-WD1 household managed tree | No | Routine pollard, boundary, orchard, or productive-tree maintenance should remain household work, not a concealed bark farm. |
| S-WD2 renewal coppice | Yes, on qualifying stems | The recurring harvested stem relationship can credibly supply usable bark alongside wood. |
| S-WD3 bank-binding tree | Conditional | Only a justified intact removal may provide usable bark; bank protection overrides tanning demand. |
| S-WD4 public repair reserve | Yes, on lawful harvest | Bark can be recovered from a necessary main harvest, but never opens access to the reserve. |

This distribution gives tanning bark more than one player source relationship without allowing every Woodcutting action to become a bark route. Ordinary forestry and yard recovery remain parallel suppliers.

## Charcoal suitability distribution

Charcoal suitability is a destination judgment, not a resource trait that creates a new wood item.

| Source family | Suitable ordinary flow | Protected or rejected flow |
|---|---|---|
| S-WD1 household managed tree | Sound prunings not needed for repair | Living source, productive growth, and household-reserved pieces |
| S-WD2 renewal coppice | Accepted surplus and ordinary fuel-directed harvest | Reserved regrowth and repair-priority wood |
| S-WD3 bank-binding tree | Removed damaged pieces or offcuts accepted by fuel workers | Healthy roots, living bank cover, contaminated or waterlogged rejects |
| S-WD4 public repair reserve | Only rejected offcuts after custody review | All sound reserved structural flow |

Fuel workers determine acceptance and perform conversion. Players do not classify a source as a separate fuelwood material or receive charcoal directly.

### Minimum count judgment

## Aggressive rejection ledger

| Rejected candidate | Why considered | Reason rejected or deferred |
|---|---|---|
| **Five-species progression ladder** | Conventional readable Woodcutting progression | Reskins one action, pressures new inventory identities, and makes species carry mastery instead of judgment. |
| **Dedicated tanning-bark grove** | Clear bark source and tannery connection | Creates a bark-only route and incentives to treat legitimate wood work as packaging for bark. |
| **Fuelwood or charcoal tree** | Direct link between Woodcutting and Smithing fuel | Makes charcoal ecology nonsensical, bypasses fuel workers, and adds a single-purpose source. |
| **Rare masterwork hardwood** | Easy aspirational identity | No missing equipment function requires it; aspirational progression comes from workmanship, fitting, custody, and repairability. |
| **Magically resonant or memory-bearing timber** | Mythic late-band distinction | Unsupported escalation; ritual or emotional significance does not create a magical material. |
| **Stonebound-grown or Stonebound-altered wood** | Cross-system mystery | Stonebound are passive responders and are not resources or production infrastructure. |
| **Great War ruin timber as renewable salvage** | Strong historical atmosphere | War remains are finite, custodial, and evidentiary. They cannot support repeatable gathering. |
| **Imported prestige timber as progression ceiling** | Cross-region trade sink | Lowmark v1 progression cannot depend on an unverified route or superior imported material. Imports may supplement ordinary supply later. |
| **Deadwood field node** | Low-cost damaged-source content | Treats damage as a static resource field, ignores habitat and recovery judgment, and risks endless salvage logic. |
| **Orchard-wood inventory material** | Gives household source a distinct output | Adds an unnecessary material identity; ordinary wood and contextual use already carry the relationship. |
| **Bark rarity roll** | Simple scarcity control | Contradicts the approved source-conditional guaranteed output rule. |
| **Universal bark from every cut** | Simplifies supply | Removes source knowledge, distorts household work, and turns all Woodcutting into tanning supply. |

## Provisional recommendations for Creative Director review

### P-WCP01 — Four source relationships

**Recommendation:** carry four relationships into node/content production. S-WD1 includes pollard, boundary, orchard, and productive-tree variants through shared household management logic.

**Alternative:** split productive household trees into a fifth family. Rejected because the distinction can be expressed through source condition and retained value without another node/asset family.

### P-WCP02 — Bounded bark-bearing relationships

**Recommendation:** treat renewal coppice as the strong baseline bark relationship; keep authorized bank-tree removal and lawful reserve harvest conditional on final ecology; keep household managed trees ineligible by default.

**Alternative:** decide eligibility only after final species selection. This is ecologically safer but leaves the portfolio unable to prove a bounded bark distribution. If chosen, the current assignments remain test assumptions rather than production facts.

### P-WCP03 — Ten opportunity compositions as proof, not launch quota

**Recommendation:** retain the ten compositions as a design coverage set. Later production may combine or omit them if every progression decision remains represented.

**Alternative:** treat all ten as required standalone nodes. Rejected because several are authored conditions or compound briefs that can reuse sources, sites, and interaction logic.

### P-WCP04 — Ordinary wood remains tangible and broadly useful

**Recommendation:** valid work produces the single broad ordinary-wood inventory state. The player may keep, bank, use baseline trade, or supply a specialist destination. Purpose-bound work preserves its disclosed destination, and content exposes at most one special destination choice when the distinction matters. Do not create species or grade sorting.

**Alternative:** route all wood automatically to one store or destination. Rejected by D11 because it removes accumulation, banking, trade, and later-use choice.
