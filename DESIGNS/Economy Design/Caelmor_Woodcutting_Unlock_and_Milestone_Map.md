# Caelmor — Woodcutting Unlock and Milestone Map

**Status:** Proposal-stage progression placement after representative-method calibration
**Scope:** When Woodcutting methods, stewardship responsibilities, access, and mastery opportunities become available from levels 1–99

## Progression principles

Woodcutting progression is a deepening relationship with inhabited woodland, banks, repair demand, source condition, and custody. It is not a ladder of increasingly valuable trees.

- Levels establish readable capability anchors; stewardship and access conditions still govern actual work.
- One broad tangible ordinary-wood state serves the whole portfolio. Bark remains a conditional guaranteed co-output of eligible legitimate work.
- The same cutting, selection, and recovery action keeps its fixed XP at every player level.
- Earlier sources remain useful for convenience, bark, ordinary trade, repair, and fuel-worker supply.
- WM8 is an approved post-80 mixed route with no circuit bonus XP.

## Band narrative

### Novice, levels 1–30

The player learns that cutting is a custodial act. WM1 teaches inspection, authorization, retained growth, recovery, and tangible ordinary-wood destinations. WM2 introduces managed renewal, rotation, and the first bark co-output. By level 20, the player can understand why bark appears only through legitimate work and why ordinary wood remains primary.

### Competent, levels 31–50

WM3 makes water-edge stability and public protection part of the skill. The player now chooses between convenient household work, dependable renewal supply, and slower bank-protection intervention. Destination planning creates meaningful tradeoffs among market, repair, tannery, fuel, and storage.

### Advanced, levels 51–70

WM4 rewards route familiarity by chaining known renewal work. WM5 adds bounded damage recovery, where diagnosis matters more than simply finding a damaged source. The player learns to distinguish a useful recovery from a dangerous or nonproductive one.

### Masterful, levels 71–98

WM6 introduces trusted public-repair custody, and WM7 combines stability, fire, access, route, demand, and retained growth. At level 80, WM8 makes practical mastery a familiar mixed-stewardship route. Levels 80–98 refine source-purpose choices, repair relationships, efficient ordering, and personal stewardship without withholding ordinary wood or bark behind the cap.

### Completion, level 99

Level 99 should commemorate the player’s relationship with Lowmark’s working landscape: household maintainer, renewal steward, bank protector, repair supplier, or integrated practitioner. It should not create a superior timber state, mandatory final tool, or large power spike.

## Density and obsolescence checks

- The longest intentional quiet span is levels **81–98**, eighteen levels between Practical Mastery and completion. It is quiet in authored unlocks but active in source-purpose specialization, route mastery, custody, familiar routines, trust, constraint recombination, and personal stewardship.
- The 1–15 opening is intentionally simple so the player learns selective work before managing regeneration and bark eligibility.
- The major method beats at 15, 31, 51, 56, 71, 75, and 80 are spaced across the bands; supporting beats reinforce real responsibilities rather than filling levels.
- WM1 remains the most convenient household source; WM2 remains the balanced wood/bark route; WM3 remains civic bank work; WM4 remains sustainable route XP; WM5 remains condition-specific recovery; WM6 remains trusted repair work; WM7 remains multi-constraint judgment; WM8 remains integrated route mastery.
- No level introduces a superior wood tier, bark-only node, fuelwood item, repair-stock item, or arbitrary salvage resource.
- Civic and reserve access are concentrated in WM3/WM6/WM7, so ordinary progression does not depend on commissions.
- Tool capability remains a future functional question rather than a ladder of named tiers.

## Unlock-structure readiness

All eight methods have a proposed access point, all five progression identities have meaningful responsibilities, Practical Mastery lands at level 80, and level 99 is commemorative rather than a power spike. Exact unlock implementation, final access conditions, and map placement remain later production checks.
