# Caelmor — Lowmark Hunting Content Portfolio

**Status:** PROVISIONAL proposal-stage content portfolio for Creative Director audit. The functional fauna labels, opportunity compositions, access states, and content selections below are inventions for testing the approved Hunting architecture. They are not canon species, final nodes, encounter definitions, or implementation commitments.

## Purpose and authority

This portfolio proves that the six approved Hunting patterns can support meaningful progression from Novice through Practical Mastery with a compact fauna set:

- **H1:** guided margin opportunity;
- **H2:** crop-pressure response;
- **H3:** concealed wet-ground opportunity;
- **H4:** route-crossing or moving-range opportunity;
- **H5:** threat-complicated recovery;
- **H6:** stewardship commission.

It carries forward the approved output model:

```text
eligible ordinary game
→ read, prepare, approach, resolve, recover
→ edible relationship where physically supported
→ raw hide when hide-bearing and successfully recovered
→ suitable fat may route into ordinary rendering
```

This document does not select final species, exact sites, levels, XP, timings, yields, prices, drop rates, tool tiers, encounter controls, food items, recipes, schemas, or map routes. It introduces no material state, skill, ranged content, supernatural source, or player-processing profession.

## Portfolio selection standard

A fauna candidate belongs in the v1 portfolio only if it:

1. creates a behavior or recovery problem that the other candidates cannot express equally well;
2. supports at least two approved Hunting patterns or a necessary progression function;
3. has an ecological role that fits Lowmark's worked fields, wet margins, and ordinary habitat;
4. connects to at least one real output or civic relationship beyond XP;
5. remains useful after its introductory band;
6. does not require a unique material, minigame, biome, or production chain; and
7. can be implemented through the shared Read–Prepare–Approach–Resolve–Recover grammar.

## Selected compact fauna portfolio

The minimum recommended portfolio is **four functional fauna candidates**. These are behavior families, not final biological taxonomies. Final species work may map one canon-supported animal to a candidate, split a candidate only when ecology demands it, or replace the label while preserving the function.

### F1 — Small field-margin forager

**Classification:** PROVISIONAL ordinary fauna candidate.

| Requirement | Provisional definition |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Feeds and shelters along cultivated margins, drainage edges, hedges, or scrub close to worked land. It is common enough to teach responsible use without becoming an infinite stationary resource. |
| Broad habitat | Worked-land margins and nearby ordinary cover; no exact field, settlement, or route selected. |
| Supported patterns | H1 strongly; H2 where verified feeding pressure exists; H5 only when recovery ground is complicated. |
| Behavior difference | Relies on short movement, concealment, and a brief escape response rather than territorial confrontation, flock movement, or long-range travel. |
| Evidence language | Small feeding traces, disturbed cover, short runs, and repeated margin use. Evidence is clear enough for Novice instruction but can be confused with incidental activity if the player ignores context. |
| Edible relationship | **Yes at functional level** when the target and recovered condition qualify. No item, cut, quantity, or recipe is selected. |
| Raw-hide eligibility | **No for this equipment chain.** Any minor skin or pelt value is outside the approved raw-hide foundation and does not justify another state. |
| Renderer relationship | **No required equipment-chain relationship.** It does not exist to feed tallow production. |
| Stewardship | Open or guided take where ordinary pressure permits; household need or local recovery can close or redirect it. Small size does not make unrestricted removal responsible. |
| Progression relevance | Teaches H1, basic sign, clean resolution, output eligibility, and the fact that edible value does not imply raw hide. Later variants test evidence quality, route convenience, or household destination. |
| Continued usefulness | Accessible food relationship, household demand, convenient market supply, and a low-risk way to practice clean recovery after more difficult opportunities unlock. |
| Replaceability test | Waterfowl could also teach edible-without-hide, but wet footing and flock behavior add too much at the first introduction. Rooters and grazers would teach the wrong expectation that most useful game yields raw hide. **Retain.** |

### F2 — Field-edge rooter

**Classification:** PROVISIONAL ordinary fauna candidate. It may later be reconciled with a canon-supported Lowmark rooter without importing any legacy material catalog.

| Requirement | Provisional definition |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Disturbs soil and consumes crops or stored field-edge food while also turning neglected ground. It creates real household conflict without being treated as vermin everywhere. |
| Broad habitat | Cultivated edges, recovering ground, orchard margins, scrub, and ordinary cover adjoining worked land. |
| Supported patterns | H1 in a guided introduction; H2 strongly; H5 when territorial behavior or hazardous recovery complicates the work; H6 for disputed pressure or bounded response. |
| Behavior difference | Follows feeding and rooting paths, can reverse direction quickly, and may defend space when pressed. Correct approach matters more than simply following a visible trail. |
| Evidence language | Rooted soil, damaged boundary growth, feeding marks, bedding cover, and route traces. Similar damage from water or work must remain distinguishable through context. |
| Edible relationship | **Yes at functional level** when legitimately recovered. No meat catalog is created. |
| Raw-hide eligibility | **Yes, source-conditionally guaranteed** when the final animal choice is hide-bearing and recovery succeeds. All usable output remains the existing raw-hide state. |
| Renderer relationship | **Yes as a possible routed relationship** when suitable fat is present and the recovery is directed through ordinary animal processing. Fat is never a required player inventory item. |
| Stewardship | Households may request a response to verified damage. The correct answer may be a limited hunt, changed access, or refusal if the account does not match current evidence. |
| Progression relevance | Carries H2 from guided learning into independent evidence reading. Later use adds territorial risk, competing household claims, and recovery under pressure without upgrading the material. |
| Continued usefulness | Supplies food, eligible hide, renderer flow, crop-pressure response, and tannery or market demand. Its economic usefulness does not depend on remaining the best training method. |
| Replaceability test | The grazer supplies similar outputs, but it cannot reproduce rooted damage, close-cover behavior, or the household evidence problem without becoming behaviorally identical. **Retain.** |

### F3 — Reed-margin flocking game

**Classification:** PROVISIONAL ordinary fauna candidate. It may later be reconciled with an approved Lowmark waterfowl concept if ecology and Creative Director review support one.

| Requirement | Provisional definition |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Feeds, rests, or moves in groups between wet margins, shallow water, and worked land. Its warning behavior makes habitat disturbance visible. |
| Broad habitat | Managed wet meadow, reed margin, drainage edge, ordinary shallows, and agricultural margins where map verification supports them. |
| Supported patterns | H1 where locally guided; H2 where feeding pressure is verified; H3 strongly; H4 through repeated movement between resting and feeding ground; H5 through difficult wet recovery. |
| Behavior difference | Group alertness, sudden collective movement, and water-edge escape make disturbance control more important than following one individual's trail. |
| Evidence language | Feeding traces, tracks at soft margins, disturbed reeds, droppings or feathers as signs only, and changes in flock presence. Signs do not become inventory outputs. |
| Edible relationship | **Yes at functional level** when the final animal and recovery support it. No named bird, cut, or Cooking recipe is selected. |
| Raw-hide eligibility | **No.** Feathers, skins, and similar outputs are excluded because no approved equipment-chain function requires them. |
| Renderer relationship | **No required equipment-chain relationship.** It is not included to create tallow supply. |
| Stewardship | Nesting, local pressure, water condition, and household crop need can open, narrow, or close an opportunity. Exact seasons and rules remain deferred. |
| Progression relevance | Introduces H3 through visibility, footing, and group alert. H4 variants teach interception and changing presence without real-time migration simulation. |
| Continued usefulness | Provides a food relationship, crop-response role, wet-ground practice, and ordinary market or household value. Its non-hide identity prevents Leatherworking demand from defining all Hunting. |
| Replaceability test | The small forager teaches edible-without-hide but cannot support group alert, wet recovery, or movement-window decisions. The grazer uses group movement differently and carries hide value. **Retain.** |

### F4 — Wet-meadow traveling grazer

**Classification:** PROVISIONAL ordinary fauna candidate. It does not assume that any war-affected or magically disturbed animal is a renewable resource.

| Requirement | Provisional definition |
|---|---|
| Ecological role | Moves between feeding, shelter, and water across wet meadow, recovering ground, or broad habitat transitions. Its passage can reveal changing habitat pressure without a population simulation. |
| Broad habitat | Wet meadow, scrub transition, ordinary flood-shaped ground, and movement corridors whose exact placement remains **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. |
| Supported patterns | H3 through wet-ground sign and recovery; H4 strongly; H5 where ordinary danger complicates recovery; H6 for evidence-led stewardship or competing destination need. |
| Behavior difference | Wary group movement, repeated crossings, and long alert distance reward anticipation and route reading rather than close-cover pursuit. |
| Evidence language | Browsing pattern, bedding areas, crossing traces, water-edge use, direction of group movement, and absence from a normally used area. |
| Edible relationship | **Yes at functional level** when legitimately and cleanly recovered. No food inventory definition is made. |
| Raw-hide eligibility | **Yes, source-conditionally guaranteed** when the final animal choice is hide-bearing and recovery conditions succeed. No superior hide tier is implied. |
| Renderer relationship | **Yes as a possible routed relationship** when the recovered animal provides suitable feedstock and the player chooses an ordinary processing destination. |
| Stewardship | A viable opportunity depends on current pressure, safe access, recent take, habitat condition, and household or civic claims. A knowledgeable player may redirect or refuse the hunt. |
| Progression relevance | Carries H4 route inference and supports the harder H3/H5 combinations. At Practical Mastery, it tests whether the player can distinguish genuine imbalance from convenient testimony. |
| Continued usefulness | Offers a full food–hide–renderer relationship, planned self-supply, longer route decisions, and mastery compositions without a new material. |
| Replaceability test | The rooter also supplies food, hide, and renderer flow, but cannot reproduce long-alert herd movement, corridor inference, or wet-meadow recovery without losing its own identity. **Retain.** |

All six Hunting patterns are supported without a one-species-per-pattern structure. H5 and H6 deliberately recombine earlier fauna and habitat knowledge instead of demanding mastery-only quarry.

### Shared group-movement implementation rule

F3 flocking game and F4 traveling grazer must use one reusable alert-and-movement framework with parameterized presentation. F3 emphasizes many nearby observers, collective disturbance, and short wet-margin escape; F4 emphasizes longer alert distance, repeated crossings, and broader route inference. They do not justify separate AI systems merely because one is presented as a flock and the other as a herd.

## Mastery payoff contract

Every Advanced, Masterful, or Practical Mastery composition must provide at least one nonnumeric advantage that an attentive player can understand:

1. **Earlier diagnosis:** recognize the valid target, route, or closure before committing.
2. **Avoided invalid work:** refuse stale, unsafe, wasteful, or incorrectly attributed opportunity.
3. **Cleaner recovery:** protect already-approved outputs through preparation and route choice.
4. **Combined purpose:** satisfy more than one compatible destination or civic need through one legitimate hunt.
5. **Purpose-limited access:** receive trusted access to a bounded problem without a permit item or mastery-only resource.
6. **Efficient redirection:** know when ordinary supply, another open opportunity, or a different destination is the better answer.

These payoffs prevent mastery from becoming a sequence of visually different animals with higher numbers. They do not promise extra yields, guaranteed profit, unique drops, or exemption from stewardship.

## Output and destination discipline

Every purpose-bound opportunity must disclose at least one valid destination before the player commits to gathering. That destination cannot disappear during successful resolution. Contextual buyers may refuse unrelated surplus, but an authored opportunity may not strand the output it explicitly asked the player to recover. Hunting recovery routes eligible game through the chosen animal-processing relationship without creating a carcass, fat, or hidden-token inventory state.

| Candidate | Personal use | Household or market | Tannery / Leatherworking | Renderer | Civic value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| F1 small forager | Future food relationship | Yes | No | No | Margin pressure where verified |
| F2 rooter | Future food; raw hide for personal Leatherworking | Yes | Yes when recovery qualifies | Possible routed relationship | Crop-pressure response |
| F3 flocking game | Future food relationship | Yes | No | No required relationship | Wet-margin and crop-pressure response |
| F4 grazer | Future food; raw hide for personal Leatherworking | Yes | Yes when recovery qualifies | Possible routed relationship | Movement, safety, and stewardship response |

The portfolio does not require all outputs to be equally valuable. Its purpose is to give each opportunity several legitimate reasons to exist while keeping the approved inventory foundation intact.

## Ordinary supply coexistence

- Husbandry remains a baseline source of raw hide and renderer feedstock.
- Hunting provides control, discovery, responsive civic value, and direct recovery, not a monopoly.
- A player who does not Hunt can buy raw hide or finished leather and continue equipment progression.
- A player who Hunts heavily still depends on eligible opportunities, recovery conditions, processor acceptance, and real sinks.
- Food relationships give non-hide fauna lasting value, but their inventory and Cooking form remain a later design responsibility.
- Ordinary processors may accept suitable routed animal flow; they do not buy unlimited output or convert every hunt into tallow.

## Dependencies and morning review

### Required before final node production

1. **Final fauna reconciliation:** choose final species or canon-supported expressions for F1–F4 and verify ecological fit. The four functions should be preserved even if labels change.
2. **Map verification:** confirm broad worked-margin, wet-margin, transition, and hazard-overlap contexts without inventing routes or site counts.
3. **Encounter ownership:** confirm whether Hunting resolves contact through one dedicated noncombat framework or can hand dangerous contact into normal combat while Hunting retains eligibility and recovery.
4. **Recovery presentation:** select how the recovery choice is shown without creating carcass, fat, or work-in-progress inventory states.

### Safe to defer

- exact species names, appearance, animations, and sound;
- exact progression unlocks, opportunity counts, and locations;
- final edible states and Cooking recipes;
- exact hide and renderer eligibility by final species;
- exact closure causes, cadence, and responsible offices;
- exact tools, success handling, quantities, rates, prices, and XP;
- all S01 ranged implications.

## Portfolio result

Four provisional fauna candidates are sufficient to prove all six Hunting patterns across Novice, Competent, Advanced, Masterful, and Practical Mastery. Eleven opportunity compositions demonstrate progression through behavior, evidence, access, preparation, recovery, stewardship, destination, risk, and economic purpose rather than material tiers. H5 and H6 reuse existing fauna as compound content, avoiding mastery-only quarry and late resource bloat.
