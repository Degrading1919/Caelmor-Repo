**Status:** Proposal-stage audit of Hunting and Woodcutting unlock placement
**Scope:** Meaningful progression beats, method access, quiet spans, obsolescence, and level-99 identity

## Audit basis

This audit uses the approved fixed-XP calibration and the closed milestone envelopes:

| Milestone | Closed envelope |
|---:|---:|
| 30 | ~10–12 h |
| 50 | ~28–34 h |
| 70 | ~58–68 h |
| 80 | ~85–92 h |
| 99 | ~135–150 h |

The unlock maps add no skill, resource, species tier, tree tier, or XP modifier. They place the existing HM1–HM8 and WM1–WM8 methods behind readable combinations of level, knowledge, location, trust, source condition, and route familiarity.

## Early-content relevance audit

### Hunting

- HM1 remains the convenient low-attention food and familiar-route method.
- HM2 remains the main crop-response and raw-hide self-supply method.
- HM3 remains a dependable food route and one leg of HM8.
- HM4 remains the focused movement-window XP method.
- HM5 remains the focused hide/renderer supply method even after HM8.
- HM6 remains difficult recovery practice rather than a universal replacement.
- HM7 remains an authored judgment and civic-access method.
- HM8 remains a route integration method, not an exclusive-output method.

### Woodcutting

- WM1 remains the shortest, safest household source.
- WM2 remains the balanced ordinary-wood and bark route.
- WM3 remains valuable for bank and public-protection work.
- WM4 remains a durable route-efficiency method.
- WM5 remains useful only when a real damage state exists, preserving its authored character.
- WM6 remains the trusted repair-custody method.
- WM7 remains the high-attention constraint method.
- WM8 remains the integrated route method without a new wood tier.

## Gate-ownership audit

| Progression question | Level gate | Knowledge/access gate | Tool/location dependency | Combination/mastery gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can the player attempt the method at all? | Establishes minimum readiness | Confirms the player understands the relevant grammar | Source must exist in the world | Later circuits require multiple valid legs |
| Can the player recover valuable output? | Does not guarantee it | Eligibility, preparation, and recovery determine it | Habitat, route, bank, processor, or custodian may limit it | Higher methods combine several conditions |
| Can the player access civic or reserve work? | Sets a readable competence floor | Trust and disclosed purpose govern access | Household, bank, reserve, or repair context must exist | Practical mastery handles competing claims |
| Does a tool improve progression? | No fixed tier is required here | Capability must answer a demonstrated friction | Future tool handling may affect safe work/access | It must not alter action XP or create material tiers |

This keeps level requirements readable while preventing the level number from replacing knowledge, access, or place.

## Progression-risk audit

| Risk | Current control | Remaining production check |
|---|---|---|
| HM8/WM8 become the only rational methods | Focused hide, bark, repair, food, civic, and low-attention alternatives remain explicitly useful | Test market values, output quantities, route density, and attention burden |
| Trust gates feel arbitrary | Each trust gate has a disclosed household, bank, reserve, or repair purpose | Prototype the evidence and access explanation |
| Quiet 86–91 ranges feel empty | Specialization and personal route refinement provide goals without fake unlocks | Verify feedback, route readability, and visible mastery progress |
| Method access arrives before the player understands prerequisites | Each major method follows a supporting beat or prior method familiarity | Playtest tutorialization and access messaging |
| Tool capability becomes a hidden tier ladder | No tool stats, materials, or item IDs are finalized | Only add a capability when a concrete friction is demonstrated |
| Civic work overtakes ordinary gathering | HM7/WM6 are optional and conditional; ordinary market/household routes remain | Verify availability and demand cadence |
| Level 99 feels empty | Both maps reserve a commemorative identity treatment without power creep | Design the final presentation later |

## Remaining dependencies

- exact map placement for wet margins, movement routes, banks, reserves, and mixed circuits;
- final ecology and source-condition selection;
- final access messaging and trust presentation;
- tool capability testing if ordinary actions expose a real friction;
- exact unlock implementation and UI presentation;
- output quantities, prices, market values, NPC stock, and balance;
- schemas, JSON, C#, art, and runtime implementation.

These dependencies do not require inventing new methods, materials, skills, or progression bands.

## Final audit result

The proposed maps give all eight methods per skill a coherent place, preserve active learning across 1–99, put practical mastery at level 80, keep early content relevant, and give level 99 a commemorative role. No filler tier ladder or hidden XP scaling is required.
