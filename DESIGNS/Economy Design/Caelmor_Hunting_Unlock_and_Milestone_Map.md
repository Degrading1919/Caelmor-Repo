# Caelmor — Hunting Unlock and Milestone Map

**Status:** Proposal-stage progression placement after representative-method calibration
**Scope:** When Hunting methods, responsibilities, access, and mastery opportunities become available from levels 1–99

## Progression principles

Hunting progression is a widening of judgment, recovery, access, and route responsibility. It is not a ladder of animals with larger hides.

- Levels are readable anchors, not a promise of one authored unlock at every level.
- A level gate establishes that the player has enough practical foundation to attempt the next responsibility; knowledge, access, trust, location, and prior familiarity still matter.
- The same Hunting action keeps its fixed base XP at every level.
- Earlier methods remain useful for food, raw hide, ordinary trade, self-supply, low attention, and convenient routes.
- HM8 is an approved post-80 mixed route with no circuit bonus XP.

## Band narrative

### Novice, levels 1–30

The player begins by learning to read a nearby margin and recover responsibly. HM1 teaches that a successful hunt is more than contact: approach, eligibility, recovery, and destination all matter. HM2 then introduces testimony, crop pressure, raw-hide eligibility, and the first meaningful self-supply choice. By level 24 the player can preserve qualifying hide without treating all fauna as loot.

### Competent, levels 31–50

The player gains a second identity through HM3: flock alert, wet footing, concealment, and a dependable food route. The central question becomes which opportunity is appropriate, not which one is numerically highest. Destination planning and route familiarity make household, market, tannery, and personal use distinct choices.

### Advanced, levels 51–70

HM4 teaches movement windows and fresh versus stale evidence. HM5 adds a deliberate grazer supply route with stronger hide and renderer value but greater recovery and processing burden. The player now chooses between XP density, output value, convenience, and route risk.

### Masterful, levels 71–98

HM6 makes danger a complication rather than a new quarry. HM7 makes evidence, trust, refusal, and redirection part of mastery. After level 80, HM8 combines known methods into a familiar route, while 80–98 refinement comes from specialization, cleaner recovery, unusual evidence combinations, and preferred custody—not superior animals or mandatory commissions.

### Completion, level 99

Level 99 is a public or personal mark of completion. It should commemorate the player’s chosen relationship with Lowmark—food supplier, hide practitioner, route reader, civic problem-solver, or some combination—without adding a major power spike or a required final output.

## Density and obsolescence checks

- The longest intentional quiet span is levels **86–91**, six levels while the player refines a chosen HM2/HM5/HM8 routine. It is quiet in authored unlocks but active in method specialization and route learning.
- The 1–15 opening is intentionally simple; HM1 teaches the core loop before HM2 adds evidence and output responsibility.
- Levels 31, 51, 56, 71, 78, and 80 carry the major band transitions. They are spaced to avoid a cluster of isolated method unlocks.
- HM1 remains useful for low attention and convenient food; HM2 remains a hide/crop response; HM3 remains a reliable food route; HM4 remains movement-window XP; HM5 remains focused hide supply; HM6 remains difficult recovery; HM7 remains authored judgment; HM8 remains route integration.
- No level introduces a superior hide, rare trophy, supernatural quarry, or mandatory post-80 resource.
- Civic access is concentrated in HM7 and selected HM6/HM8 variants, so Hunting does not depend on commissions for ordinary progression.
- The proposed tool treatment is functional and conditional, not a tier ladder.

## Unlock-structure readiness

All eight methods have a proposed access point, all five progression identities have meaningful responsibilities, Practical Mastery lands at level 80, and level 99 is commemorative rather than a power spike. Exact unlock implementation, final access conditions, and map placement remain later production checks.
