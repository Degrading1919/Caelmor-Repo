# Caelmor — Lowmark Economy Final Gap and Contradiction Audit

**Status:** Final proposal-stage audit after integration and revision
**Scope:** Lowmark v1 gathering and supply economy architecture only

## Audit basis

This audit covers the integrated proposal pack:

1. Lowmark gathering and supply architecture;
2. Hunting progression architecture;
3. Woodcutting progression architecture;
4. ordinary supply and player interaction architecture;
5. Lowmark placement requirements;
6. node-content blueprint;
7. source-to-sink dependency map;
8. economy stress-test report;
9. this final gap and contradiction audit;
10. the Creative Director morning decision ledger.

The review tested the approved six-material foundation, nine represented equipment-chain states, four recipe families, Hunting and Woodcutting relationships, ordinary supply, placement logic, content scope, and source-to-sink continuity. It did not test numerical balance, implementation data, exact maps, final species, or S01.

## First hostile audit

An independent reviewer assessed the integrated drafts before final revision.

| Area | Initial result | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Tannery access | **CONTRADICTORY** | Placement implied a normal commission service while the ordinary-supply architecture deliberately withheld unrestricted NPC input conversion. |
| Hunting band mapping | **CONTRADICTORY** | The Hunting document used overlapping bands while the first node blueprint presentation appeared to hard-lock several archetypes to one later band. |
| S01 boundary | **PARTIAL** | Two ordinary-wood sink descriptions named future Fletching despite ranged scope remaining deferred. |
| Pattern count | **PARTIAL** | The first count could be read as 18 unique pieces of content even though H5, H6, and W6 are compound or mastery wrappers. |
| Authored-state burden | **PARTIAL** | Reusable conditions lacked an explicit cap per authored instance and could multiply into excessive variants. |
| Discovery and mastery payoff | **THIN** | Knowledge, trust, and efficiency were named but lacked a minimum content contract. |
| Animal-processing handoff | **PARTIAL** | “Deliver eligible game” could imply an undeclared carcass or hidden-token inventory state. |
| Action-sequence burden | **PARTIAL** | Phase grammars could be mistaken for compulsory clicks rather than authoring structure. |
| Post-mastery recurrence | **PARTIAL** | Continuing value was plausible but needed an explicit non-volume payoff rule. |

The hostile review found no resource bloat, hidden skill, lore breach, supernatural material source, renewable war salvage, multiplayer dependency, source-less represented state, or missing equipment sink.

## Revisions applied

1. **Tannery boundary reconciled.** Ordinary tanners may buy accepted hide or bark and sell bounded finished leather. Player Leatherworking retains transformation identity; no universal on-demand NPC conversion is assumed. Any later bounded service requires separate approval.
2. **Hunting bands reconciled.** The node blueprint now preserves the source architecture’s overlap: H2 spans Novice to Competent, H3 Competent to Advanced, H4 Advanced, and H5 Advanced to Masterful. Bands express emphasis rather than hard node locks.
3. **S01 wording removed.** Ordinary wood points only to separately approved future uses. No ranged or Fletching demand justifies this economy.
4. **Pattern vocabulary clarified.** Six Hunting, six Woodcutting, and six ordinary-interface patterns remain the bounded vocabulary. Compound/mastery wrappers do not require standalone sites, species, mechanics, or interfaces.
5. **Authored-state scope capped.** The shared five-state vocabulary is open; guided/purpose-limited; reserved/commissioned; disrupted/recovery; unsafe/closed. An ordinary instance receives at most one altered state unless story-critical, and no archetype must implement every state.
6. **Mastery payoff contract added.** Every Advanced-or-later instance must deliver at least one of: earlier diagnosis, invalid-work avoidance, safer or cleaner recovery, combined delivery efficiency, or legitimate purpose-limited access. No new output or material is created.
7. **Animal handoff clarified.** Eligible game is routed during recovery through an animal-processing interface. There is no carcass, fat, or hidden-token inventory state in this equipment chain.
8. **Interaction burden bounded.** Phase sequences are authoring responsibilities. Later design exposes only meaningful decisions and may compress non-decisions.
9. **Post-mastery logic completed.** Recombined constraints, civic repair demand, maintenance, authored condition changes, and the mastery payoff contract provide continuing reasons to participate without new material tiers or compulsory daily work.

## Second-pass classification

| Audit category | Final classification | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Hunting progression identity | **STRONG** | Six patterns change evidence, habitat, preparation, recovery, purpose, and routing across five overlapping bands. H5 and H6 reuse rather than multiply systems. |
| Woodcutting progression identity | **STRONG** | Six patterns change cutting purpose, source condition, stewardship, recovery, public demand, and logistics without species tiers. W6 is a mastery recombination. |
| Ordinary supply coexistence | **STRONG** | Every represented state has an ordinary or trade path; player gathering and craft add control, knowledge, and transformation without becoming the world’s sole supply. |
| Processing and custody clarity | **STRONG** | Direct textile commission, bounded tannery supply, market-facing fuel/rendering, imported steel receiving, storage, workshops, and repair have distinct roles. |
| Source-to-sink completeness | **STRONG** | All nine states reach equipment, construction, repair, finishing, trade, or storage sinks. Edible, ordinary wood, and suitable fat remain explicit relationships rather than accidental inventory bloat. |
| Repair scarcity | **STRONG** | Repair consumes physically relevant represented materials, does not restore unsafe primary structures cheaply, and coexists with new construction. |
| Player-choice viability | **STRONG** | Never-Hunt, never-Woodcut, buying-heavy, and self-supply-heavy play remain viable through ordinary supply and lawful exchange. |
| Discovery and mastery payoff | **STRONG** | Advanced content must satisfy the nonnumeric payoff contract; practical mastery recombines constraints instead of substituting materials. |
| Resource restraint | **STRONG** | No new equipment material, biological byproduct catalog, generic repair kit, capstone ingredient, or XP-only sink is needed. |
| Skill restraint | **STRONG** | No Farming, Textile, Rendering, Charcoal-making, Fabrication, Adornment, Tracking, or Trapping skill is introduced. Cooking and Fletching are not forced into this chain. |
| Content burden | **STRONG** | Twelve gathering patterns reuse two compact grammars; six ordinary interfaces reuse custody verbs; five condition patterns are capped per instance. |
| Environmental integration | **STRONG** | Eight placement classes connect habitat, agriculture, dirty work, fuel work, receiving, workshops, storage, water, fire, and repair. |
| Map certainty | **PARTIAL BY DESIGN** | Exact sites, routes, river links, travel times, and counts remain **MAP VERIFICATION REQUIRED**. Placement requirements are sufficient for later map review without inventing geometry. |
| Numerical economy balance | **PARTIAL BY DESIGN** | Prices, rates, yields, quantities, stock limits, and linen/tow proportions await balance work. The architecture supplies the levers and sinks needed to test them. |
| Governance detail | **PARTIAL BY DESIGN** | Custodian and stewardship roles are sufficient; exact offices and powers remain deferred and do not block content architecture. |
| Final species and wood catalog | **PARTIAL BY DESIGN** | Ecology and physical eligibility must be selected later. No species name is needed to validate progression or supply logic. |
| Canon and hard guardrails | **STRONG** | No C03/C06/C07 resolution, exact governance, exact map fact, player spellcasting, Stonebound source, Mire source, Volkhari supply, renewable Great War salvage, or live-lore edit appears. |

No category is **THIN**, **MISSING**, **CONTRADICTORY**, or fixably **PARTIAL** at the architecture level after revision.

## Final contradiction sweep

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Does any document make bark a separate activity or random drop? | No. It is guaranteed only when an eligible legitimate Woodcutting action physically produces usable bark. |
| Does any document make raw hide universal or random? | No. It requires an eligible hide-bearing animal and successful recovery. |
| Does any document make the player the sole supplier? | No. Husbandry, forestry, households, processors, trade, and markets remain active. |
| Does ordinary supply erase player skill identity? | No. Player gathering supplies control and knowledge; Leatherworking owns player transformation; Smithing and Leatherworking own equipment work. |
| Does any processor become a hidden skill or minigame? | No. Ordinary conversions are labor, service, routing, or exchange relationships. |
| Does the architecture require a global market or other players? | No. Interfaces and stock are local, authored, and solo viable. |
| Does practical mastery require superior materials? | No. It deepens diagnosis, purpose, constraints, fitting, repair, custody, and route knowledge. |
| Does disruption become continuous simulation? | No. The selected model uses bounded authored states; cadence remains a Creative Director review item. |
| Is exact geography invented? | No. All dependent placement remains marked for map verification. |
| Is ranged content used as a sink? | No. S01 remains deferred and no ranged demand supports the architecture. |

## Production readiness

Later node and content production can now begin without inventing missing economic logic. It has:

- a bounded vocabulary of opportunities and interfaces;
- distinct long-form identities for Hunting and Woodcutting;
- a credible ordinary economy alongside player gathering;
- full source-to-sink relationships for all represented equipment-chain states;
- broad placement and environmental requirements;
- explicit stewardship, disruption, custody, repair, and mastery rules;
- a decision ledger separating Creative Director choices from safe production deferrals;
- no dependency on a new material or player skill.

Creative Director review remains required before this proposal becomes approved design. Numerical balance, exact placement, species, and implementation remain later gates.
