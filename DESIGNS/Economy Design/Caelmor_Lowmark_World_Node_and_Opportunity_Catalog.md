# Caelmor — Lowmark World Node and Opportunity Catalog

**Status:** Proposal-stage world-content foundation after Creative Director decisions D01–D11
**Scope:** Lowmark v1 Hunting and Woodcutting opportunities; no exact nodes, species, placements, balance, or implementation data

## Purpose and authority

This catalog converts the approved Lowmark economy architecture into a bounded set of reusable world-opportunity patterns. It preserves:

- no more than six reusable Hunting archetypes and six reusable Woodcutting archetypes;
- four provisional fauna source relationships and four provisional woodland source relationships;
- one broad tangible ordinary-wood inventory state outside the nine equipment-chain states;
- conditional guaranteed raw hide and tanning bark when physical eligibility and successful recovery support them;
- ordinary supply alongside player gathering;
- authored, legible disruptions used occasionally rather than continuous simulation;
- no species tiers, superior hides, wood grades, bark-only activity, hidden skills, or ranged dependency.

An **archetype** is a reusable interaction grammar. An **opportunity pattern** is a production-facing composition of one or more archetypes with a source relationship, purpose, placement class, and output route. Several world instances may reuse one pattern. Counts below are pattern counts, not node counts.

## Shared world-opportunity contract

Every later Hunting or Woodcutting instance must answer:

1. What ordinary world source makes the opportunity possible?
2. What signs let the player understand its current condition?
3. What action and preparation does the approved skill own?
4. What guaranteed or source-conditional outputs follow successful work?
5. Where can those outputs go after recovery?
6. What stewardship or access rule limits the action?
7. Why does the opportunity remain useful after later patterns unlock?

The common flow is:

```text
world source and current condition
→ readable signs and access state
→ Hunting or Woodcutting action
→ successful recovery
→ represented inventory state or explicit ordinary-labor relationship
→ keep / bank / baseline market trade / contextual delivery / processing
```

Normal lawful transferable goods have a dependable baseline market outlet. Contextual household, civic, specialist, tannery, renderer, and fuel-worker demand can create better purpose or different acceptance, but it is not an unlimited high-value deletion sink.

## Hunting archetype vocabulary

| ID | Reusable archetype | What changes the work |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Guided margin hunt | Basic evidence, bounded approach, clean recovery, and known destination |
| H2 | Pressure-response hunt | Testimony and physical evidence must establish a legitimate crop or household need |
| H3 | Concealed wet-ground hunt | Group alert, cover, footing, timing, and recovery route interact |
| H4 | Movement-window interception | Route knowledge and a temporary ordinary movement pattern matter |
| H5 | Hazard-complicated recovery | Existing terrain or normal danger complicates access or return without creating special quarry |
| H6 | Stewardship inquiry | Conflicting evidence, claims, or closure status changes whether hunting is the right response |

Hunting owns reading, preparation, approach, eligibility, and recovery. A normal combat encounter may resolve dangerous contact when later content uses an already-supported danger, but combat alone never grants Hunting recovery.

## Catalog readiness

The catalog supplies a bounded content vocabulary with real output routes, progression differences, repeat value, and ordinary-economy integration.
