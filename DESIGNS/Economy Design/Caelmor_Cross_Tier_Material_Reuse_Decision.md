# Caelmor Cross-Tier Material Reuse Decision

**Status:** Proposal for Creative Director approval

**Scope:** Lowmark v1 material continuity from early supply through practical mastery, aspirational commissions, repair, and ordinary trade

## 1. Recommended ruling

Approve **selective layered reuse**.

Early source materials remain valuable when they feed a durable processing chain, even if they are not physically placed into late equipment. Processed construction, fuel, lining, damping, fitting, and maintenance goods retain direct late demand only where an approved equipment function, repair need, commission process, or ordinary-world use consumes them.

The governing rule is:

> Preserve the material relationship across progression; require the original inventory state directly only when the later work physically needs that state.

This rejects both disposable tier ladders and universal reuse quotas. It also replaces the legacy question of whether a chosen percentage of band-one materials appears in band-four recipes. The current Lowmark foundation has its own materials, methods, and 99-level progression structure.

## 2. Reuse classifications

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Direct late demand** | The represented material itself can be consumed in late construction, refurbishment, maintenance, civic work, or an ordinary late-market use |
| **Indirect late relevance** | The represented source state remains useful because it converts into a different approved state with direct late demand |
| **Repair demand** | The material is consumed directly by physically justified equipment or civic repair, refurbishment, or maintenance |
| **Commission demand** | The material can be required directly by an aspirational commission because its physical role is present in the approved form |
| **Ordinary-market relevance** | Households, processors, workshops, civic work, or trade provide a use independent of player progression XP |

Direct demand does not mean that every late item uses the material. Commission demand remains conditional on the final physical form and primary craft of each item.

## 4. Direct versus indirect late demand

### Direct late demand

The following materials have a demonstrated reason to be consumed directly after practical mastery:

- **vegetable-tanned leather** for flexible construction and repair;
- **linen textile** for justified lining, joining, wrapping, and repair;
- **flax tow** for justified damping, padding, and renewal, conditional on final equipment forms;
- **ordinary wood** for civic and household repair and fuel-worker supply, rather than automatic aspirational-equipment construction;
- **wood charcoal** for Smithing construction and refurbishment heat;
- **rendered tallow** for justified maintenance and finishing;
- **plain carbon steel stock** for hard construction and refurbishment.

### Indirect-only late relevance

The following source states should not be inserted directly into late equipment recipes:

- **raw hide** remains relevant through conversion into leather;
- **flax bundle** remains relevant through conversion into linen and tow;
- **tannin-rich bark** remains relevant through tanning and therefore through every later leather demand.

Indirect relevance is sufficient. Requiring a raw hide, flax bundle, or loose bark unit alongside its processed output in an aspirational commission would double-charge the same material chain without a physical reason.

## 5. Repair relevance

Repair is the primary recurring cross-tier sink, but it remains damage-specific:

| Repair class | Direct materials | Upstream relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Hard working surface or structure | Plain carbon steel stock and wood charcoal; tallow only for justified care | Imported supply and suitable ordinary wood routed to fuel workers |
| Flexible structure, facing, fastening, or wear part | Vegetable-tanned leather; linen where the construction uses it; tallow for justified conditioning | Raw hide and tannin-rich bark through tanning |
| Lining, seam, wrap, or padding | Linen textile and, where present, flax tow | Flax bundle through textile conversion |
| Household or civic wood repair | Ordinary wood | Managed Woodcutting and ordinary forestry |

Repair must not consume every listed material on every service action. The damaged physical function determines the inputs. Routine care consumes less than structural refurbishment, and workshop repair must not become a disguised requirement to recraft the whole item.

No repair kit, scrap tier, maintenance token, or prestige consumable is needed.

## 6. Aspirational commission relevance

Aspirational equipment continues to use ordinary materials. Its value comes from selection, workmanship, fit, tolerances, matched construction, inspection, custody, and repairability.

| Aspirational path | Direct material continuity | Deliberately excluded forced reuse |
|---|---|---|
| Keeper's Blade | Steel and charcoal; leather, linen, or tallow only if the final form physically requires the relevant control or care function | Raw hide, bark, flax bundle, ordinary wood, and tow are not included merely to connect skills |
| Crestbridge Guard | Steel and charcoal; leather, linen, tow, or tallow only for approved control, retention, damping, lining, or care functions | No mandatory wood core or textile package before final form selection |
| Bridgewarden Harness | Steel and charcoal; leather and justified linen/tow/tallow integration | No requirement that every textile and animal-chain input appear in each commissioned section |
| Riverpath Leathers | Leather with justified linen/tow/tallow integration; steel only for a physically necessary hard fitting | No prestige hide, capstone tanning agent, or mandatory metal merely to increase interconnection |

Documented custody remains an acquisition and legitimacy condition. It is not a material sink.

## 7. Ordinary-market relevance

Early materials should remain tradable because Lowmark's economy uses them, not because an abstract buyer deletes unlimited stock.

- Raw hide and bark meet at tanneries.
- Flax bundles meet textile labor and return as linen and tow.
- Ordinary wood serves repair, public work, households, and fuel workers.
- Charcoal serves ongoing Smithing.
- Leather, linen, tow, and tallow serve workshops and ordinary maintenance.
- Steel stock serves ordinary smithies and repair queues as well as player commissions.

These uses support revisiting early gathering areas. A later player may Hunt for leather supply, food relationships, or sale; Woodcut for ordinary repair demand, bark, or fuel-worker supply. The player is not sent back solely because a late recipe arbitrarily lists an early raw item.

## 8. Oversupply implications

| Material or flow | Risk | Cross-tier finding | Required later control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary wood | **Highest material-flow risk** | Direct civic demand and charcoal conversion help, but they do not justify unlimited output; WM8 reaches about 55.4 wood units/h continuously and about 45.9/h in sustainable post-mastery play | Tune real repair, civic, household, fuel-worker, and storage demand; do not add filler recipes |
| Raw hide | **High** | Late leather demand preserves chain value, but HM2/HM5 can produce substantial hide supply and husbandry also supplies it | Balance tannery capacity, leather construction/repair demand, spoilage boundaries, and ordinary buyers |
| Tannin-rich bark | **High and coupled to hide** | Tanning is a legitimate persistent sink; direct late recipe use would only duplicate that role | Keep bark eligibility and tanning ratio aligned with hide flow; never create bark-only grind |
| Flax tow | **Surplus watch** | Its direct late role exists only where damping or padding is real | Tune linen/tow conversion proportions and actual form demand; do not invent padding recipes to consume surplus |
| Edible Hunting flow | **High but outside this material decision** | Cross-tier equipment reuse cannot solve HM8 food pressure | Cooking, preservation, household demand, and sale relationships require their own design pass |
| Steel stock | **Scarcity watch** | Direct late construction and refurbishment provide strong demand against imported supply | Tune import availability and workshop priority rather than creating local ore tiers |
| Leather, linen, charcoal, tallow | **Manageable pending quantities** | Construction, repair, and ordinary work provide legitimate recurring uses | Validate recipe quantities, repair frequency, and ordinary supply during later balancing |

Cross-tier reuse is not an excuse to absorb excess gathering output. When supply exceeds legitimate demand, adjust source throughput, conversion, ordinary demand, storage, or pricing during balance work.

## 9. Missing legitimate sinks

No missing sink blocks this decision, but three relationships require later quantitative validation:

1. **Ordinary wood:** its civic, household, repair, and charcoal demands must be specified strongly enough to support the approved Woodcutting throughput without becoming an unlimited buyer.
2. **Flax tow:** final equipment forms must prove how much padding or damping is used. If demand is small, conversion proportions or ordinary production volume should change; no filler sink should be added.
3. **Edible Hunting output:** the largest Hunting output needs Cooking and ordinary-consumption architecture. It is not solved by equipment material reuse.

These are balance and adjacent-economy questions, not reasons to make every early material a late commission input.

## 10. Early-area revisit test

| Player purpose | Early Hunting areas | Managed woodland areas | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-supply | Hide can become leather; edible relationships remain useful | Bark supports tanning; wood supports repair or charcoal | Meaningful without universal late recipes |
| Trade | Tanners, renderers, food relationships, and ordinary buyers create several destinations | Tanners, fuel workers, civic repair, storage, and ordinary sale create several destinations | Relationship density supports choice |
| Repair preparation | Hide supports later leather repair indirectly | Wood supports civic repair directly and hard repair indirectly through charcoal | Early sources feed recurring late maintenance |
| Commission preparation | Hide becomes commission-grade leather through processing and selection | Bark enables leather; suitable wood can feed commission Smithing through charcoal | Commission demand reaches backward without listing every raw state |
| Mastery routine | Later knowledge improves opportunity selection, recovery, stewardship, and routing | Later knowledge improves source choice, custody, public purpose, and route integration | Revisit value comes from method mastery as well as goods |

**Result:** early gathering areas remain worth revisiting. Their value comes from multiple destinations and mature methods, not from forcing every early material directly into every late recipe.

## 11. Rejected alternatives

### Universal direct reuse

Rejected. It would force raw hide, flax bundles, bark, or ordinary wood into late equipment where processed goods already perform the physical job. It would create recipe clutter and convert relationship density into checklist complexity.

### Disposable early materials

Rejected. Replacing steel, leather, textile, fuel, or maintenance goods with prestige tiers would undermine the approved ordinary-material aspirational identity and make early production obsolete.

### Fixed reuse quota

Rejected. A target such as seven of ten directly reused materials treats catalog arithmetic as design authority. Physical construction and economic purpose determine reuse.

### Repair as an unlimited sink

Rejected. Repair remains recurring but bounded by actual wear, damage class, maintenance choices, and workshop access. Artificial breakage would turn reuse into a chore loop.

## 12. Creative Director decision required

Approve or revise this ruling:

> Caelmor uses selective layered reuse. Ordinary processed construction, fuel, textile, and maintenance materials remain directly relevant through late construction, commissions, repair, civic work, and ordinary demand where their physical functions apply. Raw source states may remain indirectly relevant through processing. No material is forced into a late recipe to satisfy a reuse quota.

Approval would close the cross-tier reuse-strength decision. It would not set recipe quantities, repair frequency, market prices, final equipment forms, or the broader catalog ceiling.
