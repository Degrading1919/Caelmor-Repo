# Caelmor Cross-Region Source / Process Dependency Summary

**Status:** proposal-stage synthesis for Creative Director review. This document connects the four regional foundation briefs without approving materials, goods, recipes, nodes, tiers, schemas, routes, volumes, prices, or monopolies. It does not amend live canon.

## Purpose

Parts 2–4 establish that the regions depended on one another before the Great War and remained connected through work, food, passage, care, migration, repair, and disputed obligations. The bounded source/process pass adds only enough physical and labor logic to make those dependencies usable in later design.

The core result is intentional unevenness:

- **Lowmark** is strongest in food, markets, waterways, household production, and repair at modest scale.
- **Thornfell** is strongest in mountain access, stone, metal, structural work, weather protection, and guarded stewardship.
- **The Mire** is strongest in wet-season adaptation, reeds, preservation, repair, route knowledge, care, and custody, while depending heavily on imported durable inputs.
- **Emberholt** is strongest in high-heat transformation, finishing, industrial labor, glass and forge practice, and inspection, while depending on outside food, timber, fibers, hides, and part of its conventional mineral supply.

No region is reduced to a single export, and no dependency establishes an exact source or route.

## Carrier and custody model

A credible later material proposal should identify at least four different roles where relevant:

1. **Source custodian:** household, landholder, work group, local steward, mine or quarry crew, or outside supplier able to allow or refuse taking.
2. **Processor:** worker or workshop responsible for making a source physically usable and containing its waste.
3. **Carrier or market intermediary:** person or institution responsible for condition and records while the material moves, without implying one continent-wide company.
4. **Receiving custodian:** household, guild, civic works, shelter, care site, or repair shop that checks condition and determines use.

One record at one stage cannot prove the whole chain. A dispatch can be honest while a delivery fails; an official receipt can be complete while households remain undersupplied; a repaired object can have uncertain original provenance.

## Dependency constraints

1. **No exclusive source by implication.** A region's strong precedent does not prove that all other regions buy from it.
2. **No exact route by convenience.** The map does not authorize precise roads, river connections, travel time, or shipment order.
3. **No stable volume by historical contact.** A marriage, relief movement, or old contract does not prove present bulk trade.
4. **No material property from cultural meaning.** Mourning, repair, color, marks, or “spirit” do not make matter magical.
5. **No supernatural provenance.** Dreams, Stonebound responses, earth-memory manifestations, and resonance effects cannot identify source, maker, owner, event, or safe process.
6. **No extraction from atrocity by default.** Glassed Hollow, battlefield remains, sealed workings, and memorial ground are evidence and hazard contexts. Any later recovery requires separate custody and safety approval.
7. **No Volkhari supply chain under C06.** Volkhari bodies, inherited practice, ash, or hidden communities cannot be treated as industrial inputs.
8. **No Founder craft lineage under C07.** A useful method cannot be attributed to a Founder or rediscoverer without later selection.
9. **No precursor monopoly under C03.** Ordinary industry and trade do not establish the precursor's name, personnel, or later Remnant continuity.
10. **No system implication.** These relationships do not set gatherable nodes, recipes, progression, prices, skill scope, or v1 access.

## Readiness consequence

The four briefs now provide plausible reasons for materials and craft traditions to exist or remain absent. Later designers can begin with a desired practical or cultural function, identify a conventional source class, locate the necessary labor and process, and test its trade and scarcity against this dependency web. They should still seek Creative Director approval for every actual material or craft selection.
