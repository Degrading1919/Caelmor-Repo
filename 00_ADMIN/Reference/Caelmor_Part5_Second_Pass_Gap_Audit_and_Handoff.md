## 3. Corrections made by the second pass

The first synthesis exposed several risks that have been bounded in the final document set:

1. **Five Lowmark framework anchors could have become competing main arcs.** The architecture assigns each a limited function inside one water-centered arc.
2. **Later-region evidence could have weakened the v1 ending.** Every major callback now states the complete local meaning and resolution that exists before later reinterpretation.
3. **The side-story count could have become a volume target.** The lattice is a candidate pool; production must select for pressure, location, character load, and structural variety.
4. **Optional discoveries and micro-events could have doubled scope.** The two lists are one shared selection pool where they overlap.
5. **Recurring anchors could have acquired unsupported biographies.** The story web limits existing people to established roles, custody, observations, and beliefs, and uses unnamed role-based households where canon supplies no safe identity.
6. **Mystery could have become indefinite ambiguity.** Each substantial thread has an evidence owner and a civic or emotional payoff. Facts the Creative Director has not selected are labelled rather than filled by invention.
7. **Magic could have replaced investigation.** Resonance supplies effects and limits; people, records, workmanship, and accountable decisions supply historical proof.

No additional expansion was required after these controls. The remaining questions affect later selection or canon reconciliation, not the structural completeness of Part 5.

## 5. Known source corrections for a later canon pass

These do not block Part 5, but they should not be forgotten when proposal material is promoted:

- Older Phase 1 shorthand saying magic was universally outlawed must be reconciled with C12's regional, uneven restrictions.
- Any stale character language implying a speaking or vision-giving Stonebound, a Volkhari Fire Titan, captive Kaelith, or literal Mire ghosts must not survive canon reconciliation.
- The Part 3 name slip **Kaldren Stone-Echo** should be corrected to the established **Kaldren Deepdelver** when that source is next revised.
- Exact routes, travel times, settlement placement, and interregional meetings still require map and custody verification before final quest implementation.

## 6. Part 5 completion gate

**Result: PASS at proposal-architecture level.**

- Lowmark has one coherent main-story architecture with a satisfying local ending.
- Side stories represent distinct human and systemic pressures.
- Recurring people can change meaning through evidence without all becoming main-plot figures.
- Long mysteries have controlled truth, evidence ownership, and planned payoffs.
- Future-region callbacks deepen earlier observations while preserving v1's truth and emotional closure.
- Optional discoveries remain optional.
- The Remnant and supernatural phenomena stay bounded.
- No major Part 5 category remains **Thin, Missing, Contradictory, or Gated**.

This pass does **not** declare Lore Foundation Lock or material-culture readiness. It confirms only that the narrative lattice is deep and coherent enough to proceed to Creative Director selection and later production planning.

## 7. Recommended Part 6 focus

Part 6 should be a controlled **selection and canon-integration pass**, subject to a separate brief and Creative Director approval:

1. decide C03, C06, C07, C09, and C16 where the selected content needs them;
2. select the exact twelve main-arc movements, participating anchors, and Lowmark locations without scripting dialogue;
3. select a balanced v1 side-story and micro-event slate from the candidate pools;
4. assign claim cards for every chosen fact: authority, evidence owner, reveal owner, map dependency, and canon destination;
5. reconcile outdated canon wording and the known name slip through a reviewed source-by-source change plan;
6. verify selected narrative placements against the map and character custody ledgers;
7. establish a production mystery bible that preserves author truth and controls later reveals.

Reward, equipment, resource, recipe, and final quest implementation work should remain outside that selection pass unless separately authorized.
