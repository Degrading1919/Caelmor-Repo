# Caelmor Part 4 — map and route plausibility audit

**Status:** proposal-stage verification note for Creative Director review. It authorizes no map, road, travel time, settlement, or canon change. It tests the Part 4 relationship web against `LORE/Regions/Caelmor_Map_Concept.png`, the four detailed regional dossiers, corrected `LORE/Factions/Caelmor_Factions.md`, the Part 2 proposals, and the Part 3 continuity map. Director rulings through C15 govern conflicts.

## What the available map can establish

The image is a **concept map**, without scale, distance key, road key, dates, or a confirmed directional flow for its rivers. It depicts Thornfell broadly in the northwest, Emberholt in the northeast, the Mire in the southwest, and Lowmark in the southeast. Stonewake and Crestbridge are mapped labels also supported by dossiers. Reedcross and Bonebank appear as map labels but are not established as playable or administratively defined settlements merely by appearing there. No exact route, crossing, jurisdictional border, water flow, or journey duration follows from their positions.

The dossiers independently establish Lowmark barges and Crestbridge as a major bridge/trade artery (`Caelmor_Region1_LowmarkVale.md`, II, IV–V); Thornfell high passes, Windscar Pass as an east–west artery, Shatterfall Gorge, bridge and horn work (`Caelmor_Region2_ThornfellMarches.md`, II, V–VI); Mire shifting channels and Mirebone Crossing (`Caelmor_Region3_MireOfFallenBanners.md`, VI); and Emberholt's trade outward through merchants and guilds (`Caelmor_Factions.md`, Emberholt merchant and regional-relations sections). These make exchange credible. They do not identify a single continuous road linking the named sites.

**Custody is easier to support than itinerary.** A letter, ledger copy, memorial notice, or account can be held in another region through ordinary exchange without asserting that two named people met or that one road was open. A person-to-person movement needs a host, work, kin, relief, or trade reason and a plausible but deliberately unspecified passage until a map pass confirms it.

## Geographic tensions and source limits

1. **Eastern-front wording:** the Thornfell dossier calls the Marches an eastern barrier and describes eastern approaches, while the concept image places Thornfell northwest of Emberholt. The Mire dossier also says it buffered Lowmark and Thornfell from eastern incursions despite its southwest image position. These may refer to a particular campaign approach, local orientation, or an earlier map, but Part 4 cannot silently pick one. Use “high-route pressure,” “Mire relief,” or “regional movement” without fixing an invasion vector. This is a map/campaign reconciliation matter, not a blocker for civilian exchange.
2. **Rivers and ash:** Lowmark lore says its rivers carried debris and emotional aftermath from other regions; the concept image shows waterways but supplies no flow arrows or connected navigability. A floating object in Lowmark therefore cannot be assigned an Emberholt or Mire origin solely because it appears downstream on the image. Require a witnessed transfer or record if origin matters.
3. **Mire channels:** C14 makes Rowan Mirewake the only known living guide who reliably knows the safe shifting-channel approach to the single Chapel of the Sinking Star/Sunken Chapel/Sunken Church. A general Mire crossing, ferryman, or relief route does not confer that chapel access. No proposed Part 4 civilian route uses the chapel as an automatic shortcut.
4. **Stonebound geography:** C11 confirms a node at Echochamber Summit and a rare Echohewn coupling. It does not map further nodes or permit a “Stonebound route” between Thornfell and Emberholt. The node may respond physically or resonantly to disturbance but cannot identify a traveler or historical event.
5. **Site status:** proposed Part 3 sites such as Drowsebank, Nine Stakes, Bridge of Seven Repairs, and Half-Name Wall remain location options, not points with approved coordinates. The Part 4 evidence ledgers should be readable even if these names are later moved or rejected.

## Route claim standard for later selection

A selected movement must have (a) a source-supported reason and sending/receiving institutions or households; (b) a documented custody or witness chain at each end; (c) a statement of what could be carried by intermediary rather than direct travel; and (d) a map check before naming intermediate stops, season-specific passability, or duration. A repeated mark, stitch, verse, or meal form cannot substitute for this chain. The Part 4 documents may propose **that** an exchange occurred and **why** it mattered while leaving **how far**, **how long**, and **which exact path** open.

**Audit result:** all six broad relationships are plausible at the level of trade, kin, care, diplomacy, and record exchange. Exact route geometry and the two directional tensions remain unverified. They must be resolved before a specific itinerary or campaign map becomes canon, but they do not require a Creative Director decision to develop Part 4's evidence-bounded social web.
