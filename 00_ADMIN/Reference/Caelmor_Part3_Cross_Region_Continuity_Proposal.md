## Continuity rule

The four regions share food, passage, care, craft, and war memory without sharing one culture or one correct archive. Each proposed cross-region thread below has a local problem that can end where it began, a second perspective held elsewhere, and a changed interpretation. A repeated mark or story is insufficient evidence on its own. The worker responsible for a later reveal must identify a person, record, or place that can corroborate it.

**Directed boundaries:** the Great War lasted 5–10 years and ended about 30 years ago; Dreamers emerged only after it; the wartime precursor later became the formal Remnant; a precursor initiate became the Titan; Kaelith escaped and remains free; Thalan is the sole current captive; Founders are teachers distinct from rediscoverers; “spirit” names living/emotional imprints interacting with elemental resonance and is no fifth discipline; postwar authorities broadly rejected weaponization, amplification, uncontrolled ritual experiments, and dangerous research, but no center enforced a universal magic ban. Current Emberholt civic firecraft and Lowmark farm water spells therefore remain possible. Thornfell acoustics are mostly natural with rare dormant magic; Mire threats are capped at two major ones with minor anomalies allowed. Stage A C01–C15 and Part 2 C08/C12 updates.

**Geographic caution:** `LORE/Regions/Caelmor_Map_Concept.png` is a concept image that places the four named regions broadly; it does not verify travel times, exact roads, hydrology, or that any proposed convoy used a particular route. The detailed dossiers support regional trade, pass control, river movement, and Mire channels. All more precise route links below are proposed and need a map pass before canon or quest placement. Reedcross and Bonebank are labels on the concept image, not automatically approved settlements.

## Shared-history braid without a master conspiracy

1. **Before war:** crossings, passes, safe marsh ground, and volcanic civic work make recurring exchange useful. Part 2 proposes local passage bargains, not a vanished continent-wide treaty or empire. The same practical act can be recorded as a noble favor, clan obligation, chapel care, or guild service.
2. **During war:** pressure on routes moves food, wounded people, soldiers, and families through overlapping systems. The proposed Shatterfall denial, Ashen Flats scarcity, Mire relief movement, and Glassed Hollow catastrophe each create a different kind of omission. Fear, triage, damaged paper, local self-protection, and precursor concealment can all produce missing records; select a cause per story.
3. **After war:** no durable central magic law or victory history imposes one account. Local repair institutions gain authority while later Remnant branches shape Emberholt's public story. A thirty-year span permits living witnesses, adult children who remember evacuation, and younger custodians who know the war only through work.
4. **Present:** Dreamers, household legends, environmental imprints, and altered archives can reopen a question but cannot prove it without independent evidence. A future return to the same place should change understanding of a person or duty, not merely reveal that the Remnant touched everything.

## Handoff conditions for a later canon or story pass

- Confirm actual geography, travel time, and source status of map labels before assigning route order or placing new landmarks.
- Obtain the Director's C11 and C14/C15 choices before using summit Stonebound or Mire chapel/threat truth. C06, C07, C03, and C09 remain narrower detail gates; do not invent a population, Founder roster, precursor name, or local farm-water code here.
- For each selected callback, write a claim card: local human stake, source anchor, initial observation, two independent evidence channels, later changed reading, knowledge held by each person, release scope, and facts still unproved.
- Preserve region-specific causes. A Mire rite need not originate in the war; a Thornfell echo need not be magic; an Emberholt archive gap need not be a conspiracy; a Lowmark ledger need not be honest simply because it is precise.
- This document claims neither Lore Foundation Lock nor material-culture readiness and authorizes no equipment/resource design.
