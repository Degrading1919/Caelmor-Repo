# Caelmor Regional Source / Process — Second-Pass Readiness Audit

**Status:** proposal-stage completion audit. It tests whether the bounded regional source/process pass closes the specific lore gaps identified in Part 6. It does not approve any proposal as canon, approve a material catalog, or declare Lore Foundation Lock.

## Audit standard

Each required foundation is rated:

- **SUPPORTED FOR RERUN:** the proposal identifies a plausible source or import dependency, gatherer or importer, processor, broad process, fuel or water burden, waste or risk, stewardship or access, scarcity, cultural meaning, and broad cross-region dependency.
- **PARTIAL:** one or more of those fields remains too weak for the Lore Foundation Lock readiness test.
- **CONTRADICTORY:** the proposal conflicts with reconciled canon or a Creative Director ruling.
- **OUT OF SCOPE BY DESIGN:** the question belongs to actual material, craft, economy, map, legal, or system selection and should remain open.

A region does not fail because it imports a material or lacks a local industry. A deliberate import dependency passes when the reason, users, maintenance, risks, and broad relationship are coherent.

## Contradiction and drift sweep

- **C03 preserved:** the wartime precursor's name, exact transition, and personnel continuity are not used to justify any industry.
- **C06 preserved:** Volkhari population and community form remain unresolved; no Volkhari supply chain exists.
- **C07 preserved:** no Founder, rediscoverer, First Listener, or teaching chain is assigned a material tradition.
- **C08 preserved:** “spirit” remains living and emotional imprint interacting with elemental resonance, never a material class or fifth discipline.
- **C09 preserved:** Lowmark water practice remains layered and does not become a player system.
- **C11 preserved:** Stonebound responses remain passive and non-testimonial.
- **C12 preserved:** regional restraint replaces the false universal-ban account; Emberholt ordinary work remains lawful.
- **C14/C15 preserved:** the Mire has one chapel and exactly two major non-undead threats.
- **Chronology preserved:** the Great War lasted 5–10 years and ended about thirty years ago, and Great War terminology is used consistently.
- **Map uncertainty preserved:** all dependencies remain route classes or broad relationships.

No new contradiction requiring a Creative Director ruling was found within this bounded pass.
