## Audit standard

- **Developed proposal:** several connected cases have a local cause, people or institution that carried it, another region's partial account, a disagreement, independent possible evidence, and a local owner of the consequence. It is substantial enough for Director selection, not canon.
- **Partial detail:** a chosen case still needs a name, age, exact record, local rule, or map placement before canon or scene design. The broad relationship can be evaluated without it.
- **Thin/Missing/Contradictory/Gated:** the major connection lacks a causal chain, depends on a motif or unsupported coincidence, conflicts with directed truth, or requires a Director decision before any responsible proposal can be made. This rating fails the Part 4 completion gate.

The test is causal and evidentiary, not numerical. A dozen similar deliveries would still be thin if no one knew who handed them off or why the accounts differed.

## First synthesis and targeted second pass

The initial synthesis produced twelve pairwise historical braids, six pairwise civilian chains, eight shared-event chains, a source-limited character ledger, nine conditional cultural-callback candidates, and a six-pair map audit. It established everyday trade, care, route work, and institutional obligations alongside war damage. Two material weaknesses remained:

1. **Marriage, fosterage, adoption, and household change were Partial.** Generic migration did not yet show enough durable family consequences. A targeted Terra pass added five different household arcs: a prewar Lowmark–Thornfell marriage/work tie; Thornfell–Mire fosterage after separation; Lowmark–Mire postwar elder-care return; Lowmark–Emberholt non-war service dependence; and a Mire–Emberholt host-family/work-care return. Each has separate custodians, a disagreement, corroboration requirement, present consequence, and no invented named-anchor kinship. Formal adoption status is deliberately unselected until local law is known.
2. **The shared-event ledger over-weighted the Great War.** A targeted Terra pass added three later-postwar civilian events: Lowmark–Mire household placement, Thornfell–Emberholt civic repair labor, and Mire–Emberholt convalescence/return care. Their evidence paths do not require Remnant action, supernatural testimony, or one continent-wide organization.

The Sol pass also caught a **source-name slip**: the existing Thornfell character anchor and detailed Part 3 regional proposal call the Echohewn keeper *Kaldren Deepdelver*, while the Part 3 cross-region proposal calls him “Kaldren Stone-Echo” once. The Part 4 character ledger uses Deepdelver and flags the Part 3 wording for later approved correction. It does not create a second person or change a Part 3 file in this PR.
