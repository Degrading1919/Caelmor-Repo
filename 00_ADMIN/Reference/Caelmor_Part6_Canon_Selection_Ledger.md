# Caelmor Part 6 — Canon Selection Ledger

**Status:** recommendation ledger for Creative Director review. “Recommend canon” means suitable for approval and later integration; it does not make the entry canon. Classification applies to the stated cluster, not automatically to every example or name within its source document.

## Classification key

- **RECOMMEND CANON:** coherent, useful, and sufficiently bounded for Director approval.
- **RECOMMEND REVISE:** preserve the function but narrow, reconcile, or verify details first.
- **RETAIN AS OPTIONAL:** useful candidate material that should not all become simultaneously true.
- **RETAIN AS IN-WORLD BELIEF:** valuable as a partial, mistaken, political, devotional, or disputed account.
- **REJECT / SUPERSEDED:** conflicts with a Director ruling, current scope, or the selected world model.

## Rejected or superseded claims

- Great War dating roughly 160–150 years before present, century-long Great War language, or a centuries-long single conflict.
- Dreamers existing before the Great War or causing magical rediscovery.
- A formal Remnant conducting wartime operations before its postwar formation; those actions belong to its unnamed precursor.
- A Volkhari Fire Titan; the Titan was a precursor/Remnant initiate.
- Captive Kaelith; Kaelith escaped and remains free.
- Spirit as a fifth element, school, or Founder lineage.
- A durable universal postwar ban on all magic.
- A speaking, prophetic, question-answering, champion-selecting, or soldier-like Stonebound.
- Multiple Mire chapel sites created from the three established names.
- More than two major Mire supernatural threats, literal undead, or memory manifestations treated as surviving souls.
- Non-Lowmark “v1” region labels and any implied v1 player fire-spell training.
- Legacy equipment/resource lists treated as approved catalogs merely because they appear in old dossiers.

## Selection conclusion

The strongest path is selective approval: canonize the causal backbone, regional obligations, directed supernatural limits, evidence controls, and a small number of verified relationship chains. Preserve alternatives as options or beliefs. Reject stale factual conflicts. This keeps Caelmor deep without turning every proposal into simultaneous history.
