# Caelmor Part 4 — Character and family continuity ledger

**Status:** proposal-status continuity control. This ledger does not amend canon, assign a quest, or make a proposed connection true. It records what present character anchors can safely carry across regional history. Authority is Phase 1 canon, the detailed regional dossiers, current factions, Creative Director rulings through C15, and the Part 2/3 proposal set. Where an old anchor conflicts with a Director ruling, the ruling is the editorial truth and the anchor can only be used later as an explicitly marked belief.

## Reading rules

- The Great War lasted 5–10 years and ended about 30 years ago. A present adult **may** be a witness; nobody is made one from a title, scar, or grief alone. Exact age is withheld unless a source supplies it.
- “Witness” below means that the source directly puts the person in a war-era activity. “Inherited” means it supplies a record, craft, or remembered account without placing them there. “Unstated” preserves uncertainty.
- “Knows” means defensible direct operational knowledge or record custody. “Believes/repeats” means a claim the source frames as interpretation, folklore, or an unverified personal impression. Neither earth-memory, a Dreamer fragment, nor a Stonebound response can certify historical identity.
- The Emberholt character list has broad, older story premises. Claims in it remain anchor text, not automatic cross-region facts. In particular, no individual is given a new journey, family tie, or access to another region merely because their role could permit one.

## Contradiction and future-correction queue

1. **C01 / chronology:** old timeline and some regional text place the war far earlier. This ledger follows the Director’s ~30-year result and treats any conflicting character premise as source text awaiting coordinated canon reconciliation, not an alternative present truth. Stage A C01.
2. **C03 / organization name:** use “wartime precursor” for war-era culpability unless a quoted source is being identified as obsolete. The later formal Remnant is directed; its early name and membership continuity are open. Stage A C03; Part 2 backbone, “Managed Memory.”
3. **C04–C06 / Volkhari:** the Titan was an initiate, Kaelith is free, Thalan is sole current captive, and survivor population is unselected. Legacy character material that contradicts these requires explicit belief labelling or correction in a future canon pass. Stage A C04–C06.
4. **C11 / Stonebound:** remove any proposed use of an Emberholt “memory wall,” gate, voice, or Summit response as a speaker, vision source, historical archive, or actor. Stonebound are passive, place-bound responders; Echochamber Summit’s rare Echohewn/node coupling has physical or resonant effects only. Part 3 Thornfell proposal, directed C11.
5. **C14–C15 / Mire:** chapel labels are one location, and the General Who Wouldn’t Fall and Sorrowback are the only major supernatural threats. Chapel Shades, Banner Wraiths, and similar phenomena are local memory impressions; none are literal dead. Part 3 Mire proposal:12.
6. **Thornfell anchor name / Part 3 slip:** the character anchor and detailed Part 3 Thornfell proposal call the Echohewn keeper **Kaldren Deepdelver** (`LORE/Characters/Caelmor_Characters_Region2_Thornfell_Anchors (Not Full List).txt`, III; `Caelmor_Part3_Thornfell_Proposal.md`, Echohewn section). The Part 3 cross-region continuity map once calls him “Kaldren Stone-Echo.” Treat that as an editorial error, not a second person or alias, pending a later approved correction to that Part 3 document. Part 4 uses Deepdelver.

## Coverage result

The ledger identifies continuity-safe custodians in every region and keeps their cross-region role evidentiary rather than coincidental. It leaves kinship, fosterage, adoption, marriage, exact ages, travel itineraries, population, and direct meetings open until a later proposal can attach each claim to two sources and a map-safe route. It claims neither Lore Foundation Lock nor material-culture readiness.
