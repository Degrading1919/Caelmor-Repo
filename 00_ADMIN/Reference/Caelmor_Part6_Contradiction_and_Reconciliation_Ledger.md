## Source-by-source reconciliation order

1. **Decision sheet:** approve or reject the recommended Part 2–5 clusters and settle only open facts needed by selected material.
2. **Timeline and War History:** establish dates, precursor/formal Remnant sequence, war events, Glassed Hollow, and recovery.
3. **Factions:** align membership, formation, magic framework, Volkhari/Stonebound facts, and current roles.
4. **Detailed regional dossiers:** apply regional law, site identity, threat hierarchy, institutional selection, and chosen ordinary-life/history additions.
5. **Merged overview:** rebuild from reconciled detailed sources rather than editing it as an independent authority.
6. **Character anchors:** correct age/service implications, Kaelith/Thalan/Titan claims, Stonebound behavior, and evidence access.
7. **Phase 1 scope files:** resolve S01 and retire stale non-Lowmark v1 or player-magic implications without changing world lore.
8. **Reference manifest and status labels:** identify canon, approved proposal, optional seed, belief, and superseded material explicitly.

This order prevents a correction in one file from creating a fresh contradiction elsewhere.
