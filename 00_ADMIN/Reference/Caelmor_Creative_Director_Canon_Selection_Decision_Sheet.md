# Caelmor — Creative Director Canon Selection Decision Sheet

**Status:** proposal-stage decision aid. This sheet condenses `Caelmor_Part6_Canon_Selection_Ledger.md`. It does not make any choice, promote any proposal to canon, or begin canon-file reconciliation.

## How to use this sheet

Decisions **D01–D09** are the only decisions requested now. They approve bounded clusters and do not automatically approve every example, named custom, household, landmark, event, or callback in Parts 2–5.

## 1. RECOMMEND APPROVE

### D01 — Historical backbone

- **Recommended choice:** Approve the Part 2 relative chronology and causal framework: an ancient world known through partial practical remains; four increasingly interdependent regions; a 5–10-year Great War ending about thirty years ago; multiple human and institutional causes; and thirty years of uneven recovery.
- **Consequence:** Timeline and War History can be rebuilt around one causal sequence while ancient dates and the exact campaign calendar remain uncertain. The proposed seven-year campaign spine and individual civilian-event dossiers remain optional until separately selected.

### D02 — Magic and supernatural framework

- **Recommended choice:** Approve the four-tradition framework and integrate the existing Director rulings: C08 spirit, postwar Dreamers, C11 passive Stonebound limits, C12 regional postwar restrictions, C14's single Mire chapel, and C15's two non-undead major Mire threats.
- **Consequence:** Magic can influence places and investigation without becoming omniscient evidence. Old fifth-element, universal-ban, speaking-Stonebound, multiple-chapel, and literal-undead wording can be removed or explicitly retained only as belief.

### D03 — Four regional identities

- **Recommended choice:** Approve each region's central civic obligation and ordinary-life foundation: Lowmark through shared water, food, crossing, and repair; Thornfell through access, shelter, pass safety, and collective survival; the Mire through testimony, uncertain ground, route care, and memorial duty; Emberholt through useful heat, craft labor, risk, and public accountability.
- **Consequence:** Regional dossiers can gain a consistent social core without canonizing every proposed custom or local case. Exact office powers and procedures remain future details.

### D04 — Cross-region relationship principles

- **Recommended choice:** Approve a connected world in which every regional pair has at least one verified nonwar tie through work, trade, migration, refuge, care, kinship, or repair. Approve the two-custodian evidence rule and the requirement that cultural borrowing have a carrier, chronology, and independent corroboration.
- **Consequence:** Cross-region history will rest on ordinary causes and traceable evidence. The twelve historical braids, household examples, and callback candidates remain a selection pool pending map and custody checks.

### D05 — Lowmark v1 narrative foundation

- **Recommended choice:** Approve one Lowmark main arc using the Part 5 causal structure: present local decisions concentrate an older bounded water/resonance problem; the player resolves the local civic danger through evidence, repair, and accountability; the Last Field-Mage/Riverwarden layer deepens the history without granting a v1 spellcasting power fantasy.
- **Consequence:** Lowmark gains a complete local story foundation while exact responsible actors, sites, movement expression, side-story slate, and future-region callbacks remain production selections.

### D06 — Continuity and mystery controls

- **Recommended choice:** Approve the Part 4–5 control methods: distinguish personal knowledge from belief, verify record/place custody, track direct witness versus inherited account, and define author truth, public belief, observable evidence, plausible wrong readings, withheld truth, and reveal ownership for substantial mysteries.
- **Consequence:** Later canon and narrative work can preserve delayed understanding without giving characters unsupported biographies or allowing supernatural phenomena to settle history.

### D07 — Material-culture derivation boundary

- **Recommended choice:** Approve the Part 6 direction rule for future lore and economy work: desired cultural or practical function → component/material → processing/gathering → world source. Authorize a later bounded lore pass to establish missing source ecology/geology, labor, stewardship, transformation, waste/risk, scarcity, and broad exchange before any catalogs are designed.
- **Consequence:** Future materials and crafts must grow from approved regional life. Legacy resource/equipment lists remain reference material and cannot become canon catalogs merely because they appear in old dossiers.

## 2. NEEDS CREATIVE DIRECTOR CHOICE

### D08 — C09: Lowmark farm-water practice

- **Recommended choice:** Approve a layered model: small observable practice is household and neighbor-taught; shared water works use council/commons rolls and experienced teachers; Fenmere charter oversight applies only to larger irrigation works, with ordinary household practice below that threshold.
- **Consequence:** Lowmark's simple farming water spells become public, bounded work with visible access and class tensions. Combat water magic remains solely the Last Field-Mage's inheritance, and no player spell system is implied.
- **Useful alternatives:** Choose household custom alone for a more informal and exclusion-prone system, or commons stewardship alone for a more public system with stronger gatekeeping by established households.

### D09 — C16: “Long War” terminology

- **Recommended choice:** Retire **Long War** as stale wording and use **Great War** consistently.
- **Consequence:** Canon reconciliation gains one factual name and avoids suggesting a second conflict.
- **Useful alternative:** Retain **Long War** only as an explicitly attributed in-world epithet for the same Great War.

### C03 — Wartime precursor and formal Remnant

- **Recommended choice:** Defer the precursor's name, exact transition date, and personnel continuity. Use **wartime precursor** for war-era actions and **Remnant** only after formal postwar formation.
- **Consequence:** Historical files can be reconciled now without manufacturing an organization name or silently backdating the Remnant.
- **Decision timing:** Safe to defer until selected Emberholt or Remnant content needs a proper name or precise institutional history.

### C06 — Volkhari survivor population

- **Recommended choice:** Defer any exact population. Canonize only the already directed individual facts and the existence of surviving Volkhari without stating their number or political form.
- **Consequence:** Volkhari people and testimony remain possible without creating an unsupported demographic, enclave, or material-supply network.
- **Decision timing:** Safe to defer until an Emberholt/Volkhari story or community-level material tradition enters selection.

### C07 — Founder roster and rediscoverer identities

- **Recommended choice:** Keep the role distinction as canon and defer the roster, count, dates, names, and teacher-student links.
- **Consequence:** Origins can be reconciled without converting simplified regional hero stories into factual biographies.
- **Decision timing:** Safe to defer until a selected historical or future-region story requires a specific Founder or rediscoverer.

## 3. RETAIN OPTIONAL / IN-WORLD BELIEF

### Retain as optional proposal pools

- The seven-year campaign spine and individual Part 2 civilian events.
- Named regional customs, festivals, clinics, minor landmarks, hearings, households, and delayed-understanding cases beyond the approved regional principles.
- The twelve Part 4 historical braids, proposed marriage/fosterage/care cases, and nine cultural-borrowing candidates.
- The Part 5 side-story slate, role-based households, long-form mystery candidates, cross-region callbacks, optional discoveries, and future-region continuation briefs.

These remain available for later selection. None should be imported by count or treated as simultaneously canon.

### Retain only as attributed in-world belief

- “Magic was outlawed” as shorthand for uneven postwar restrictions.
- The Summit's “Stonebound guardian” as a cultural interpretation of node-coupled resonance.
- The General Who Wouldn't Fall as an actual returned commander.
- Chapel Shades or Banner Wraiths as souls.
- One heroic Founder personally rediscovering and teaching an element.
- Glassed Hollow as an ordinary forge collapse.

These beliefs may shape people and institutions, but they are not author truth.

## 4. REJECT / SUPERSEDED

- Great War dating roughly 160–150 years before the present, a century-long Great War, or a centuries-long single conflict.
- Dreamers existing before the Great War or causing magical rediscovery.
- A formal Remnant performing wartime actions before its postwar formation.
- A Volkhari Fire Titan.
- Captive Kaelith.
- Spirit as a fifth element, magical discipline, or Founder lineage.
- A durable universal postwar ban on all magic.
- Speaking, prophetic, question-answering, champion-selecting, or soldier-like Stonebound.
- Multiple Mire chapel sites derived from its three names.
- More than two major Mire supernatural threats, literal undead, or memory manifestations treated as surviving souls.
- Non-Lowmark v1 region labels and implied v1 player fire-spell training.
- Exact routes, travel times, campaign vectors, or direct supply lines inferred from the concept map.
- Legacy resource/equipment lists treated as approved catalogs.
