# Caelmor Part 6 — Regional Material-Culture Readiness

**Status:** proposal-stage readiness audit. It tests whether lore can justify future material and craft design. It does not approve legacy catalogs, invent resources, specify recipes, create gathering nodes, or design equipment.

## Test method

Each category is rated:

- **SUPPORTED:** geography, labor, social use, and source direction are sufficient for later design.
- **PARTIAL:** a credible foundation exists, but one or more of source, processing, stewardship, scarcity, training, ownership, or exchange is absent.
- **GAP:** later design would have to invent most of the justification.

Legacy named resources and finished goods in old regional dossiers are treated as **reference evidence of intent under S06**, not as approved catalog entries. A category passes only when the surrounding world supports it.

The required direction is always:

> desired item, utility, or cultural function → component or material → processing or gathering → world source

## Material-culture conclusion

The world already explains **why** many crafts matter. It often does not explain **where their inputs come from, who transforms them, what the work costs, and who controls or limits it**. That missing middle would force an equipment/economy designer to invent lore after choosing a desired output.

**Material-culture readiness: NOT READY for catalog design.** The correct next step is a bounded source-and-process lore pass around existing geography and labor, without adding named resources or gear.
