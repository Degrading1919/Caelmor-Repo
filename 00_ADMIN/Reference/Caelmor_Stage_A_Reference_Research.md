## Structural lessons for the next Caelmor stage

1. **One local resolution, one wider connection.** A side story closes a practical or emotional matter where it begins. One witness, scar, phrase, or record may later matter elsewhere. This preserves the Phase 1 ban on filler quests without making every local problem part of a conspiracy.
2. **A callback changes an earlier interpretation.** Record the initial observation, the reasonable first reading, the later corroborating evidence, and the new meaning. A repeated prop or line without reinterpretation is only repetition.
3. **Use several evidence channels.** Geography can imply an event; a habitual NPC statement can disagree; a document can narrow the date; a quest consequence can show why people care. Do not turn every truth into a single explanatory journal entry or perfect magical vision.
4. **Make uncertainty deliberate.** For each mystery, distinguish what is true for the author, what the public believes, what the player can observe in v1, and what remains withheld. An unresolved question is effective only when its evidence boundary is known to the writers.
5. **Let regional history have regional custodians.** A Lowmark record, Thornfell oath, Mire testimony, and Emberholt civic account could differ because of their functions and incentives. Stage A identifies this as a method; it does not invent any new cross-region event.
6. **Return with consequences, not constant escalation.** A recurring worker or family should have changed work, trust, status, or obligations when revisited. The eventual payoff can be an ordinary historical correction rather than a new boss or spell.
7. **Make secrets legible in-world.** An easter egg should fit its location and culture. It can reward attention with character insight or humor, but must be unnecessary for main quest comprehension and avoid arbitrary click-hunting.
8. **Keep the player's scope honest.** Four regions can have lore now while Phase 1 v1 ships Lowmark only, with 3–4 subzones and a bounded narrative budget (`01_PRE-PRODUCTION/1.5 Content Strategy/Caelmor_Phase1_5_Content_Strategy.md:30-58,157-176`). No reference example justifies adopting RuneScape's scale.

## Research-to-work guardrails

Before any Stage B writer proposes a quest or mystery, require a card with: source canon claim; local human stakes; relevant place; first observable clue; at most one cross-region dependency; intended later re-reading; what remains unproven; v1/future label; and any Director decision it depends on. This is an **editorial checklist recommendation**, not a quest schema or runtime design.

Do not automatically import the prior proposal from `4d2d6b6`. Its repair marks, civic records, household customs, and cross-region callback ideas are candidate seeds to evaluate against the reconciled claim ledger and Phase 1 budget. They are not approved events, institutions, places, or material-culture foundations.
