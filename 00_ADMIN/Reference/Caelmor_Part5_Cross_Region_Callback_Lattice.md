**Status:** additive narrative architecture proposal for Creative Director review. No future-region payoff is required in Lowmark v1, and no row is an approved quest, canon event, route, reward, or item. The Part 4 relationship web, event ledger, character ledger, cultural-borrowing standard, and map audit govern evidence and movement. Phase 1 keeps one Lowmark main arc in the only full v1 region.

## Callback standard

A callback earns its place only when an initial Lowmark observation has an intelligible **local meaning and local outcome**. Later evidence may revise one interpretation, not invalidate the earlier help. The later region or person must hold an independent account, and the community affected by the revised truth owns the emotional or civic response. Similar marks, words, stitching, or meals alone do not prove shared history. No C11 Stonebound response, C15 Mire memory manifestation, or Dreamer image can serve as a historical witness.

## Non-callback stories must remain numerous

Some Lowmark stories should end entirely in Lowmark: a caregiving dispute, bridge queue, market friendship, watergarden maintenance decision, or a child's local safety lesson can matter without a later record from another region. A side story does not need a sequel to justify its place. The Part 4 web offers connections when a later region has an independent stake, not a quota of cross-region hooks.

## Later-region handoffs without a single master plot

- **Thornfell** may return to pass closure, labor credit, or a cairn that Lowmark evidence re-dates. The Marches own their closure and household decisions. The C11 Summit node cannot name a past traveler or authenticate a horn interval.
- **The Mire** may return to a provisional name, foster household, or care testimony that changes what a Lowmark family believed. Rowan's unique reliable approach to the one Sunken Chapel is not a shortcut for civilian record recovery. The General and Sorrowback are dangerous earth-memory, never witnesses.
- **Emberholt** may return to disputed civic credit, a sincere safety memorial, and the Glassed Hollow cover story. Specific precursor culpability has directed facts, but ordinary buyer, worker, and household stories remain distinct. C03/C06 limit exact Remnant founding and Volkhari demographic claims.

For any selected callback, a later editor must check the Part 4 character ledger for direct-war experience, record custody, kinship, and travel, then the Part 4 map audit for movement. Neither this table nor a repeated visual motif supplies those facts. Phase 1's Lowmark-only v1 can show the first observation and local resolution without promising a future release date, scene, or unlock.
