# Caelmor Stage A — canon contradiction register

**Status:** audit for Creative Director review. This document records claims and required decisions; it does not amend canon. It is based on current `origin/main` at the start of Stage A. Commit `4d2d6b6` is a separate, unapproved proposal used only as a reference list for possible later review.

## Reading rule

The Creative Director's explicit decisions control this audit. Phase 1 follows; the Master Assistant Protocol states that order at `00_ADMIN/Reference/Caelmor_Master_Assistant_Protocol.md:23-35`. The Director has directed that the detailed regional dossiers and explicitly corrected Factions text govern factual truth over the merged overview, except where a newer Director decision overrides them. A **direction set** entry still needs source-file correction. An **open** entry needs a Director choice before future lore builds on it. **Scope** entries separate world lore from v1 implementation.

For this audit the Director has set: a 5–10-year Great War ending about 30 years before the present; a wartime precursor that later formed the Remnant; a Remnant initiate as the Fire Titan; Dreamers first appearing after the war; Founders as teachers distinct from the first elemental rediscoverers; Kaelith escaped and remains free; Thornfell's mostly natural acoustics with rare dormant magic; simple water spells among Lowmark farming workers, with combat water magic known only to the Last Field-Mage; a male present Flameward Speaker; and two major Mire supernatural threats with minor anomalies permitted. None of those decisions has been written into the old lore files by this PR.
