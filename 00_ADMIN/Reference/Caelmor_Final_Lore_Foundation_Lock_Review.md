## Remaining unknowns

### Non-blocking deferred canon decisions

- **C03 — precursor and Remnant continuity:** does not block ordinary materials or crafts. It restricts later attribution of secret techniques, wartime custody, named research lineages, and Remnant-specific relic provenance.
- **C06 — Volkhari population and community form:** does not block Emberholt's human civic industry. It forbids treating Volkhari people, bodies, knowledge, or settlement as a supply chain or ownerless craft source.
- **C07 — Founder roster and teaching links:** does not block regional craft traditions. It forbids assigning an exclusive material, technique, workshop lineage, or invention to a named Founder or rediscoverer.

### Non-blocking future design choices

- exact materials, ordinary species, crops, herds, fuels, colors, recipes, components, finished goods, grades, and output quantities;
- exact routes, hydrology, travel times, source sites, nodes, shipment order, and trade volume;
- exact constitutional powers, office boundaries, Assembly procedure, hearings, appeals, appointments, succession, licenses, tariffs, and monopolies;
- exact character ages or custody details beyond the reconciled character evidence;
- selection, revision, or rejection of legacy named materials;
- S01 ranged implementation before committing the v1 ranged equipment and skill-production scope.

These questions become blocking only when a proposed item, process, placement, faction claim, or production plan depends on the missing fact. Until then, later designers can use neutral wording, broad route classes, ordinary custody, and the approved regional foundations.

## Genuine blockers

**None.** No remaining uncertainty forces equipment or economy design to invent foundational history, culture, geography, labor, trade, magic, or material process after the fact.

Map uncertainty prevents exact world-node placement and route tuning, but it does not prevent equipment functions, progression architecture, component logic, or the minimum coherent material set from being designed. Governance uncertainty prevents unsupported monopolies or legal claims, but it does not prevent ordinary gatherers, processors, carriers, maintainers, and users from being defined.

Lore Foundation Lock candidate: READY FOR CREATIVE DIRECTOR APPROVAL.

If later design requires a source, process, institution, route, or supernatural property outside this foundation, that dependency should return to lore review instead of being invented inside an item or economy document.

## Recommended next handoff

The correct handoff is:

> aspirational/BIS equipment
> → progression milestones backward
> → recipes/components
> → minimum coherent material set
> → gathering actions
> → world nodes

This order begins with what equipment should mean and do, then works backward into the smallest material and gathering structure the world can support. Exact nodes come last, after map and access verification. No equipment, component, material set, gathering action, or node is selected in this review.
