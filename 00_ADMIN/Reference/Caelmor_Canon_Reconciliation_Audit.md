## Stale contradictions removed

| Area | Removed or corrected | Reconciled canon |
|---|---|---|
| Great War chronology | War roughly 160–150 years ago; century-scale conflict wording; dependent long postwar eras | Great War lasted 5–10 years and ended about thirty years ago; ancient dates remain relative and uncertain |
| Terminology | “Long War” in Phase 1 | **Great War** used consistently in live lore and scope sources |
| Dreamers | Dreamers placed before the war and tied to rediscovery | Dreamers first appear after the war; ordinary grief dreams and Hearthveil manipulation remain distinct; dreams require corroboration |
| Remnant history | Formal Remnant conducting wartime actions | An unnamed wartime precursor conducts war-era operations; the later formal Remnant follows after the war |
| Fire Titan | Volkhari mage/kin becomes the Titan | A wartime precursor initiate becomes the Fire Titan; the Titan is not Volkhari |
| Kaelith and Thalan | Captive/rescue premises for Kaelith; unnamed or multiple-current-captive implications | Kaelith escaped and remains free; Thalan Veyr is the sole known current captive |
| Volkhari status | Exact enclave, population, and “only survivor” assertions | Survivors exist; exact number, location, and community/political form remain unresolved |
| Founders | First rediscoverers equated with Founders; singular fixed Founder biographies | Early rediscoverers and later teachers called Founders are distinct people or roles; roster and links remain open |
| Spirit | Fifth element, separate school, or earth/spirit discipline | Old cross-cutting term for living/emotional imprint and the interaction of memory, grief, intention, and identity with elemental resonance |
| Lowmark water practice | No active/public water practice | Simple farm practice openly household/neighbor taught; experienced community stewards oversee shared works; charters govern only larger irrigation; combat practice belongs only to Last Field-Mage |
| Thornfell resonance | Either wholly nonmagical or broadly magical; Founder directly identified as First Air Mage | Most acoustics are natural; rare dormant resonance exists at specific old works; rediscoverer/Founder identities remain unresolved |
| Stonebound | Sentient/oracular/vision-giving or creature-like behavior | Passive, place-bound response to imbalance, pressure, intrusion, or disturbance; may create physical/resonant effects but never speaks, prophesies, answers, identifies history, chooses champions, or acts as a soldier |
| Postwar magic law | Universal ban on magic and research | Regional restrictions following broad rejection of weaponization, amplification, uncontrolled ritual experimentation, and dangerous research; no durable central ban |
| Flameward Speaker | Female pronouns in the Emberholt dossier | Present Flameward Speaker consistently male |
| Mire chapel | Three names treated as potentially separate sites | One site: Chapel of the Sinking Star formally, Sunken Chapel commonly, Sunken Church among riverfolk/ferrymen; Rowan is sole known reliable living guide |
| Mire threats | Multiple competing major-threat lists; shades/wraiths treated as souls | Exactly two major threats: General Who Wouldn't Fall and Sorrowback; neither undead; other manifestations minor/local; Chapel Shades are impressions |
| Regional release scope | Thornfell, Mire, or Emberholt framed as full v1 regions; v1 firecasting | Lowmark is the sole full v1 region; v1 has no player spellcasting system |
| Lowmark narrative | Five framework anchors read as five launch arcs; committed Founder staff reward | One Lowmark main arc; the five anchors supply functions/candidates; exact actors, encounters, and rewards remain later selections |
| Legacy catalogs | Old resource lists presented without status boundary | Each detailed regional resource section is marked legacy reference, not an approved catalog; future work must derive function → material → process → source |
| Evidence control | Supernatural events, memory objects, or dreams treated as direct historical proof | People, records, provenance, physical evidence, and bounded interpretation remain necessary |

## Approved selected foundations integrated

- A grounded ancient history known through practical remains rather than a vanished continent-wide empire.
- Four regional identities based on civic obligation and ordinary work.
- Interregional connection through food, passage, labor, trade, refuge, migration, household change, care, and repair.
- Two-custodian evidence and corroborated cultural-borrowing rules.
- One complete Lowmark v1 causal foundation centered on a bounded water/resonance crisis caused by accountable present decisions.
- Character knowledge/belief/custody limits and controlled mystery truth.
- Material-culture derivation boundary: cultural/practical function → material → processing/gathering → world source.

Optional named events, households, customs, landmarks, side stories, mysteries, and callbacks from Parts 2–5 were not imported wholesale.

## Contradiction sweep result

The final sweep covered all changed history, faction, regional, character, and Phase 1 files for:

- obsolete Great War dates and “Long War” wording;
- prewar Dreamers;
- wartime formal-Remnant claims;
- Volkhari Fire Titan or captive Kaelith claims;
- exact Volkhari population/community assertions;
- Founder/rediscoverer conflation and fifth-element spirit language;
- denials of approved Lowmark farm-water practice;
- categorical Thornfell resonance claims;
- speaking/oracular Stonebound language;
- universal postwar ban language;
- female present Speaker language;
- multiple Mire chapels, literal undead, or more than two major Mire threats;
- non-Lowmark full-v1 or v1 player-spellcasting commitments.

**Result: PASS for the required reconciliation set.** Remaining references to false accounts appear only when explicitly framed as public belief, local terminology, metaphor, interpretation, or rejected history.
