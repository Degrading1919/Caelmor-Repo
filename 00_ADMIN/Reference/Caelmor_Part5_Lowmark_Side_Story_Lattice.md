# Caelmor Part 5 — Lowmark side-story lattice

**Status:** proposal-stage narrative planning. This is not quest scripting, dialogue, JSON, item, resource, reward, map, or canon work. It is bounded by Phase 1: Lowmark is the sole v1 region, with one main arc and a selected set of locally complete side stories. Canon and Creative Director rulings through C15 prevail over every proposal below.

## Purpose and boundary

The side lattice teaches how Lowmark works by making the player useful to people who cannot simply wait for an official answer: a household facing a flooded boundary, a dock crew whose shift is unsafe, a caretaker asked to turn grief into proof, or a newcomer caught between a useful skill and a missing paper trail. Each proposal closes a human, civic, or work problem in Lowmark. A record, phrase, or person may later invite a single cross-region re-reading, but none requires a later region to make its v1 ending meaningful.

The main arc owns the escalating investigation of Lowmark's water wounds, the Last Field-Mage/Riverwarden layer, and the late access/revelation space. Side stories may establish consequences, witnesses, and ethical context for that arc; they do not duplicate its investigation or resolve its supernatural truth. In particular, a water anomaly cannot identify a historical actor, settle a claim, or function as a witness.

### Source limits carried forward

- Lowmark's anchored pressures are grain, river movement, mills, crossings, councils, wartime recovery, memorial work, and overlapping authority. `LORE/Regions/Caelmor_Region1_LowmarkVale.md`; Part 3 Lowmark proposal.
- Existing anchors retain the limits in the Part 4 character ledger. Aldric, Maelin, Corvin, Lessa, Joric, Annelise, and Tovin must not receive invented ages, service, kinship, travel, meetings, or record custody.
- The concept map supports broad exchange, not exact roads, channel continuity, travel times, or named itineraries. A later production selection must place any route-sensitive story only after a map pass.
- C09 remains open on farm-water practice. Stories can work under household custom, commons stewardship, or charter oversight, and must not silently choose one as canon.
- Routine water practice is bounded labor. Combat water practice remains the Last Field-Mage's isolated inheritance. C08 spirit is living/emotional imprint interacting with elemental resonance; it is neither a separate magic school nor a speaking archive.

## Selection envelope

Phase 1.5 allows **16–22 side quests**: 5–6 skill-introduction, 3–4 settlement-story, 3–4 environmental-discovery, 2–3 combat-challenge, and 3–5 memory/lore microquests, plus **6–10 micro-events**. This lattice proposes twenty substantial candidates and eight micro-events so a later production pass can select a coherent 16–20 rather than manufacture work to hit a count. The category labels below are planning labels only; they do not prescribe mechanics, skills, rewards, or quest structure.

| Planning type | Candidate count | Phase 1.5 range | Selection rule |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Skill introduction / work obligation | 6 | 5–6 | Select only stories that introduce a distinct social use of work, not a second harvesting or delivery loop. |
| Settlement story | 4 | 3–4 | Each settlement story has a different authority conflict. |
| Environmental discovery | 4 | 3–4 | Each uses a place as evidence; none turns discovery into a mandatory scavenger hunt. |
| Combat challenge | 2 | 2–3 | Conflict protects people or a shared place; violence is never evidence gathering. |
| Memory/lore microquest | 4 | 3–5 | A compact act of care, correction, or testimony with a complete local outcome. |
| **Total substantial candidates** | **20** | **16–22 selected** | Keep at least one story in every type and every major Lowmark settlement/landscape pressure. |

## Selection gates for later quest authoring

Before a candidate becomes a selected v1 story, a writer must confirm its local ending, participant consent/role, POI fit, and evidence limits. A cross-region reference requires one independent later evidence owner and must be removable without damaging the Lowmark ending. Any candidate that needs an invented route, direct historical meeting, exact age, magical testimony, a Remnant explanation, or a new C09 policy choice is deferred or redesigned.

**Part 5 side-lattice finding:** Lowmark can support a selected 16–20 story set with varied civilian, institutional, memorial, environmental, and restrained-supernatural pressures. The proposed stories do not make the Remnant a general cause, do not resolve the main water mystery, and preserve map and character uncertainty. The only narrative dependency carried forward is the already-recorded C09 local farm-water governance choice; no candidate is gated by it because each works under all three approved proposal models.
