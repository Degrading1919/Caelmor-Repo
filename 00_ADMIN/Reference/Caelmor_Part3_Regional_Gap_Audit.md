## Rating and depth test

- **Developed proposal:** multiple connected examples establish an older cause, present person/institution/place, a competing interest, and a possible later re-reading. This is **not** canon.
- **Partial:** usable direction exists, but evidence, procedure, or cultural variation needs selection or verification.
- **Thin/Missing:** only labels or a few detached entries, or no usable account. Such a finding requires targeted work while it can be done without a Director decision.
- **Gated:** a conflicting factual source requires a Director answer before that specific claim is written. No major Part 3 category has this rating after C11/C14/C15.

The audit tests **connection**, not paragraph count. A custom without work, people, history, or consequences does not pass. A callback without independent evidence does not pass. A proposed new landmark is not an approved map location merely because it appears in a table.

## First pass and targeted second pass

Four dedicated Terra Medium workers drafted Lowmark, Thornfell, Mire, and Emberholt separately. Sol reviewed the first drafts against the categories below. Three issues needed targeted passes rather than a claim of completion:

1. **Thornfell belief and joy were Partial.** A short domestic-faith note and maintenance ceremonies did not yet show enough variation outside war memory. A targeted Terra pass added several household stances, distinct dwarf/clan/migrant interpretations, three communal occasions that include humor and growing up, and a grounded later re-reading. The later C11 ruling now fixes the rare Summit coupling while leaving ordinary acoustics mostly natural.
2. **The Mire's shared-meal callback was poorly supported.** Similar customs in four places cannot prove a continent-wide civilian network. A targeted Terra pass narrowed it to a possible two-household postwar care link requiring a Sorrowmere route-host list and Lowmark letter. It also made one path clue a corroborated postwar migration chain rather than another battlefield mystery. Later C14/C15 rulings now identify the one chapel and two major earth-memory threats without making them witnesses to that care history.
3. **Emberholt's shared verse risked proof by resemblance and central-plot gravity.** A targeted Terra pass required a dated program, witness, or placed inscription before claiming borrowing, and added a local Cinderweave water-allocation dispute with civic consequences unrelated to the Remnant or Glassed Hollow.

Lowmark's first draft already connected history, local politics, household life, five side-story networks, and five later re-readings. Its unresolved C09 teaching/oversight models are explicitly options, not an unasked canon choice. The second pass did not add filler merely to match the other regions' entry counts.

**Final rerun after the Director's C11/C14/C15 rulings:** Thornfell's Summit, the Mire's chapel, and the Mire's threat hierarchy were checked again against each regional proposal and the cross-region evidence limits. Lowmark and Emberholt now acknowledge those decisions without claiming their own sites share a node or that Mire phenomena prove a war record. The resolved facts create no need for a fifth region, new magic school, central conspiracy, or extra major Mire threat. The ratings below are the final Part 3 ratings after that rerun.

## Cross-region audit

**Coverage:** the continuity proposal maps all six region pairs. Every pair has an older/war relationship, present reciprocal need, disagreement that can affect ordinary people, and proposed evidence held in at least two places. The source dossiers and corrected Factions support broad trade, sympathy, mistrust, and war roles; precise routes, meeting dates, and record objects are proposed. The concept map supplies broad orientation only, not travel times or verified adjacency for a convoy.

**Distinctiveness:** Lowmark argues through water access, records, and restraint; Thornfell through pass safety, labor, clan and shelter duties; the Mire through uncertain names, ground, care, and testimony; Emberholt through useful public heat, civic accountability, class, and concealed misuse. Shared relief and repair do not make the customs interchangeable. One similar meal, mark, verse, or stitch is a lead, never proof of a hidden common origin.

**Story ownership:** Lowmark's relief claim can resolve in Lowmark while Mire later supplies a name; a Thornfell cairn can be honored before a Mire arrival changes its reading; Emberholt housing or water allocation can be repaired without reaching the Hollow. The Summit node can signal disturbance but cannot name a traveler; the General and Sorrowback can leave danger and impressions but cannot verify a battle account. The Remnant is reserved for evidence of its actual operations. Other discrepancies may arise from class power, error, triage, protective silence, shame, or competing honest memories. The cross-region map gives nine candidate callbacks, not nine required quests.

**Scope:** Phase 1 v1 remains Lowmark-only with one major arc. These regional documents define world lore and future options, not four playable v1 regions, a new reputation system, magic mechanics, or release commitments. No equipment or resource catalog has been designed or judged ready.

**Residual cross-region verification:** no approved route-duration map or reconciled character-age/knowledge ledger exists. Until those are made, shared movements and direct testimony stay at proposal level. This is a narrower later verification task, not a Thin or Gated Part 3 regional category and not permission to invent exact geography or supernatural witnesses.
