# Caelmor Part 5 — long-form mystery and reveal ledger

**Status:** proposal-stage authoring control. This ledger distinguishes truth, belief, evidence, and ownership; it does not make the proposed truths canon, script quests, or promise future releases. Creative Director rulings through C15 are fixed. Where the Director has not chosen a fact, the author truth is explicitly **UNSELECTED** rather than invented.

## Mystery discipline

A substantial mystery must eventually change a person, institution, or community's action. Atmosphere may remain ambiguous, but the causal question supporting a main or side story cannot be answered by an omniscient vision, speaking Stonebound, literal ghost, Wraith behavior, or convenient Remnant confession. Lowmark v1 receives only the evidence needed for its complete local arc. Future regions own their truths and can revise a Lowmark interpretation without making the v1 resolution false.

## Mystery-load control

Only Thread 1 belongs to the Lowmark main arc. Thread 2 can support selected side/future family stories. Threads 3–6 are long-form world threads planted sparingly or not at all in v1. Each region should also retain local mysteries that never join these threads. A later narrative pass must reject any proposed clue that exists solely to tease a larger plot and does not first affect a Lowmark person, place, or decision.

**Ledger result:** all substantial mystery classes have controlled truth and ownership. The genuinely unknown C03/C06/C07 case details are labelled unselected; C08/C11/C14/C15 boundaries remain fixed. No supernatural phenomenon replaces human evidence, and the Remnant is causally central only where its actual operations are directed or independently shown.
