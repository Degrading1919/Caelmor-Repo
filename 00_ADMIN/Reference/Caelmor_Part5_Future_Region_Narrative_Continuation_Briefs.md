# Caelmor Part 5 — future-region narrative continuation briefs

**Status:** high-level proposal briefs for future narrative selection. They are not Part 6 execution, release commitments, quest outlines, scripts, dialogue, rewards, routes, or canon changes. Lowmark remains the only full v1 region; a future region must work for a player who completed only the required Lowmark main arc and missed every optional callback.

## Continuation principle

Each future region begins with its own present local pressure and ends with a locally owned civic or emotional resolution. One or two selected Lowmark observations may return as useful evidence. They cannot be the region's reason to exist, a required collectible trail, or proof that every region shares one plot. New players or returning players receive enough context in the future story itself.

## Thornfell — duty at a pass, truth at a cairn

**Local entry pressure:** a present crossing, shelter, or repair decision exposes disagreement over who may close a route, whose labor counts, and whether a household remains entitled to clan aid. The practical stakes are passage, weather, shelter, and work. Echochamber Summit is distant context until human decisions give a reason to approach it.

**Local narrative spine:**

1. participate in bridge, horn, shelter, or cairn duties and learn the difference between clan honor and shared public need;
2. encounter two honest records of a past closure—last sighting and later service—that do not initially align;
3. resolve a present household or labor claim before deciding what the historical mismatch means;
4. distinguish natural acoustics, inherited Echohewn engineering, rare dormant air resonance, and the directed C11 Summit coupling;
5. face a passive Summit response to present disturbance or pressure, never an oracle or monster with historical answers;
6. return the consequence to the Assembly, worker household, cairn keeper, or affected kin rather than allowing the phenomenon to rule.

**Safe recurring anchors:** Hadrik Stonewake can carry Assembly process and bounded war experience; Ressa Highcrag and Dorn Feldhorn can carry their own horn/watch practice; Maera Cairnwatch can own name/cairn ethics; Kaldren Deepdelver can set structural limits; Iven Ridgeborne can report observation without naming magic. Exact ages, theatres, travel, or record custody remain as in the Part 4 ledger.

**Selectable Lowmark callback:** a documented repair practice or departure/arrival record can reframe one labor debt or missing person. The matching mark alone is insufficient. Lowmark owns any original crossing correction; Thornfell owns pass duty, cairn, and household recognition.

**Local completion:** an unsafe route has an accountable status, labor or shelter credit is corrected, and a family/cairn account reflects what is now known. The Summit remains ancient and bounded; its makers/network extent may stay unknown.

**Do not import:** the Lowmark water crisis as cause, a speaking guardian, a universal repair code, or a mandatory Emberholt conspiracy.

## The Mire — care, names, and a world that remembers

**Local entry pressure:** a route closure, recovered name, household care obligation, or disputed memorial places ordinary survival beside dangerous earth-memory. The initial problem should be who is responsible for a living person, testimony, crossing, or shared ground.

**Local narrative spine:**

1. learn how Sorrowmere, Marshfall, Griefwatch, and Rootrest depend on different forms of care, food, caution, and records;
2. confront a disagreement in which a register, family, and recovered object preserve different parts of one life;
3. experience minor/local Earthen Echoes, Banner Wraiths, or Chapel Shades as environmental memory impressions, never souls or witnesses;
4. establish why Rowan Mirewake's safe approach to the one Chapel of the Sinking Star/Sunken Chapel/Sunken Church is exceptional without inventing the formal name's origin;
5. treat the General Who Wouldn't Fall and the Sorrowback as exactly the two major threats: dangerous coherent earth-memory, never undead people;
6. resolve the human case through records, care, and present action even if a major manifestation remains dangerous or partially unexplained.

**Safe recurring anchors:** Edris can hold her own direct care testimony; Calwen and Mara hold bounded banner/marker practice; Rowan alone among known living guides reliably knows the shifting-channel approach; Sennic offers fragmentary oral history; Lysa carries present care; Bran carries only his own veteran/ground observation within source limits.

**Selectable Lowmark callback:** an unreturned name, host-household letter, care relationship, or transferred record may change a Lowmark garden or family account. The Mire controls testimony disclosure and foster/care history; Lowmark controls its memorial/property response.

**Local completion:** a living care obligation, route, memorial, or household status is resolved honestly; one record may be amended or intentionally qualified. The world remembering does not make the dead walk or eliminate uncertainty.

**Do not import:** a second chapel, extra major threat, ghost ecology, Rowan as universal guide, or a Wraith that gives names.

## Emberholt — useful fire, controlled history, and civic responsibility

**Local entry pressure:** a heat, water, housing, workplace, school, guild-credit, or public-safety dispute shows that Emberholt's firecraft is ordinary civic life as well as a contested inheritance. A current resident needs relief or accountability before the Glassed Hollow truth enters.

**Local narrative spine:**

1. participate in civic fire safety, work, schooling, household care, or district allocation and see sincere competence alongside class pressure;
2. compare public record, worker/household account, and physical evidence in a case that does not require the Remnant;
3. encounter controlled archive gaps and Hearthveil-shaped visions as specific practices with provenance, not explanations for all belief;
4. establish the directed Glassed Hollow core: precursor abuse, initiate Titan, free Kaelith, current captive Thalan, and the false/incomplete “forge collapse” account;
5. protect Volkhari testimony and living people without declaring a population, universal agreement, or chosen bloodline under open C06;
6. return responsibility to civic offices, guilds, households, and the protected witnesses whose lives the official history affected.

**Safe recurring anchors:** the male Flameward Speaker can represent present civic administration but not complete secret knowledge; Oren Falx/Eraven Cyl can carry current trade only where custody is supported; Marenya Silvertide, Ilverin Qael, and Joren Vale require provenance checks for any record; Seralynd/Serix can carry belief and vision uncertainty; Kaelith and Thalan remain within directed C05/C06 boundaries. Stale Stonebound voice/vision and Volkhari-Titan claims in old character text cannot return as truth.

**Selectable Lowmark callback:** a priority-contract phrase or omitted repair credit may help distinguish ordinary civic need from coercive wartime demand. Lowmark owns its contract remedy; Emberholt owns correction of public credit/history. No Lowmark clue proves Glassed Hollow.

**Local completion:** a present civic harm is repaired; a bounded public account accepts evidence and responsibility; selected Volkhari testimony changes who is recognized without making all citizens guilty or every institution a front.

**Do not import:** a universal anti-magic ban, captive Kaelith, Volkhari Titan, fixed survivor count, speaking Stonebound, or Remnant control of every guild and dream.

## Continuation handoff requirements

State which v1 callback is optional and provide the later-region evidence independently. Preserve players' prior local resolutions; a future reveal can add responsibility or context, not announce that helping Lowmark was meaningless.

**Part 5 boundary:** these briefs identify narrative continuation opportunities only. They do not begin Part 6, write quests, set expansion order beyond Phase 1's existing Thornfell direction, or claim Lore Foundation Lock.
