# Creative Director painting checklist

Every ranked entry has a grayscale value master, four linked painting
maps, a separate height map, an OBJ with one non-overlapping paint chart,
and a ZIP ready for transfer to Files. Those are generated baselines.
The six-material Blender pilot passed, and a synthetic texture return
kept the original master and unedited technical maps. The iPad import,
live strokes, and exported file characteristics still require a
Creative Director device check.

## Begin with the six native fixtures

| Master | Paint pass | What to inspect in 3D |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Split Oak Shingle | Break the longest parallel grain marks and place a few credible split tips or replaced pieces on a separate layer. | Lapped butt rhythm stays readable from the eaves. |
| Coursed Fieldstone | Vary stone face planes and restrain the dark mortar. Keep any moss or damp seam accents on an overlay. | Bedding reads at both grazing and front light. |
| Split Slate | Paint angular flakes and lightly stepped cleavage in the larger faces. | Slate should read thin and brittle beside split wood. |
| Hewn Oak | Reduce machine-like grain repetition and describe tool marks with short strokes along the member. | Grain follows the beam and holds up under a side light. |
| Forged Iron | Group hammer marks, add handling burnish only where plausible, and separate oxide from exposed metal in the metallic map. | The silhouette stays continuous and the sheen changes with lighting. |
| Plain Linen | Break perfectly even yarn values, keeping thread direction and the same repeat. | Check cloth legibility at normal distance before increasing contrast. |

## Extend by use

- **Architectural surfaces** Group values into courses, seams, spans and
  repairs. Keep broadly repeated motifs quiet enough to use on a large
  wall or roof.
- **Terrain and vegetation** Paint large value masses and scattered
  landmarks without planting obvious repeats. Check at least a 2 × 2 tile.
- **Tools, fittings and leather** Spend contrast on physical contact,
  fasteners, wear, and construction edges. Reserve high sheen for exposed
  metal or polished surfaces.
- **Fabrics, ceramics and glass** Establish a readable base before adding
  decorative paint. Final glass transmission needs a separate Blender
  finish. The pilot package uses an opaque paintable surface.
- **All masters** Compare clay/value, palette, and raking-light reads.
  Check alignment and scale, preserve a grayscale version, and transfer
  only the maps intentionally changed.

The catalog's `paint` field carries additional per-master finishing
notes. No baselines have been hand-painted or accepted as final
production art yet.
