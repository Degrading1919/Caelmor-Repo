# Lowmark Brookoak Roof Shingles

An editable Material Maker 1.7 roof-field study for Lowmark. Open `Lowmark_Brookoak_RoofShingles.ptex` and use the Remote Controls node at the left. Revision 76 is the reviewed native MaterialPilot graph.

| Control | Starting value | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Course count | 8 | Number of courses in a V repeat |
| Minimum shingle width | 0.115 | Minimum width as a fraction of a U repeat |
| Side / butt edge irregularity | 0.012 / 0.017 | Horizontal joint wander and variation in exposed butt position |
| Riven grain strength | 0.32 | Amount of per-board directional wavelet grain |
| Lap relief | 0.37 | Weight of the down-course height ramp |
| Board color variation | 0.35 | Amount of per-board tone variation before the albedo ramp |
| Weathering scale across / down roof | 12 / 8 | Roughness patch scale and the noise driving the side-edge warp |
| Split strength | 0.28 | Weight of the sparse directional split marks |

Change the main Colorize gradient for regional palettes. The second Colorize gradient controls the roughness range. Keep the course count integral and check U/V repetition after changing controls or seeds.

## Riven bake review candidate

The [Blender build source](build_brookoak.py) creates a tile from overlapping modeled timber surfaces. Each shingle's taper, cup, twist, beveled shoulders, sidewalls and cut end are generated before baking. The outlines include selective corner breaks. The same board-local growth coordinates drive longitudinal relief, redirected grain around selected knots, tapered checks, smaller fissures, and the shader's fine fibers. Exposure and damage alter reflectance and roughness as well as relief. The bake projects material signals without direct light, cast shadows, or AO in the albedo.

Run from the checkout with Blender 5.0.1:

```powershell
blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python MEDIA/Materials/procedurally_generated/build_brookoak.py -- --verify-repeat
```

## Lowmark Structural Timber

The Creative Director approved the structural timber at `c1e9fee`. That revision is the accepted Lowmark framing material.

This Blender construction study develops a single continuous side surface for hand-hewn posts, braces and beams. Growth fibers diverge around embedded branches. Tapered checks cross fewer fibers than they follow. Groups of shallow hewing cuts and separated patches of abrasion leave quieter stretches between them. The specimen meshes carry the wide wane, fractured corners and skewed cuts. The side bake carries surface relief and contains no member outlines, joints or architectural arrangement. The crosscut is a separate radial growth section with off-center pith, variable annual rings, pores and checking. The warmer heartwood and grayer exposure patches relate to accepted Brookoak roofing; they use a separate physical construction and grain scale.

## Lowmark Forged Iron & Blackened Hardware

The Creative Director approved the forged iron and blackened hardware at `90f2085`. That revision is the accepted Lowmark hardware material.

The independent iron surface is built from shallow groups of hammer strikes over a slightly warped face. Pits, accumulated oxide scale and localized corrosion share the relief and roughness response. Blackening dims the exposed iron’s reflection. Thicker scale and corrosion replace part of the conducting surface with a rougher dielectric. The separate specimen meshes carry the strap thickness, rolled knuckle, fastener shape and edge geometry.

- [Blender 5.0.1 authoring source](build_lowmark_forged_iron.py) regenerates the geometry, bakes five channels in Cycles and renders the review scenes. It uses Blender’s bundled NumPy and the existing material helpers in this directory. Defaults use seed 28747, hammer intensity 0.94 and blackening 0.98. `--seed`, `--hammer`, `--blackening`, `--maintained`, `--weathered`, and `--corroded` control the physical rebuild. The three default conditions are 0.13, 0.52 and 0.87. `--clay-only`, `--bake-only` and `--render-only` select parts of the workflow.

